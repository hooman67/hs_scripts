### 1.5 ⚠️ NEW RISK — the immunosuppression BOOMERANG (pneumococcal / meningococcal)

Flagged by the Stage-5 red team; the upstream draft missed it. **You cannot simultaneously claim

**This is another reason titers, not the contraindication, should be Hooman's primary door.** `[ASSUMED
