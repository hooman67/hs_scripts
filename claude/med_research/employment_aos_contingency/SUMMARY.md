## Executive conclusion

`VERIFIED-HIGH` — The October 2029 I-94 dates do not preserve TN/TD through 2029 after Adobe
employment ends. The governing ceiling is a discretionary grace period of up to 60 consecutive
days from cessation of employment (or the I-94 end, if earlier), and Hooman cannot work during it
without separate authorization. The exact first day depends on Adobe's actual employment-end date,
not merely an announcement date or the last date worked.

`RECOMMENDATION — BAL MUST CONFIRM` — Do not ask BAL to hold the AOS solely to preserve a possible
visitor entry. Ask BAL to determine immediately whether the case is approvable and should be shipped
while Adobe's permanent job offer and both parties' intent remain bona fide. Filing starts the
180-day portability clock and can create a period of authorized stay, but it is not a guarantee:
before day 180, loss of Adobe's job offer creates a serious denial risk and AC21 portability is not
yet available.

`VERIFIED-HIGH` — Neither official Adobe parental leave nor genuine medical leave immunizes an
employee from a neutral reduction in force. Adobe's own FMLA/CFRA policy expressly says that an
employee whose position is eliminated may be terminated on the scheduled RIF date even with leave
remaining. Leave-based discrimination or retaliation is prohibited, but that is a different claim.

`RECOMMENDATION` — Open the official parental-leave case with Lincoln now; do not leave the December
1 absence recorded only as PTO. Parental leave cannot begin before the actual birth. A genuine own-
medical leave may begin earlier only when medically necessary and certified, but it is not a layoff
shield and could consume the rolling FMLA/CFRA balance relevant to the later parental leave.

## Answers to the five questions

### 1. Time in the United States after Adobe employment ends

- `VERIFIED-HIGH`: 8 CFR 214.1(l)(2) covers a TN principal and dependents. It permits up to 60
  consecutive days, or until the authorized-validity period ends, whichever is shorter. DHS can
  shorten or eliminate the grace period. No employment is authorized during it.
- If the last employment day were November 16, 2026, the 60-day ceiling would extend approximately
  to mid-January 2027. BAL must calculate the exact date from the separation documents.
- `VERIFIED-HIGH`: A properly pending I-485 ordinarily creates a period of authorized stay and
  prevents unlawful-presence accrual while pending, but it does not itself confer lawful TN/TD
  status or work authorization.
- `VERIFIED-HIGH`: A new TN employer's in-country I-129 must be approved before Hooman works for the
  new employer. A Canadian can alternatively seek readmission with new-employer TN evidence, but a
  pending I-485 creates nonimmigrant-intent risk.

### 2. Whether to stop or proceed with AOS

- `RECOMMENDATION — HIGH URGENCY`: Do not direct BAL to pause by default. Ask why it has not filed,
  whether a visa number is available, whether the I-140 is approved, whether the approval is at
  least 180 days old, and whether Adobe's signed Supplement J/permanent offer remains valid. Ask for
  BAL's written recommendation on immediate filing.
- `VERIFIED-HIGH`: Employment-based AOS requires a bona fide qualifying job offer and mutual intent
  at filing and adjudication. If Adobe retracts the offer before USCIS receives the packet, BAL must
  not submit a stale Supplement J as though the offer still exists.
- `VERIFIED-HIGH`: AC21 portability requires an I-485 pending at least 180 days, an approved or
  ultimately approvable I-140, and a bona fide same-or-similar permanent offer, documented with
  Supplement J. Filing immediately does not create immediate portability.
- `VERIFIED-HIGH`: Employer withdrawal can automatically revoke an I-140 if withdrawal occurs before
  the petition has been approved 180 days and before the associated I-485 has been pending 180 days.
  After either threshold, the petition generally remains approved, but the Adobe job offer is still
  gone and another qualifying offer or petition is needed.
- If USCIS has already received the I-485 when termination occurs: do not withdraw reflexively;
  notify BAL immediately, preserve receipts, determine I-140 withdrawal risk, seek a same/similar
  permanent offer, and do not travel without approved Advance Parole.
- `VERIFIED-HIGH`: TN/TD applicants are not in the H/L travel exception. Departure with a pending
  I-485 before Advance Parole is granted generally abandons the I-485.
- `VERIFIED-HIGH`: Returning as a visitor is a fragile contingency, not the main plan. B admission
  requires a genuinely finite visit and nonimmigrant intent. A U.S. home/car, a pending or recent
  AOS case, an intent to resume U.S. residence, and travel immediately before a U.S. birth all make
  admission materially harder. Admission remains discretionary.

### 4. Adobe medical leave and salary continuation

- `VERIFIED-HIGH`: Adobe medical leave is for an employee's own serious health condition requiring
  more than five consecutive business days and medical documentation. For employees scheduled at
  least 24 hours/week who meet the disability-plan rules, the Adobe leave may last as medically
  necessary, generally up to 12 months. The first 12 weeks normally run concurrently with available
  FMLA/CFRA; later time is not automatically statutory job-protected leave.
- `VERIFIED-HIGH`: The provider certification to Lincoln generally must state the condition's onset,
  probable duration, necessary leave duration, facts establishing a serious condition, inability to
  perform essential job functions, restrictions, and expected duration. It is generally due within
  15 days after requested. California VDI may require medical facts and a diagnosis/ICD code or a
  detailed symptom statement; this goes to the leave/claims administrator, not the manager.
- `VERIFIED-HIGH`: A planned claim should be filed at least 30 days before leave; an unforeseeable
  need should be filed as soon as practicable. The leave begins when the provider certifies that the
  employee can no longer work—not when it would be strategically convenient.
- `VERIFIED-HIGH`: For a covered California full-time employee, Adobe's 2026 VDI statement provides
  100% of eligible base earnings for the first 63 compensable disability days. Because benefits
  start on day eight and the first week is covered with sick time/PTO under Adobe's policy, this is
  approximately ten weeks at full base pay in total. Bonuses, overtime, and other extra compensation
  are excluded. After that, the VDI statement describes a lower percentage; Adobe's summary policy
  differs on the percentage/cap, so Lincoln must provide the controlling personalized calculation.
- `VERIFIED-HIGH`: Medical leave does not block a neutral RIF. Starting it before the birth can also
  consume FMLA/CFRA entitlement and may alter parental-leave qualification. Lincoln should model the
  sequence in writing before Hooman changes dates.
