### 2026-11-08 — Layoff, TN/AOS, and Adobe leave contingency

- TN principals and TD dependents receive at most a discretionary 60-day grace period following
  cessation of qualifying employment, or until I-94 expiry if earlier; employment is not authorized
  during that period. An October 2029 I-94 does not preserve TN/TD through 2029 after termination.
  **VERIFIED-HIGH (8 CFR 214.1(l)(2), retrieved 2026-11-08).**
- Employment-based AOS requires a bona fide job offer and mutual intent at filing/adjudication.
  AC21 portability requires an I-485 pending at least 180 days plus a same/similar permanent offer.
  A pending I-485 is authorized stay, not lawful TN/TD status or automatic work authorization.
  **VERIFIED-HIGH (8 CFR 245.25; USCIS Policy Manual Vol. 7 Parts B/E).**
- Departure with pending I-485 before Advance Parole is granted generally abandons the application;
  TN/TD are not within the H/L exception. Visitor admission remains discretionary and requires a
  truthful finite visit/nonimmigrant intent. **VERIFIED-HIGH (8 CFR 245.2; INA/FAM).**
- Adobe's current public policy offers qualifying employees 16 continuous weeks of parental leave
  at 100% eligible salary, starting no earlier than birth and requiring a Lincoln claim. **VERIFIED-
  HIGH (Adobe document 154, server updated 2026-05-08; live page retrieved 2026-11-08).**
- Adobe's FMLA policy expressly states that a neutral RIF may terminate employment on its scheduled
  date during protected leave. Leave is not layoff immunity. **VERIFIED-HIGH (Adobe document 153;
  29 CFR 825.216).**
- Adobe medical leave requires a genuine certified inability to work. For covered California full-
  time employees, the 2026 VDI statement pays 100% of eligible base earnings for the first 63
  compensable disability days after a seven-day waiting period; with the first sick/PTO week this is
  approximately ten total weeks at full base pay. **VERIFIED-HIGH (Adobe documents 45 and 155).**
- Adobe active health coverage ends on the last day of the employment-end month; COBRA is generally
  available for up to 18 months with a 60-day election period. **VERIFIED-HIGH (Adobe leaving-
  company page; U.S. DOL COBRA page, retrieved 2026-11-08).**
