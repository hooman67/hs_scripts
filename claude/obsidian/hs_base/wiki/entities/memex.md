# Memex

Vannevar Bush's 1945 concept (from "As We May Think") of a personal, curated knowledge store with
**associative trails** linking documents together. The [[wiki/concepts/llm-wiki-pattern|LLM-Wiki]]
is described as a modern realization of it.

## Connection to this pattern

Bush's vision was private, actively curated, with the **connections between documents as valuable as
the documents themselves** — closer to this wiki than to what the web actually became. The piece Bush
couldn't solve was *who does the maintenance* of all those trails. In the LLM-Wiki, the LLM does.
