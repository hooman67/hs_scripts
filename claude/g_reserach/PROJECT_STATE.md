The immediate transaction bottleneck is 11 CCR § 4045.1. The EAD is not itself listed as a direct § 4045.1(b) supplement for use with the existing marked license. Without an EAD or green card, the public DMV and DOJ lists leave a visa-exempt Canadian TN gap requiring written agency clarification.
