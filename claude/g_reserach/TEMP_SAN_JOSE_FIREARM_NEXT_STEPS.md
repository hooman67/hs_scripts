# San Jose firearm acquisition: immediate next steps

The immediate next step is **not to order a firearm or begin DROS yet**. Your California license marked “Federal Limits Apply” is the current obstacle.

1. Federal sources strongly indicate that a Canadian admitted in TN status without a visa was **not admitted under a nonimmigrant visa**. Current Form 4473 instructions therefore point toward answering question 21.m.1 “No,” entering your I-94 number in question 20, and not needing the hunting-license exception. Get this confirmed in writing by your firearm lawyer before signing the form.

2. California regulation 11 CCR § 4045.1 does not clearly accept a visa-exempt Canadian passport plus I-94/I-797A as supplemental documentation for a “Federal Limits Apply” license. A hunting license does not fix this particular problem.

3. Follow up with all three dealers after two business days. Ask for an explicit **yes or no** about accepting:

   - Federal Limits Apply California license
   - Canadian passport without visa foil
   - current I-94
   - I-797A with attached I-94
   - current hunting license
   - FSC
   - utility bill and vehicle registration

   The exact ready-to-send message is in the full report. UG Imports’ generic link was not confirmation.

4. Ask your firearm lawyer to address Form 4473, the I-797A extension, 11 CCR § 4045.1, your adjustment process, and handgun transport in writing. Ask immigration counsel whether ownership creates any case-specific adjustment concern.

5. If a dealer confirms acceptance:

   - Verify the exact handgun SKU is on California’s roster.
   - Bring all original documents and two strong residency records.
   - Obtain an itemized quote.
   - Complete DROS and Form 4473.
   - Wait ten complete 24-hour periods.
   - Pick up before DROS expires at 30 days.
   - Complete the safe-handling demonstration.
   - Transport it unloaded, locked, and directly home.

6. Before pickup, confirm your homeowners policy covers accidental firearm losses, complete San Jose’s insurance attestation, and install a California-compliant safe or locking device. I found no universal legal requirement that the safe be anchored, although anchoring is prudent. I also could not verify a currently payable San Jose gun fee.

7. Pursue CCW only after acquiring and registering the handgun. Santa Clara County currently publishes $976 in agency fees, plus Live Scan, psychological testing, training, range, and ammunition. Obtain written TN-document pre-clearance before paying its nonrefundable $488 initial fee.
