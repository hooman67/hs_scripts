## D-006 — Apply for Form I-765 and use the resulting I-766 for the REAL ID route

- **Status:** [CONFIRMED BY USER]
- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **Decision:** The user plans to apply for Form I-765 soon. If USCIS approves it and issues a physical, unexpired Employment Authorization Document (Form I-766), use that card to obtain a California REAL ID and thereby remove the 11 CCR § 4045.1(b) “Federal Limits Apply” document trigger.
- **Important limitation:** Filing Form I-765, receiving an I-765 receipt notice, or having a pending I-765 is not enough. The DMV route relies on the approved, physical, unexpired I-766 card. Immigration counsel should confirm the correct filing category and any consequence of using adjustment-based employment authorization while in TN status.
- **Revisit trigger:** I-765 denial, an expired/lost card, international travel or advance-parole re-entry, TN expiration or violation, adjustment approval, or a DMV refusal to issue REAL ID from the I-766.
