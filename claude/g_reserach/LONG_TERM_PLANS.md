# Long-term plan

1. Resolve the federal Form 4473 classification and California “Federal Limits Apply” document issue in writing.
2. Obtain written pre-clearance from a specific FFL before ordering, transferring, or paying nonrefundable charges.
3. Prepare the FSC, handgun residency evidence, San Jose insurance attestation, and compliant home storage.
4. Select only a California-roster handgun and complete the dealer/DROS process.
5. Take introductory safety instruction and practice with the intended platform before relying on it for home protection.
6. Re-check immigration/firearm eligibility after any international travel, re-entry on advance parole, TN expiration or violation, employer/status change, or adjustment approval.
7. If concealed carry remains desired, obtain licensing-agency pre-clearance, then apply after the handgun appears in California DOJ ownership records.
