# San Jose lawful firearm acquisition research

This project tracks current federal, California, San Jose, and Santa Clara County rules relevant to a Canadian citizen living in San Jose in TN status who wants to acquire a firearm lawfully for home protection and later consider a concealed-carry license.

The canonical user-facing research report is `research/san-jose-home-defense/SUMMARY.md`. The exact live checkpoint is `PROJECT_STATE.md`. Legal conclusions are separated from dealer business policy, unresolved document-acceptance questions, and practical recommendations.

Private immigration records are not copied into this project. The uploaded document at `/mnt/localssd/new_Tn_visas_Aug-2026.pdf` must not be emailed or uploaded elsewhere without a secure method and an actual need.
