# Property and School Trade-off Policy v1

**Status:** Provisional screening policy; revise after reviewing real example homes

**Prepared:** 2026-09-12

The user asked for help setting yard, HOA, renovation, and school thresholds rather than supplying arbitrary numerical cutoffs. The rules below are decision policies, not external facts. They are therefore `INFERRED` or `ESTIMATE`, with confidence stated explicitly.

## Yard

### Hard functional requirement

The home must have private outdoor space directly accessible from the house that provides a reasonably level, unobstructed play area for two young children and a dog. A steep hillside, narrow setbacks, decorative front yard, deck-only space, or pool occupying nearly all open space does not satisfy the requirement. `INFERRED | HIGH`.

### Screening bands

| Usable play area | Treatment | Evidence status / confidence |
|---:|---|---|
| Under about 800 sq ft | Manual review; reject if it cannot provide a safe contiguous play zone | `ESTIMATE | MEDIUM` |
| About 800–1,200 sq ft | Acceptable if shape, grade, fencing, and access work | `ESTIMATE | MEDIUM` |
| Over about 1,200 sq ft | Preferred, subject to maintenance and hazard trade-offs | `ESTIMATE | MEDIUM` |

“Usable” excludes the house footprint, driveway, required front setback, steep or unstable slope, inaccessible strips, major mechanical equipment, and space dominated by a pool. Lot size alone is not the decision variable. Where listing data do not state usable area, use lot size only as a discovery proxy and verify from plans, imagery, disclosures, and inspection.

## HOA

| Monthly HOA | Treatment | Evidence status / confidence |
|---:|---|---|
| USD 0 | Preferred | `INFERRED | HIGH` |
| USD 1–200 | Acceptable if governing documents, reserves, insurance, and restrictions pass review | `INFERRED | MEDIUM` |
| USD 201–350 | Review item; require clear services/amenities and include the full fee in economic carry | `INFERRED | MEDIUM` |
| Over USD 350 | Presumptive screen-out for a detached house unless it replaces material costs the household would otherwise pay | `INFERRED | MEDIUM` |

Any pending special assessment, weak reserves, material litigation, insurance gap, or restriction that defeats intended yard, garden, project, solar, or future rental use triggers separate diligence regardless of the monthly fee.

## Renovation

The standard search should favor homes that are habitable, financeable, and insurable at closing.

| Immediate project scope | Treatment | Evidence status / confidence |
|---:|---|---|
| Up to USD 50,000 | Acceptable when priced into the acquisition and reserves remain intact | `INFERRED | MEDIUM` |
| USD 50,001–75,000 | Review case; require contractor-supported scope and at least 20% contingency | `INFERRED | MEDIUM` |
| Over USD 75,000 or structural/foundation/retaining-wall/full-gut work | Exclude from the standard search; analyze only as a separate project scenario | `INFERRED | MEDIUM` |

Cosmetic listing language is not evidence of scope. Final budgeting requires inspection, disclosures, permit history, and contractor estimates. Renovation cash must not consume the post-close reserve floor.

## Schools

There should be no single GreatSchools-style cutoff and no citywide school score. Evaluate the verified assigned elementary, middle, and high-school path for each address.

### Evidence dimensions

1. Multi-year English-language arts and mathematics outcomes, with cohort and suppression rules preserved.
2. Academic growth where a comparable official measure exists.
3. Chronic absenteeism, suspension, graduation/college-career readiness where age-appropriate, and other official climate indicators.
4. Program fit, class/services evidence, travel burden, and direct family observations.
5. Montessori, private, charter, or homeschooling alternatives, with availability, admissions, commute, and tuition recorded separately.

### Decision bands

- **Strong public default:** consistently solid evidence across academics and climate, with no material assignment or fit concern.
- **Workable trade-off:** mixed but not persistently weak evidence; the location/property advantage is meaningful and the family accepts the school after direct review.
- **Alternative-school dependent:** persistent serious weakness across multiple measures or a material fit problem; retain only if a realistic alternative school plan and separate tuition budget are confirmed.
- **Unknown:** assignment or essential evidence is missing. Unknown never becomes a neutral score.

These bands are `INFERRED | MEDIUM`. They deliberately avoid pseudo-precise thresholds before the candidate comparison reveals the distributions and the children’s current educational needs are specified.

## Revisit rule

After viewing at least five plausible properties, record which yard sizes, HOA packages, renovation scopes, and school trade-offs actually feel acceptable. Revise these bands once from those examples before final scoring. `INFERRED | HIGH` that concrete examples will produce more reliable preferences than abstract cutoffs.
