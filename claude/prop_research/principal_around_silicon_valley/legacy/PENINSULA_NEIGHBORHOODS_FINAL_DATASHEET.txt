DATA SOURCES & METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Primary Sources (High Confidence):
- FBI Uniform Crime Reporting (UCR) 2023-2025 via City-Data.com
- US Census Bureau American Community Survey (ACS) 2024 data
- Google Maps (commute time estimates)
- Zillow.com & Redfin.com (housing prices, market data)
- GreatSchools.org (school ratings and data)
- CAL FIRE SRA Maps (wildfire risk zones)
- FEMA Flood Zone Database

Secondary Sources (Medium Confidence):
- Local news archives
- School district websites
- Community Facebook groups
- LinkedIn/Indeed job databases

Data Quality Notes:
- Commute times: +/- 5 minutes variance depending on traffic pattern & time of day
- Housing prices: Snapshot in time (June 2026); market fluctuates
- Crime rates: FBI UCR official data; per-1000-resident normalization enables comparison
- School ratings: Composite of test scores and parent reviews; subjective component
- Lot sizes: Median from recent listings; individual properties vary significantly
- Fire/flood risk: Official government mapping; always verify specific property address

Confidence Levels:
- HIGH: Crime rates (FBI UCR), Census demographics, flood zones (FEMA), SRA mapping
- MEDIUM: Housing prices (market snapshot), school ratings, commute times
- LOW: Vaccination policies (varies by school), parent network strength (subjective), AI job counts (estimated)
