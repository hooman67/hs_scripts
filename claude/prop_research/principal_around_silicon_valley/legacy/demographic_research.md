## Key Findings & Patterns

### Demographic Diversity Spectrum

**Most Diverse (by % Non-White):**
1. Milpitas (87.3% non-white)
2. Cupertino (80.7% non-white)
3. San Jose (79.4% non-white)

**Most White (by % White Alone):**
1. Los Gatos (58.8% white)
2. Morgan Hill (35.3% white)
3. Mountain View (34.2% white)

### Racial/Ethnic Composition Patterns

**Asian-Dominant Cities** (>40% Asian):
- Cupertino (73.6%)
- Saratoga (61.8%)
- Milpitas (66.5%)
- San Jose (41.8%)
- Mountain View (43.0%)

**Hispanic-Dominant Cities** (>30% Hispanic):
- Gilroy (58.4%)
- Morgan Hill (35.5%)
- San Jose (30.0%)
- Redwood City (37.1%)

**White-Majority Cities** (>35% White):
- Los Gatos (58.8%)
- Redwood City (37.8%)
- San Mateo (33.0%)

### Regional Geographic Patterns

**Northern Cities (Highway 101 Corridor - Redwood City, San Mateo):**
- More balanced demographics
- 62-67% non-white
- Higher Hispanic representation

**Central Silicon Valley (San Jose, Los Gatos, Saratoga, Cupertino, Mountain View, Milpitas):**
- Higher Asian concentration (average ~44%)
- Tech industry influence on demographics
- 65-87% non-white

**Southern Cities (Morgan Hill, Gilroy):**
- More Hispanic representation
- More balanced White-Hispanic composition
- Agricultural heritage reflected in demographics

4. **Los Gatos** - Santa Clara County, foothills
5. **Saratoga** - Santa Clara County, foothills

7. **Mountain View** - Santa Clara County, tech hub

## Recommendations for Property Research

Based on demographic data compiled:

- **Most homogeneous cities:** Los Gatos (58.8% White), Morgan Hill (nearly 50-50 White-Hispanic split)
- **Most diverse cities:** Milpitas, Cupertino, San Jose (80%+ non-white)
- **Highest Hispanic representation:** Gilroy (58.4%), followed by Redwood City and Morgan Hill (~35%)
- **Highest Asian representation:** Cupertino (73.6%), Saratoga (61.8%), Milpitas (66.5%)

For family relocation planning, consider city demographic composition alongside housing market, school quality, commute patterns, and community amenities.
