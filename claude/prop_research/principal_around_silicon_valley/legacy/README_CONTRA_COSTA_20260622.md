# Contra Costa County Neighborhoods Research - June 2026

## Executive Summary

Comprehensive systematic research on 7 Contra Costa County neighborhoods extracting **11 specific investment metrics** for a family of 4 (AI engineer at Adobe DTSJ, Brazilian wife, 2 young daughters, unvaccinated).

**Budget:** $2M max  
**Timeline:** Property purchase within 3 years (by mid-2029)  
**Primary Constraint:** Need schools accepting CA personal belief vaccination exemptions

---

## Research Scope & Methodology

### 11 Metrics Extracted
1. **Commute to DTSJ** (minutes, peak traffic via I-680-S/I-880-S/101-S)
2. **Commute to SF** (minutes, peak traffic via I-680-N or I-80)
3. **AI/ML jobs within 30 min** (realistic count from LinkedIn/Indeed June 2026)
4. **GreatSchools average rating** (1-10 scale)
5. **Alternative schools** (private, homeschool co-ops, exemption-friendly options)
6. **5-year appreciation** (%, 2021-2026)
7. **Violent crime rate** (per 1,000 population, FBI UCR 2024)
8. **Property crime rate** (per 1,000 population, FBI UCR 2024)
9. **Median 3BR home price** (2026, Zillow/Redfin/MLS)
10. **Typical lot size** (sqft)
11. **Diversity index** (% non-white, Census ACS 2024)

### Primary Data Sources
- **Commutes:** Google Maps peak traffic (8 AM weekday simulation)
- **Crime:** FBI Uniform Crime Reporting (UCR) 2024 + local police
- **Schools:** GreatSchools.org + district websites
- **Home Prices:** Zillow, Redfin, MLS recent sales
- **Demographics:** US Census ACS 2024
- **Jobs:** LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor (June 2026 snapshot)

### Confidence Standards
- **HIGH:** >90% (2+ independent government sources; recent data)
- **MEDIUM:** 70-89% (single authoritative source; recent date 2025-2026)
- **LOW:** <70% (estimates, ranges, older data, limited sample)

## Important Caveats & Limitations

1. **Commute times** based on Google Maps peak traffic modeling; actual times vary based on specific departure time, route, weather, incidents. Real-time verification with home addresses essential.

2. **AI/ML job counts** are snapshot from public job boards (June 2026); actual market significantly larger. Counts indicate relative concentration between neighborhoods, not absolute market size.

3. **Home prices** based on Zillow/Redfin median 3BR homes; luxury market and specific micro-neighborhoods show wide variance. Price per sqft ranges $700-$1000 depending on age/condition/location.

4. **School exemption policies** vary by school and change over time. Direct contact with school district and specific schools required before purchase decision; exemption policies are not guaranteed.

5. **Crime data** is 2024 preliminary (FBI UCR); local police data may vary slightly. Reported crimes represent incidents known to law enforcement; actual community safety may differ based on neighborhood micro-variations.

6. **Diversity data** based on 2024 Census ACS 5-year estimates; 2025+ data not yet available. Census estimates have ±2-3% margin of error at neighborhood level.
