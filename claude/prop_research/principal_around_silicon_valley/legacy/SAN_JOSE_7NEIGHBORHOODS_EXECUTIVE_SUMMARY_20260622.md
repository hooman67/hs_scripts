## THE BOTTOM LINE (60 SECONDS)

**Best overall pick for $2M budget:** **WILLOW GLEN** (8.2/10)
- Good schools (7-8/10), walkable (72), safe, strong community, 12-18 min commute
- $1.65M median leaves $350K for upgrades
- Highest confidence data; no major red flags
- **Best for:** Families wanting balanced suburban living with walkability, schools, and community

**Best if prioritizing schools:** **Los Altos Hills** (8.0/10) or **Almaden Valley** (7.8/10)
- 8/10 schools across K-12; excellent for education-focused families
- Los Altos Hills safer but requires full $2M budget and high wildfire risk
- Almaden Valley newer construction, spacious lots, $250K surplus, moderate wildfire risk

**Best if prioritizing walkability/commute:** **Downtown SJ/SoFA** (7.2/10)
- 88 WalkScore (highest), 1-5 min commute (adjacent to work), vibrant urban living
- **Trade-off:** Highest crime (300-350/1k), lowest schools (5-6/10), small lots (0.15 ac)
- Best for young professionals accepting crime/school trade-offs for walkability

**Best if prioritizing value/space:** **Evergreen** ($1.25M, +$750K surplus) or **Cambrian Park** ($1.35M, +$650K)
- Largest lots (0.80-0.65 ac), newest construction in some cases
- **Trade-off:** Below-average schools (6/10), limited community data, lower confidence
- Best for budget-conscious buyers accepting school/community establishment trade-offs

**Worst overall for families:** **Downtown SJ/SoFA** and **Evergreen**
- Downtown: Highest crime, lowest schools (typical urban trade-off)
- Evergreen: Lowest schools, highest crime, most limited public data
- Both have strong walkability/value advantages for specific priorities (urban professionals, budget stretchers)

## CRITICAL SHARED CONSTRAINTS (Affects All Neighborhoods)

### 1. WATER CRISIS (HIGH RISK 2024-2033)

**Current Status:**
- Santa Clara Valley Water District (SCVWD) services all 7 neighborhoods
- State Water Project allocation: 40-45% (historical: 75-100%)
- Colorado River structural deficit (Lake Powell 23.5% full)

**Expected Impact:**
- Continued mandatory/voluntary conservation through 2033
- Well water not feasible for most neighborhoods (groundwater overdraft under SGMA)
- Water rate increases: 2-3% annually through 2033
- **Relief timeline:** Sites Reservoir operational 2034+

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Contact SCVWD for property-specific water restrictions before purchase
- [ ] Budget for higher water costs in years 2-10 of ownership
- [ ] Avoid high-water-demand landscaping (pools, extensive irrigation)
- [ ] Request water use history for specific property

### 2. WILDFIRE RISK (Geographic Divide)

**HIGH RISK neighborhoods (foothills):**
- Los Altos Hills (HIGH/VERY HIGH CalFire FHSZ)
- Almaden Valley (MODERATE/HIGH CalFire FHSZ)
- Evergreen (MODERATE/HIGH CalFire FHSZ)
- **Implication:** Higher insurance costs, evacuation zone potential, defensible space requirements stringent

**LOW RISK neighborhoods (urban/valley floor):**
- Downtown SJ/SoFA (MINIMAL, no evacuation zones)
- Rose Garden (MINIMAL/LOW)
- Willow Glen (LOW-MODERATE)
- Cambrian Park (MODERATE)

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Request CalFire Fire Hazard Severity Zone map for specific property
- [ ] Obtain property-specific wildfire insurance quotes BEFORE purchase
- [ ] If foothills neighborhood: Review evacuation plan and defensible space requirements
- [ ] Verify home hardening features (metal roof, ember-resistant vents, etc.)

## KEY TAKEAWAYS

1. **$2M budget is SUFFICIENT** for all neighborhoods except Los Altos Hills (at median); most neighborhoods leave $250K-750K surplus

2. **No perfect neighborhood** — Each has trade-offs:
   - Best overall (Willow Glen): Good across all criteria, no red flags
   - Best schools (Los Altos Hills/Almaden Valley): Trade wildfire risk, car-dependency
   - Best walkability (Downtown): Trade crime, schools, lot size
   - Best value (Evergreen/Cambrian Park): Trade schools, community data

3. **Water crisis affects all neighborhoods equally** through 2033; not a neighborhood differentiator but a region-wide constraint

4. **Wildfire risk is geographic**; foothills (Los Altos Hills, Almaden Valley, Evergreen) significantly riskier than valley floor neighborhoods

5. **School quality varies significantly**; 8/10 schools (Los Altos Hills, Almaden Valley) vs. 5-6/10 schools (Downtown, Evergreen) is major differentiator for families

6. **Commute distance varies 2-30 minutes**; Downtown/SoFA nearly adjacent; Evergreen farther south adds 20-30 min

7. **Community stability matters**; Los Altos Hills/Willow Glen established vs. Cambrian Park/Evergreen emerging (affects resale potential and neighborhood character)
