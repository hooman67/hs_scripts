# Contra Costa County Neighborhoods - Systematic Research
**Date:** June 22, 2026  
**Geography:** 7 neighborhoods in Contra Costa County  
**Scope:** 11 key metrics for investment/family decision matrix  
**Confidence Standards:** HIGH (>90%), MEDIUM (70-89%), LOW (<70%)

---

## RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

### Data Collection Strategy
1. **Commute Times:** Google Maps peak traffic (8 AM weekday simulations)
2. **Crime Data:** FBI UCR + local police departments (cross-reference)
3. **Home Prices:** Zillow, Redfin, MLS recent sales
4. **Schools:** GreatSchools.org + district websites
5. **Demographics:** Census ACS 2024, Census.gov
6. **Jobs:** LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor (current postings June 2026)

### Confidence Assessment Criteria
- HIGH: Data from 2+ independent government sources or major verified databases
- MEDIUM: Data from single authoritative source with recent date (2025-2026)
- LOW: Estimates, ranges, or older data (pre-2024)
