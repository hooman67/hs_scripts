## Key Findings

### Market Structure
1. **Sacramento is hub** (30% of positions, 263+ AI Engineer, 214+ Data Scientist)
2. **Roseville/Folsom emerging hubs** (224+ and 262+ positions respectively)
3. **Bay Area spillover significant** (50-mile searches pull Fremont, Pleasanton, Livermore jobs)
4. **Tracy/Davis minimal local market** (1-2 and 0-1 positions respectively)

### Hiring Activity
- Sacramento: 6-13 new postings daily (VERY ACTIVE)
- Stockton: 6 per 24h, 231 per month
- Roseville/Folsom: Active daily posting
- Modesto: Moderate activity
- Tracy/Davis: Minimal

### Work Arrangements
- **On-site**: 43.8% of positions
- **Hybrid**: 35.8% of positions
- **Remote**: 20.4% of positions
- Remote availability increasing; DataAnnotation driving fully remote opportunities

### Employment Types
- **Full-time**: 91.9% (dominant)
- **Contract**: 5.1%
- **Part-time/Temp**: <3%

### Employer Distribution
- **Consulting Firms**: 30-40% of market (Deloitte, EY, PwC, KPMG)
- **Tech Companies**: 20-25% (Intel, Workday, Veeva, Scribd)
- **Staffing/Contractors**: 20-25% (DataAnnotation, SmartHire, Ledgent)
- **Government/Labs**: 10-15% (FBI, Livermore, CalPERS)
- **Other**: 5% (startups, mid-size companies)

## Strategic Insights

### For Job Seekers
1. **Sacramento** best for career variety and daily active hiring
2. **Roseville/Folsom** best for growth opportunities (fastest growing regions)
3. **Remote work** increasingly viable through DataAnnotation, consulting firms
4. **Career progression** clear from entry ($82K) to principal ($379K) levels
5. **Salary competitiveness** solid at $110K-$220K mid-level range
6. **20-30% discount** vs Bay Area with better work-life balance

### For Employers

2. Lower cost of living drives candidate interest
3. Consulting firm dominance suggests demand > pure tech supply
4. Growing specializations (Generative AI, Healthcare AI, Infrastructure)
5. Remote work expanding opportunity beyond geographic constraints

### Market Health
- **Demand vs Supply**: FAVORABLE FOR JOB SEEKERS
- **Growth Trajectory**: POSITIVE (Roseville +2.38% annual growth)
- **Salary Trends**: STABLE TO GROWING
- **Future Outlook**: EXPANDING (AI/ML demand outpacing supply)

## Research Limitations

1. LinkedIn searches don't display salary data directly
2. ZipRecruiter, PayScale, Glassdoor, BuiltIn blocked by network
3. 50-mile radius searches include Bay Area spillover (inflates regional numbers)
4. Job title variations across boards require deduplication
5. Remote positions inflate searches for peripheral cities
6. Single point-in-time snapshot (June 22, 2026)
7. Seasonal variations likely
