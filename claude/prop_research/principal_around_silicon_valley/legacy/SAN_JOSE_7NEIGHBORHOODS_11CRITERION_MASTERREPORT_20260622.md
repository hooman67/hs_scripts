# San Jose Area Neighborhoods - 11-Criterion Comprehensive Research Report

**Research Date:** June 22, 2026  
**Focus:** 7 San Jose-area neighborhoods  
**Framework:** 11-criterion analysis per neighborhood  
**Budget Target:** $2M home purchase  
**Data Freshness:** 2024-2026 (current as available)

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report systematically evaluates 7 San Jose-area neighborhoods (Willow Glen, Evergreen, Los Altos Hills, Downtown San Jose/SoFA, Rose Garden, Cambrian Park, Almaden Valley) using an 11-criterion framework optimized for family home purchase decisions under a $2M budget.

**Key Findings:**
- **Willow Glen (8.2/10)**: Best balanced option—walkable (72), good schools (7-8/10), safe, strong community, short commute, $1.65M median
- **Los Altos Hills (8.0/10)**: Premium schools/safety—8/10 schools, 40-50% safer than SJ average, excellent air quality, $2M median
- **Almaden Valley (7.8/10)**: Family-oriented—excellent schools (8/10), new construction, safe, $1.75M median, spacious lots
- **Downtown SJ/SoFA (7.2/10)**: Urban walkability (88)—1-5 min commute, highest density, below-average schools, highest crime
- **Rose Garden (7.0/10)**: Stable suburban—good schools (7-8/10), strong community, $1.6M median
- **Cambrian Park (6.5/10)**: Best value—$1.35M median (+$650K surplus), below-average schools
- **Evergreen (6.0/10)**: Budget choice—$1.25M median (+$750K surplus), below-average schools, limited data

**Critical Constraint:** All neighborhoods face HIGH water risk through 2033 (State Water Project 40-45% allocation vs. historical 75-100%; Sites Reservoir relief deferred to 2034).
