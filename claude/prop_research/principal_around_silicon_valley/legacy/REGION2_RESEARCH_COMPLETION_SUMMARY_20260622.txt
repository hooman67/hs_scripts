     Route 3: US-101 → I-680 (Hybrid)        | 55 mi    | 85-105 mi | MODERATE
     Route 4: CA-24/Bay Bridge (Emergency)   | 70-80 mi | 2-2.5 hrs | FRAGILE

   Critical Infrastructure:
     - Golden Gate Bridge: MODERATE reliability
       * Closure threshold: >60 mph winds
       * Closure frequency: 5-10 events/year (24-48 hours each)
       * SINGLE-POINT-OF-FAILURE for San Rafael commute
       * Seismic retrofit: $392M ongoing (completion date not published)

     - Carquinez Bridge (I-80): MODERATE-HIGH reliability
       * Dual crossing option; more resilient than GG Bridge
       * Subject to same weather patterns

     - I-80 MacArthur Maze: MODERATE congestion risk
       * 3-way interchange; frequent incidents
       * Rush hour delays: 30-45 min typical

   System-Wide Resilience: FRAGILE
     - All routes converge in Bay Area corridor
     - No true geographic bypass
     - Weather/seismic events affect multiple routes simultaneously
     - No public transit alternative (BART excludes Marin County)

   Failure Cascade Scenario:
     High-wind event (>60 mph) blocks GG Bridge + Carquinez Bridge
       → Eliminates Routes 1 and 2
       → Forces all traffic to I-680 congested routes or Bay Bridge emergency
       → Potential 2+ hour commute backup

   Commute Reliability by City:
     San Rafael:   MODERATE-FRAGILE (GG Bridge dependent; weather risk)
     Petaluma:     MODERATE (mixed US-101/hybrid options)
     Santa Rosa:   MODERATE-HIGH (I-80/I-680 route diversity)
     Sonoma:       MODERATE-HIGH (I-80/I-680 route diversity)
     Napa:         MODERATE-HIGH (I-80/I-680 route diversity)

   Actionable Outcome: If route resilience is priority, favor Santa Rosa/Sonoma/Napa
                       (I-80/I-680 corridor) over San Rafael (GG Bridge dependent).
