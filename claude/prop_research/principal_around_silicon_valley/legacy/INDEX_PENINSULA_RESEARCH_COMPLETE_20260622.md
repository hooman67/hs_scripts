## ⚠️ IMPORTANT CAVEATS

### Data Limitations
1. **Commute times:** Google Maps estimates; actual times vary by traffic pattern/time of day
2. **Housing prices:** Snapshot in June 2026; market fluctuates
3. **School ratings:** Composite of test scores + parent reviews (subjective element)
4. **Lot sizes:** Median from recent listings; individual properties vary significantly
5. **AI jobs:** LinkedIn/Indeed estimates; actual postings change daily
6. **Vaccine policies:** Must verify directly with schools (policies change)

### Confidence Levels
- **HIGH:** Crime (FBI UCR), demographics (Census), fire/flood zones (official maps)
- **MEDIUM:** Housing prices, school ratings, commute times, appreciation trends
- **LOW:** Vaccine policies, parent network strength, AI job exact counts

### What You Still Need to Verify
1. **Commute:** Drive it yourself during rush hour with YOUR work address
2. **Schools:** Call 5-10 schools directly about vaccine policies
3. **Community:** Visit neighborhoods, talk to residents, join Facebook groups
4. **Properties:** Get flood/fire/insurance reports for specific addresses
5. **Homeschool:** Research local homeschool co-ops (not covered in this research)
