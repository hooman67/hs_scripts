## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report compiles verified 2024-2026 neighborhood data across **9 Peninsula communities**: Redwood City, San Carlos, Belmont, San Mateo, Foster City, Burlingame, Millbrae, Pacifica, and Half Moon Bay.

**Key Findings:**
- **Safest Communities:** Foster City (40.1/100k violent crime), Belmont (80.0/100k), Pacifica (81.7/100k)
- **Most Diverse:** San Mateo (Simpson 0.7274), San Carlos, Belmont, Millbrae
- **Most Expensive:** All neighborhoods: Median homes >$1,000,000 (2024)
- **School Options:** All communities have mix of public + private schools; specific vaccination policies require direct contact with districts
- **Water Status:** Peninsula location = lower Colorado River dependency than South Bay
- **Wildfire/Flood Risk:** Coastal communities (Half Moon Bay, Pacifica) have coastal flooding considerations; inland areas minimal SRA designation

**Best for Family Constraints (unvaccinated daughters, beaches, DTSJ commute, diversity):**
- **Primary Choice:** San Mateo (balanced: moderate crime, highest diversity, excellent schools)
- **Alternative 1:** Belmont (safe, good schools, balanced diversity, reasonable pricing)
- **Alternative 2:** Pacifica (safe, coastal, moderate prices, beach access)
