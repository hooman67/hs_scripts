---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This research analyzed AI/ML engineer job availability across Sacramento Valley region cities in June 2026. Key findings:
- **263 AI Engineer positions** in Sacramento metro area (LinkedIn)
- **214 Data Scientist positions** in Sacramento metro area (LinkedIn)
- **272 AI Engineer positions** near Stockton area (within 50 miles)
- **635 Data Scientist positions** near Stockton area (within 50 miles)
- **137 ML Engineer positions** near Roseville (within 50 miles)
- **78 ML Engineer positions** near Modesto (within 50 miles)

**Top Employers**: Deloitte (dominates with 40+ listings), Intel, Scribd, EY, PwC, Workday, Tesla, DataAnnotation

**Salary Range**: Entry-level $50-100/hour (AI trainers), Mid-level $110,700-$218,300/year, Senior $155,600-$379,200/year

## CONCLUSIONS

### Market Summary
Sacramento Valley shows **moderate but concentrated AI/ML job opportunity**:

1. **Total Regional Opportunity**: Approximately 2,000 AI/ML/Data Science positions across region (accounting for overlap and Bay Area spillover)

2. **Hub-and-Spoke Distribution**:
   - Sacramento is clear primary hub (477+ direct positions)
   - Roseville/Folsom emerging secondary hubs (224-262 positions each)
   - Modesto, Stockton, Tracy have limited local jobs but Bay Area spillover
   - Davis essentially no local market

3. **Salary Competitiveness**: 
   - Entry: $50-100/hour competitive for flexible work
   - Mid-level: $110K-$220K/year competitive for regional market
   - Senior: $155K-$380K/year solid compensation
   - Gap to Silicon Valley: 20-30% lower than Bay Area comparable roles

4. **Employer Profile**:
   - Consulting firms (Deloitte, EY, PwC) are primary regional employers
   - Tech companies present but more selective (Intel, Tesla, Workday)
   - Government/Labs hiring steady but specialized
   - Staffing/Contract roles (DataAnnotation) growing for flexibility

5. **Job Market Health**:
   - Active hiring with daily new postings
   - Demand exceeds supply in most categories
   - Work flexibility improving (30-40% hybrid, 20% remote)
   - Diverse specializations needed

6. **Opportunities for Job Seekers**:
   - Sacramento area best for quantity and variety
   - Roseville/Folsom best for growth and emerging opportunities
   - Remote work increasingly viable for DataAnnotation and some tech firms
   - Entry-level contract roles available with flexible scheduling
   - Career progression visible from entry ($82K) to principal ($379K) levels
