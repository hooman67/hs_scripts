## Research Methodology

### Sources Prioritized (by confidence tier)
- **HIGH (Official Government):** Zillow API, Redfin, Census Bureau (ACS 2024), GreatSchools.org, FBI UCR, Cal Fire SRA, USDA Plant Hardiness Zones
- **MEDIUM (Aggregators):** NeighborhoodScout, Google Maps commute data, CityData
- **LOW (User-generated/Partial):** Reddit, local forums, blog posts, outdated aggregates

### Data Collection Strategy
For each neighborhood and data point:
1. Primary source search (official/government)
2. Secondary source validation (2-3 cross-checks)
3. Current date verification (mid-2026 focus)
4. Confidence rating assignment
5. Source URL documentation

## Data Quality Notes

### Known Limitations
1. **Commute Times:** Will reflect mid-June 2026 off-peak estimates; traffic can vary significantly
2. **Price Data:** Mid-2026 snapshot; market may have changed since last MLS refresh
3. **Crime Stats:** Most recent available (may be 2025 or early 2026 depending on agency reporting lag)
4. **Diversity Data:** Census ACS typically lags 1-2 years behind current date
5. **Alternative Schools:** May not capture all unlisted/micro schools in area

### Data Validation Approach
- Cross-referencing prices across Zillow, Redfin, and local MLS data
- Verifying crime statistics against multiple agency sources
- Checking demographic data currency (2023-2024 focus)
- Flagging any data points that couldn't be sourced with "DATA NOT FOUND"
