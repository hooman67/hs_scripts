## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR PRECISE DATA

To obtain the exact March-May 2026 sales data you requested:

1. **Zillow Direct Method (15 min per city):**
   - Visit https://www.zillow.com/homes/for_sale/
   - Filter: City → Beds: 3+ → Sqft: 1700+ → Type: Recently Sold
   - Note min/max/median prices; scroll for 20-40 comparable sales
   - Screenshot or record data in spreadsheet

2. **Redfin Direct Method (20 min per city):**
   - Visit https://www.redfin.com/city/[city]/[state]/homes-for-sale
   - Filter: Beds 3+, Sqft 1700+, Status: Sold
   - Set date range: March 1-May 31, 2026
   - Export CSV or manually tabulate sales

3. **County Assessor Method (45 min per county):**
   - Access online or visit recorder's office
   - Search for "recent sales" by property type/size
   - Cross-reference with Zillow/Redfin for date/price verification

4. **Real Estate Agent CMA:**
   - Contact local RE/MAX, Berkshire Hathaway, or independent brokers
   - Request Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) for 3BR 1700+ sqft SFH
   - Often provided free for buyer consultation
