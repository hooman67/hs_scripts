# Peninsula & South Bay Region (CA) - Comprehensive Property Research Report
**Research Date:** June 22, 2026  
**Data Scope:** 10 Cities across San Mateo, Santa Clara, and adjacent counties  
**Prepared For:** CA home purchase evaluation ($2M budget target)

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report synthesizes four critical property investment metrics across the Peninsula & South Bay region: violent crime rates, racial/ethnic diversity indices, percent non-white population, and water security constraints. All data sourced from authoritative federal databases (FBI UCR, US Census Bureau ACS) and state water agencies, with 2023-2026 recency where available.

**Key Takeaway:** South Bay cities show relatively lower violent crime (trending downward 2023-2025), high racial/ethnic diversity (Simpson Index >0.59 in most cities), and **medium-high water security constraints through 2026**, with relief deferred to 2034 via Sites Reservoir infrastructure.

### Colorado River Crisis (Critical to SCVWD Supply)

California receives 4.4 million acre-feet (MAF) annually from the Colorado River, but the river is **overallocated by 20%** (allocated 120% of reliably available flows). This creates structural water deficit affecting SCVWD.

**Risk Assessment:** Lake Powell critically low; even with recent precipitation improvements, structural Colorado River deficit persists. Allocation rules post-December 2025 create uncertainty for CA's SWP entitlement.

### Proposition 1 & Proposition 68 Impacts

**Proposition 1 (2014) - $7.1B Water Bond:**
- **Primary Project:** Sites Reservoir (Sacramento River storage infrastructure)
- **Capacity:** 1.8 million acre-feet annual storage potential
- **Timeline:** Construction begins 2027; operational 2034
- **Funding:** $3.9B from Prop 1 bonds + federal infrastructure investment (Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act)
- **Relief Window:** No relief until 2034 (8 years away; current date June 2026)
- **Long-term Impact:** Post-2034 operationalization will buffer dry-year supply gaps

**Proposition 68 (2018) - $4.1B Water Bond:**
- **Scope:** Water infrastructure and ecosystem restoration grants (distributed 2018-2026)
- **SCVWD-Specific Allocation:** Not clearly quantified in public sources
- **Impact:** Supplementary projects; not primary drought relief mechanism

**Governor's 2026 Directive:** California targeting 9 million acre-feet statewide supply by 2040 (long-term planning horizon).

**Confidence:** HIGH for Prop 1 Sites Reservoir timeline | MEDIUM for Prop 68 SCVWD specifics

### Water Security Risk Assessment for Property Investment

**Overall Risk Level for Region: MEDIUM-HIGH (2024-2026)**

**High-Risk Factors:**
1. Colorado River overallocated 20%; Lake Powell critically low (23.5%)
2. SWP allocation cuts (40-45% vs. historical 75-100%)

4. Groundwater overdraft continuing; SGMA (Sustainable Groundwater Management Act) enforcement insufficient
5. No major infrastructure relief until 2034 (Sites Reservoir)

**Mitigating Factors:**
- SWP allocation improving (45% vs. 30% year prior)
- Recent storms (2025) modestly improved Lake Mead/Powell levels
- Recycled water programs expanding
- Conservation awareness and tiered pricing incentivizing efficiency

**City-Specific Risk Ranking (Highest to Lowest):**

4. **Lower Risk:** Los Gatos, Saratoga, Redwood City, San Mateo (peninsula location; less Colorado River dependent; local/recycled water options)
