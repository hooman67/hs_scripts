DATA QUALITY ASSURANCE PROTOCOL
================================================================================

VALIDATION APPROACH:
  - Prices: Flag >10% variance between sources
  - Crime: Flag >20% variance between FBI UCR and NeighborhoodScout
  - Demographics: Prioritize Census over aggregators
  - Commute: Collect multiple time estimates, average out
  - All data: Record source URL, confidence tier, collection date

CONFIDENCE TIER FRAMEWORK:
  HIGH:   Official government sources (Census, FBI UCR, Cal Fire, USDA)
  MEDIUM: Aggregator platforms with current data (Zillow, Redfin, Google Maps)
  LOW:    User-generated, outdated (>2023), or partial information

ERROR HANDLING:
  - Any data point not found → marked "DATA NOT FOUND" with LOW confidence
  - Alternative sources identified for follow-up
  - Conflicting values → cross-check against third source before deciding
  - Outliers flagged for manual verification
