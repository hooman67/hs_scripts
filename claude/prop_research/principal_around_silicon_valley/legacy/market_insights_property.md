### 9. PROPERTY INVESTMENT THESIS

**For Family Seeking $2M Budget in N.CA/Santa Barbara Region:**

**Silicon Valley Strategic Considerations:**

1. **Santa Clara/Sunnyvale Zone (51,000+ employees combined):**
   - PROS: Highest employment concentration, strongest demand
   - CONS: Highest prices, limited inventory, mixed industrial/residential zoning
   - Best for: Proximity-focused families wanting minimal commute
   - Est. homes in $2M range: Limited (mostly $2.2M+)

2. **San Jose (30,000+ employees):**
   - PROS: More housing inventory, reasonable commute to Santa Clara cluster
   - CONS: Urban growth boundary constraints
   - Best for: Families willing 20-30 min commute
   - Est. homes in $2M range: Moderate supply

3. **Fremont (25,000 Tesla employees):**
   - PROS: More affordable than north valley, newer construction
   - CONS: Commute to Santa Clara/Mountain View (30-50 min)
   - Best for: Families working at Tesla or OK with longer commute
   - Est. homes in $2M range: Better supply than south SF

4. **San Francisco (40,000+ tech employees, AI premium):**
   - PROS: City living, AI job concentration, walkability
   - CONS: $2M = smaller space, limited family-size homes
   - Best for: Smaller households, tech/AI workers
   - Est. homes in $2M range: Smaller SF condos/townhomes only

5. **Peninsula (Palo Alto, Menlo Park, Mountain View):**
   - PROS: Proximity to major tech hubs, excellent schools
   - CONS: Very limited $2M homes (mostly $3M+ range)
   - Best for: Above $2M budget required

**South Bay Secondary Markets to Monitor:**
- Saratoga, Los Gatos: $2.2-2.8M range, 30-40 min to Santa Clara
- Cupertino: $2.8M+ (Apple premium pricing)
- Milpitas: $1.9-2.3M (closer to north valley hubs)
