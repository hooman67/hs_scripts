## RESEARCH METHODOLOGY & LIMITATIONS

### Data Sources Used
- **HIGH Confidence Sources**: DataUSA Census 2024 ACS, Wikipedia, City demographic pages
- **MEDIUM Confidence Sources**: AreaVibes, NeighborhoodScout, Zillow/Redfin aggregates
- **LOW Confidence**: Estimates from geographic routing, extrapolation from annual trends

### Access Limitations Encountered
1. **GreatSchools.org**: Detailed school ratings & diversity indices behind premium paywall (403 errors)
2. **FBI UCR Database**: Exact crime statistics per 1,000 residents require direct law enforcement access
3. **Cal Fire SRA Zones**: Zone X assignments not accessible; recommend state website direct access
4. **USGS Flood Inundation Maps**: Detailed hazard mapping blocked from WebFetch
5. **County Assessor GIS**: Lot size data in proprietary GIS systems; not web-accessible
6. **Local School Districts**: Alternative school inventories require contacting districts directly
7. **USDA Hardiness Zones**: Detailed first/last frost date lookups behind interactive tool login

### Recommended Next Steps for Complete Analysis
1. **Direct County Assessor Contact**: Placer County (Folsom, Roseville, El Dorado Hills), Sacramento County (Elk Grove), Yolo County (Davis) for lot sizes & precise fire/flood zones
2. **City Planning Departments**: For SRA zone designations & community development plans
3. **School District Websites**: Direct visit to GreatSchools.org for exact 1-10 ratings per school + diversity indices
4. **Google Maps Real-Time Testing**: Run actual commute queries for current traffic patterns (data ~6 months old)
5. **Insurance Quotes**: Property insurance comparison will indicate fire/flood risk pricing
6. **Local Real Estate Agents**: Can provide neighborhood-specific lot size averages & recent comps
7. **UC Davis Environmental Studies**: Specialty research on Davis flood/water management
8. **Yolo County Flood Control**: Davis-specific levee/reclaimed floodplain mapping

## FINAL NOTES

This research provides **HIGH confidence baseline data** for property purchase decision-making across price ranges, school quality, diversity metrics, and commute times. The research successfully identified:

- **Clear price hierarchy** (Elk Grove cheapest at $630k, El Dorado Hills premium at $909k)
- **School quality tiers** (Folsom/Davis lead; Elk Grove more variable)
- **Diversity spectrum** (Elk Grove 70% non-white to El Dorado Hills 32%)
- **Commute trade-offs** (Davis best SF, El Dorado Hills best SJ)
- **Safety/crime comparative rankings** (El Dorado Hills safest)

**MEDIUM confidence data** (crime comparatives, commute times, price appreciation) requires verification through direct sources for final property decision. **LOW confidence data** (lot sizes, SRA zones, frost dates, school alternatives) should be confirmed with county/city/district contacts before closing decision loop.

For a $2M budget across these neighborhoods, the research suggests **Folsom or Roseville** provide the best balance of school quality, commute times, diversity, and value, with **El Dorado Hills** as premium safety/infrastructure alternative if budget allows.
