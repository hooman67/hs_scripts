# San Jose Area Neighborhoods - Comprehensive Property Research
**Research Date:** June 22, 2026  
**Focus:** 7 San Jose-area neighborhoods  
**Framework:** 11-criterion analysis  
**Budget Target:** $2M home purchase  
**Purpose:** CA home purchase pipeline evaluation

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Research on 7 San Jose-area neighborhoods (Willow Glen, Evergreen, Los Altos Hills, Downtown San Jose/SoFA, Rose Garden, Cambrian Park, Almaden Valley) using 11-criterion framework. Key finding: **Willow Glen and Los Altos Hills emerge as optimal for $2M budget with moderate commute, strong schools, lower crime; Almaden Valley offers affordability with acceptable crime/diversity; Downtown/SoFA highest walkability but elevated crime; Evergreen, Rose Garden, Cambrian Park present limited public data and warrant neighborhood verification.**
