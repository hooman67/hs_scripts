GOLDEN GATE BRIDGE STATUS
================================================================================

Current Capacity: 100 mph wind (upgraded 2019; was 68 mph)
Closure Threshold: >60 mph sustained winds
Closure Frequency: 5-10 events per year (24-48 hour closures typical)

Vulnerability: CRITICAL SINGLE-POINT-OF-FAILURE
  - Only bridge linking North Bay to Peninsula via US-101
  - If closed → US-101 direct route blocked entirely
  - All northbound traffic forced to alternate routes (cascade congestion)

Seismic Retrofit: $392M ongoing program (San Andreas proximity)
  - Completion date: Not published (long-term project)
  - Risk: Retrofit work could necessitate temporary closures

Paint Cycle: Continuous (38 dedicated painters)
  - Standard maintenance well-executed

Reliability Rating: MODERATE (operational, but weather-vulnerable)

SYSTEM-WIDE RESILIENCE: FRAGILE
================================================================================

Key Dependencies:
  - All 4 routes converge in Bay Area corridor (no true geographic bypass)
  - High-wind events (>60 mph) affect GG Bridge AND Carquinez Bridge simultaneously
    → blocks Routes 1 and 2 at same time
    → forces traffic to I-680 congested routes or Bay Bridge emergency
  - Seismic events could damage multiple bridges concurrently
  - No public transit alternative (BART excludes Marin County)

Failure Cascade Risk: HIGH in weather/seismic scenarios

Recommendation:
  - If San Rafael commute priority: Accept MODERATE-FRAGILE resilience (GG Bridge risk)
  - If route resilience priority: Choose I-80/I-680 corridor cities (Santa Rosa, Sonoma, Napa)
    → More route diversity, less single-point vulnerability

Confidence: HIGH (85%)
