## Research Methodology

**Sources Used:**
1. **Wikipedia** - Demographics, economics, housing characteristics for all 8 neighborhoods
2. **DataUSA** - Median household income, median property values, employment, commute times (Census/ACS data)
3. **NeighborhoodScout** - Property crime rates per 1,000 residents for all neighborhoods
4. **GreatSchools.org** - School district identification and general rating categories
5. **LinkedIn Job Search** - AI/ML job listings and employer headquarters data
6. **USGS** - Earthquake fault maps and seismic hazard information
7. **CA Route 17 Wikipedia** - Drive times and route characteristics to Santa Cruz
8. **Company Directories** - Major employer headquarters and employment data (Apple, Google, Netflix, Cisco, etc.)

**Gaps & Limitations:**
- Real estate listing sites (Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com) blocked direct access - data sourced from Wikipedia aggregations and DataUSA Census values
- School ratings on 1-10 scale not standardized - GreatSchools uses different methodologies
- Alternative school directory was largely inaccessible; counts are estimates from scattered web sources
- Commute times are averages; actual 8 AM rush hour times vary by specific route and traffic conditions
- Natural disaster risk is general assessment based on fault proximity; specific FEMA flood maps would require individual property assessment
- 5-year appreciation percentages are estimates based on DataUSA median property value changes; individual properties vary significantly

## Recommendations for Further Research

1. **Price Verification**: Use Zillow/Redfin desktop browsers to verify 3BR median prices and track recent sales data
2. **School-Specific Ratings**: Visit GreatSchools.org individual school pages for detailed 1-10 ratings and parent reviews
3. **Alternative Schools**: Contact Montessori and Waldorf associations directly (AWSNA, AMS) for verified school directories
4. **Commute Validation**: Use Google Maps with "Depart at 8:00 AM" option for real-time traffic estimates on specific workdays
5. **Flood Zone Assessment**: Cross-reference FEMA Flood Insurance Rate Maps (FIRMS) and Santa Clara County GIS flood data
6. **Earthquake Risk**: Review USGS earthquake hazard maps and consider seismic retrofitting costs by neighborhood
7. **Job Market**: Access LinkedIn with account for more detailed job counts by specific neighborhoods
8. **Property Details**: County assessor databases (Santa Clara County) for detailed lot sizes and property characteristics
