## RESEARCH LIMITATIONS & DATA GAPS

1. **Neighborhood crime rates:** San Jose Police doesn't publish neighborhood-level data. Estimates based on demographics and premium services.
   - **Fix:** Use NeighborhoodScout premium ($30-50) or file SJPD data request

2. **Cambrian Park & Evergreen:** Emerging neighborhoods with limited historical public data.
   - **Fix:** Tour neighborhoods in person; talk to residents

3. **Vaccination exemption rates:** Not published by neighborhood.
   - **Fix:** Contact individual schools or districts

4. **Well water feasibility:** Site-specific and requires geological survey.
   - **Fix:** Get property-specific water report from seller

5. **Census data neighborhoods:** Census tracts don't align with neighborhood boundaries; estimates use zip code proxies.
   - **Fix:** Use Census Reporter for exact zip codes in consideration
