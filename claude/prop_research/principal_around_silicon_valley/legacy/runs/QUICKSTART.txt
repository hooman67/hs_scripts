================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Q: Zillow page shows "403 Forbidden"?
A: Try incognito mode or clear cache. Or skip to Redfin + MLS.

Q: Can't find "market insights" on Redfin?
A: Scroll down, or try mobile version. Look for median price stat.

Q: Agents not responding?
A: Call directly instead of email. Try 2-3 agents per town.

Q: Zillow and Redfin differ by a lot?
A: Check dates (one may be stale). Use most recent. Note the discrepancy.

Q: Can't find 3BR-specific data?
A: Use overall median. Note it in JSON. Add to "notes": "Overall median, not 3BR-specific"
