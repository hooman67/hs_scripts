### Vaccination Policy & School Options (CRITICAL)

**California SB 277 Context:**
- Since 2016, California eliminated personal belief exemptions for vaccines
- Only MEDICAL EXEMPTIONS (signed by licensed physician) are accepted for public and private schools
- SB 276 (2019) added oversight requiring state review of medical exemptions

**Options for Unvaccinated Children in Pleasanton:**

1. **Homeschooling (BEST OPTION):** California homeschoolers are NOT subject to immunization requirements (confirmed by HSLDA). Three legal options:
   - File private school affidavit (PSA) as a home-based private school
   - Enroll in a private school satellite program (PSP)
   - Use a credentialed private tutor

2. **Independent Study Charter Schools:**
   - **Pleasanton Virtual Academy** (PUSD-operated) — Note: as a public school program, vaccination would still be required for enrollment
   - **California Virtual Academy (CAVA)** — statewide online charter; vaccination requirements may be waived for fully online students
   - **Connecting Waters Charter School** (various East Bay locations) — independent study model

3. **Private Schools with Potentially Higher Medical Exemption Rates:**
   - **Big Minds Unschool** (22 students, alternative philosophy) — Small size and unschooling philosophy may indicate more flexibility; contact directly
   - **Lighthouse Baptist School** (12 students) — Very small enrollment; religious schools sometimes have communities more sympathetic to exemptions
   - **Fusion Academy** (1:1 model, no vaccination policy mentioned on website) — individualized instruction may offer flexibility
   - **Hacienda School (Montessori)** — Small enrollment (41 students); Montessori communities sometimes have higher exemption rates

4. **Homeschool Co-ops in the Area:**
   - Tri-Valley Homeschool Co-op (Facebook group, ~200+ families)
   - East Bay Homeschoolers (regional group with Pleasanton members)
   - Classical Conversations Pleasanton/Tri-Valley community
   - Wild + Free Tri-Valley (nature-based homeschool group)

**RECOMMENDATION:** Given California's strict vaccination laws, homeschooling with participation in co-ops and enrichment programs is the most viable path. The Pleasanton community has a strong homeschool network given its affluent, education-focused population.

## Section 3d: Survivability & Environment

### Water Supply

**Water District:** Zone 7 Water Agency (Tri-Valley wholesale supplier)
- **Service area:** Pleasanton, Livermore, Dublin, Dougherty Valley
- **Sources:** State Water Project (Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta), local groundwater (Livermore Valley Basin), local surface water
- **Storage:** Lake Del Valle, groundwater recharge facilities
- **Reliability:** ADEQUATE — Zone 7 has invested significantly in supply diversification and groundwater banking
- **Conservation:** Voluntary conservation measures; rebate programs for efficient landscaping
- **Concerns:** Dependent on State Water Project (~80% of supply), which is vulnerable to Delta earthquake, drought, and regulatory curtailment

**Well Drilling:**
- Groundwater in the Livermore Valley Basin is managed by Zone 7
- Private wells exist in unincorporated areas but are regulated within city limits
- Depth to water table: 30-100 feet depending on location
- City lots typically cannot drill private wells

### Wildfire Risk

**CAL FIRE FHSZ Designation:** LOW to MODERATE for most of the city

- **Eastern hills and Augustin Bernal Park area:** Moderate to High fire hazard severity
- **Downtown/valley floor:** Minimal risk (non-FHSZ or Low)
- **Ruby Hill and south Pleasanton:** Low to Moderate
- **Western Pleasanton (flat developments):** Non-FHSZ (minimal risk)

### Flight Path & Noise

- **Livermore Municipal Airport:** ~5 miles east. Small general aviation, no commercial service.
- **Impact on Pleasanton:** Minimal.
- **Oakland International/San Jose International:** Neither directly affects Pleasanton.

## Section 3e: Immigration & Legal

### Sanctuary Status

**California is a sanctuary state** under SB 54 (California Values Act, 2017):
- Prohibits state/local law enforcement from assisting federal immigration enforcement
- Limits information sharing with ICE
- Prohibits honoring ICE detainer requests without judicial warrant

**Alameda County:** Strongly pro-sanctuary with additional local protections.

**City of Pleasanton:** Protected by state and county sanctuary laws.

Pleasanton offers exceptional quality of life for families with top-tier public schools, outstanding safety, strong property values, and a charming downtown core. The $1.5M-$2M budget comfortably gets a 4BR/2,000+ sqft home with a backyard. This is an ideal suburb for school quality and safety but requires cultural compromises.
