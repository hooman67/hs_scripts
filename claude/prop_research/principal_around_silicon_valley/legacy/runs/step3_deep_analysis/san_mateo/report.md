### What $1.4M-$2.0M Buys in San Mateo

Based on market data and neighborhood analysis:

- **$1.4M-$1.6M:** Entry-level 3BR/2BA ranch-style home (1,200-1,500 sqft) in Hayward Park or North Central. Likely built 1940s-1960s, 4,000-5,000 sqft lot. May need cosmetic updates. Below the 1,700 sqft minimum at this tier.
- **$1.6M-$1.8M:** Solid 3BR/2BA home (1,500-1,800 sqft) in Beresford or Hillsdale neighborhoods. Mid-century homes with updated kitchens, 5,000-6,000 sqft lots. Some at or near the size requirement.
- **$1.8M-$2.0M:** Well-maintained 3-4BR/2BA home (1,700-2,100 sqft) in San Mateo Park or Baywood. These are the desirable family neighborhoods. 5,000-7,000 sqft lots with mature landscaping and usable backyards. Likely updated post-war construction.
- **At $2.0M ceiling:** Access to the lower end of Baywood/Aragon or a nicely renovated Hillsdale home with 1,800-2,200 sqft, 3-4BR, backyard suitable for kids and dog.

### Property Tax Detail

- **Base rate (Prop 13):** 1% of purchase price
- **Additional voter-approved bonds & assessments:** ~0.1-0.3% depending on location
- **Mello-Roos:** The city of San Mateo has LIMITED Mello-Roos districts. Older established neighborhoods (San Mateo Park, Baywood, Hillsdale) generally do NOT have Mello-Roos. The Bay Meadows redevelopment area does carry Mello-Roos assessments (~$3,000-5,000/year additional).
- **Effective total rate:** ~1.1-1.2% for most established neighborhoods
- **Annual tax on a $1.8M purchase:** ~$19,800-$21,600

### Market Conditions

- **Days on Market:** San Mateo remains competitive; well-priced homes in desirable neighborhoods typically sell within 14-21 days
- **Sale-to-List Ratio:** Competitive market with homes frequently selling at or above asking in popular sub-neighborhoods
- **Appreciation (last 5 years):** 22.93% total (4.22% annually) - modest by Bay Area standards
- **Appreciation (last 12 months):** 0.13% - essentially flat, creating a potential buyer's window
- **Inventory:** Low - typical of Peninsula markets. San Mateo's housing stock is 77% built before 1970, limiting new supply

### Recent Market Trends

The near-flat appreciation (0.13% YoY) combined with high interest rates has created slightly softer conditions compared to 2021-2022. Price reductions are more common than during peak pandemic demand, particularly for homes priced above $1.8M. This represents a relatively favorable entry point compared to recent years.

### CRITICAL: Vaccination & School Options

**California Law (SB 277, effective 2016; SB 276, effective 2020):**
- Eliminated personal belief exemptions for school vaccinations
- Only MEDICAL exemptions remain for brick-and-mortar schools (public AND private)
- Medical exemptions must be issued by a licensed California physician and are reviewed by CDPH
- Since SB 276 (2020), the state reviews and can reject medical exemptions, making them harder to obtain

**Options for Unvaccinated/Under-vaccinated Children:**

1. **Home-Based Private School (Homeschool via PSA)**
   - File a Private School Affidavit with the state
   - No vaccination requirement
   - Parent acts as teacher
   - Complete flexibility in curriculum
   - California does NOT require specific qualifications to homeschool

2. **Independent Study Charter Schools (Homeschool Charters)**
   - Programs serving the Bay Area include:
     - Connecting Waters Charter School (serves statewide)
     - Pacific Coast Academy (virtual, serves Bay Area)
   - These provide curriculum funds ($2,500-3,500/student/year) and credentialed teacher oversight
   - Note: Independent study charters technically require immunization records, but enforcement varies

3. **Homeschool Co-ops (No vaccination requirement)**
   - San Mateo County Homeschoolers (Facebook group - active community)
   - Bay Area homeschool community groups
   - Peninsula families form informal learning pods
   - Groups meet in parks, community centers, and homes

4. **Private Schools with Higher Medical Exemption Rates**
   - Small private schools and Montessori programs historically had slightly higher medical exemption rates
   - Post-SB 276, ALL schools (public and private) must comply with state vaccination mandates
   - No school in California can legally admit unvaccinated children without a valid medical exemption

**Recommended Path:**
- **Best option: File a Private School Affidavit (homeschool) combined with participation in homeschool co-ops and enrichment programs**
- Supplement with classes at community centers, sports programs, and art schools (which do not require vaccination records)
- Consider hiring a part-time tutor or joining a learning pod with like-minded families

| Drought Resilience | Good - SFPUC maintains multiple backup sources |

**Assessment: ADEQUATE** - San Mateo benefits from one of the most reliable water systems in California (SFPUC/Hetch Hetchy). Crystal Springs Reservoir is literally within the city boundaries.

### Well Drilling Feasibility

- **Regulation:** San Mateo County requires permits for new wells
- **Water table depth:** Varies; shallow in flatlands near bay (~20-50 ft), deeper in western hills
- **Practical consideration:** Given the reliable municipal supply, wells are uncommon in urbanized San Mateo
- **Cost:** $15,000-50,000 depending on depth and geology
- Not recommended as primary strategy given excellent municipal supply

### Wildfire Risk

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|-----------|
| CAL FIRE FHSZ (flatlands) | Non-VHFHSZ / Moderate at most |
| CAL FIRE FHSZ (western hills) | Moderate to High in hillside areas |

| Hills Risk | MODERATE for homes near 280/western hills |

**Assessment: LOW** for the neighborhoods within budget ($1.4M-$2M). The fire risk areas are in the expensive hillside homes above the buyer's budget. Flatland and lower-elevation neighborhoods (Hillsdale, Baywood, Beresford, Hayward Park, San Mateo Park) have minimal wildfire exposure.

## Section 3e: Immigration & Legal

### Sanctuary Status

**California is a sanctuary STATE under the California Values Act (SB 54, 2017).**

Key protections:
- Local law enforcement CANNOT inquire about immigration status
- Police cannot detain individuals solely for immigration purposes without a judicial warrant
- No transfer of individuals to ICE without judicial warrant
- Resources cannot be used for immigration enforcement
- Schools must protect student confidentiality regarding immigration status
- Applies to K-12, community colleges, and CSU system

**San Mateo County** operates under these state protections. The county has historically been progressive on immigration:
- 50.1% Democratic, 15.5% Republican voter registration (as of 2019)
- County Board of Supervisors has repeatedly affirmed support for immigrant communities
- SMC Connect (county service portal) has dedicated "Immigrant Services" category

### Political Climate

- **Very progressive/liberal** - San Mateo County consistently votes Democratic by wide margins
- Strong immigrant community representation (36.2% foreign-born population)
- Multiple immigrant advocacy organizations operate in the county
- The area has a long history of welcoming diverse immigrant communities (Filipino, Chinese, Hispanic, South Asian)
- Low anti-immigrant sentiment compared to state/national levels

### Local Immigrant Support Organizations

- **Legal Aid Society of San Mateo County** - Free/low-cost immigration legal services
- **Peninsula Family Service** - Comprehensive family support including immigrant services
- **SMC Connect** - County immigrant services portal
- **International Institute of the Bay Area** - Immigration legal help, citizenship classes
- **El Concilio of San Mateo County** - Services for Hispanic/Latino community
- Multiple churches and community organizations offer immigrant support

### Diversity of Local Government

- San Mateo has a diverse city council reflecting its population
- Police force serves a 67% minority-majority city
- County government has representation from multiple ethnic communities
- 25.74% Hispanic/Latino, 26.30% Asian population creates genuinely diverse governance

## Executive Summary

San Mateo represents a **solid, safe, family-friendly choice** that checks most boxes but with notable trade-offs. The city offers exceptional safety, reliable water infrastructure (SFPUC/Hetch Hetchy), strong schools (9/10 district rating), and genuine ethnic diversity (67% non-white). The $2M budget can secure a 3-4BR home meeting the 1,700+ sqft requirement in desirable neighborhoods like Baywood, Hillsdale, or San Mateo Park with usable backyards. The commute to DTSJ Adobe office is manageable (~30 min via 101). However, the Persian and Brazilian communities are present but not concentrated here, the school vaccination situation requires a homeschool strategy, and the nearly flat appreciation signals plateauing values.
