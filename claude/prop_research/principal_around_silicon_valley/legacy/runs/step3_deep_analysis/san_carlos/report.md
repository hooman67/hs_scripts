### What $1.5M-$2.0M Actually Buys Here

**CRITICAL FINDING:** The median home price in San Carlos is $2.7M as of mid-2026, making the buyer's $2M budget significantly below median. At $2M, you are shopping the bottom ~30% of the market.

**Typical $1.5M-$2.0M property in San Carlos:**
- 2-3 bedroom, 1-2 bathroom
- 1,200-1,600 sqft living space (below the 1,700 sqft minimum requirement)
- 4,000-6,000 sqft lot
- Older construction (1940s-1960s era) needing updates
- East side / lower-demand locations (near El Camino Real or freeway)
- Likely a "fixer" or cosmetically dated home

**Key challenge:** Finding a 3BR/1,700+ sqft home with a backyard in San Carlos for under $2M is extremely difficult in the current market. Only ~39 homes are listed under $2M total, and many of those are smaller condos/townhomes or 2BR units. The 1,700 sqft minimum pushes the realistic budget to $2.3M-$2.8M for San Carlos.

### Vaccination Policy Analysis (CRITICAL)

**California Law (SB 277 + SB 276):**
- Personal belief exemptions ELIMINATED since 2016
- Only MEDICAL exemptions allowed for public/private schools with classroom instruction
- Medical exemptions require physician approval and are reviewed by CDPH
- SB 276 (2019) added state oversight of medical exemptions to prevent abuse

**IMPORTANT EXCEPTION -- Independent Study Programs:**
Under California law, students in independent study programs who are NOT receiving classroom-based instruction are NOT subject to mandatory vaccination requirements. This is the primary legal pathway for unvaccinated students.

## Section 3d: Survivability & Environment

### Water Supply

**Water Providers:**
1. **California Water Service (Cal Water) -- Mid-Peninsula District**
2. **Mid-Peninsula Water District (MPWD)**

**Primary Water Source:** San Francisco Regional Water System (SFRWS) -- Hetch Hetchy Reservoir water delivered via Bay Division Pipelines 1 and 2 through 3 turnouts.

**Supply Outlook: ADEQUATE**
- Hetch Hetchy is one of the most reliable water systems in California
- 2024 state assessment: 97% of urban suppliers projected no shortages
- Over 45,650 water quality tests conducted in 2024
- SFPUC system serves 2.7M Bay Area residents with excellent reliability
- Recent concern: water rate increases under discussion (Oct 2024 council meeting)

**Drought Resilience:** GOOD -- Hetch Hetchy's Sierra Nevada snowmelt supply is among the best-positioned in California for long-term climate change scenarios, though periodic drought restrictions apply.

### Well Drilling Feasibility

**Regulations:**
- Permit required from San Mateo County Environmental Health Division
- Need: drilling permit, certification, and permit to operate
- Setback requirements from property lines apply
- Must comply with DWR Bulletin 74 standards

**Feasibility:** MODERATE -- Wells can be permitted but San Carlos is primarily served by municipal water. The area has some groundwater access but depth varies. Nine groundwater basins exist in San Mateo County. Practical for irrigation/backup rather than primary supply.

### Wildfire Risk

**CAL FIRE FHSZ Designation: MODERATE to HIGH (varies by location)**

| Area | Risk Level |
|------|-----------|
| Flatlands / East side | Low to Moderate |
| Highlands / Western hills | High to Very High |
| Beverly Terrace / Alder Manor | Very High |

**Key Data:**
- ~27% of all homes in San Carlos are in very high-risk fire zones
- The Highlands neighborhood (western hills) carries the highest risk
- Flatland areas near downtown and El Camino are lower risk
- City maintains active wildfire preparedness programs
- Interactive fire hazard map available at cityofsancarlos.org/firehazardmap

**Recommendation:** If wildfire risk is a concern, focus on properties in the flatlands (east of Alameda de las Pulgas) rather than the Highlands. Price is generally lower in flatland areas anyway, which aligns with budget constraints.

### Flight Path Proximity

- Located 2 miles NE of San Carlos center
- General aviation airport (no commercial flights)
- ~206 daily operations (75,243/year in FY2020)
- Single runway: 2,621 x 75 feet
- Aircraft limited to 12,500 lbs gross weight (small planes only)

**Noise Impact:**

- Primary impact on Redwood Shores and Foster City (east side approach/departure)
- San Carlos residential areas west of the airport are less impacted
- Avoid properties in direct approach/departure path (NE/SW of runway)

## Executive Summary

San Carlos is a charming, well-run Peninsula city with excellent schools (district rated 10/10), strong walkable downtown, very low crime, and a 4-minute drive to a hospital. The Caltrain commute to Downtown San Jose is ~27 minutes. However, **the $2M budget is critically inadequate for the buyer's space requirements in this market.** The median home price of $2.7M means finding a 3BR/1,700+ sqft home with backyard for under $2M is extremely challenging -- likely requiring a major fixer in a less desirable location (east side near airport/freeway). The sanctuary status is strong, Brazilian dining access is good, and independent study charter options exist for unvaccinated children. The western Highlands carry significant wildfire risk.

## Overall Recommendation: PASS

**Confidence: HIGH**

### Rationale
The primary disqualifier is the budget-to-market mismatch. At $2.7M median and $1,390/sqft, finding a 3BR/1,700+ sqft home with backyard for $2M is effectively impossible without a severe compromise (teardown-level fixer, undesirable location near freeway or airport, or non-standard property). The few homes that do list under $2M in San Carlos are typically 2BR/under 1,500 sqft or condos/townhomes without yards.

If the budget were $2.5M+, this would be a STRONG BUY given the schools, commute, walkability, and community character.
