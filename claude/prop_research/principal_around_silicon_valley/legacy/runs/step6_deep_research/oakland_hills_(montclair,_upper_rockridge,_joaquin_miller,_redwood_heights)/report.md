## 6b: Visit Planning

### Best Time of Year to Visit

**PRIMARY: Late September - Early October**
- Weather: Warm (70-80F), dry, clear days - see the area at its best
- Schools: In session (observe drop-off/pick-up, meet parents)
- Community: Fall events beginning (Halloween prep shows neighborhood spirit)
- Fire season: Still technically fire season - witness any smoke haze reality
- Vegetation: See the dry hillside conditions (important for fire risk assessment)

**SECONDARY: Late January - February**
- Weather: Rainy season peak - test drainage, see how steep driveways handle rain
- See if the hills feel claustrophobic in grey weather
- Observe: Mudslide-prone areas, water runoff patterns, moss/damp conditions
- Schools: In session
- Commute: See worst-case rain traffic on 880/580

### Insurance Broker (CRITICAL - Fire + Earthquake)

**This is the #1 financial risk in Oakland Hills and the most important expert to engage EARLY.**

**Fire Insurance Reality (2025-2026):**
- Many major carriers (State Farm, Allstate, Farmers) have stopped writing new policies in Oakland Hills VHFHSZ
- Remaining options: smaller carriers, surplus lines (Lloyd's), or California FAIR Plan
- California FAIR Plan = insurer of last resort; basic fire coverage only (no liability, no theft, no water damage)
- FAIR Plan requires supplemental "Difference in Conditions" (DIC) policy for full coverage
- Annual premiums for Oakland Hills: $5,000-$15,000+ depending on home value, proximity to vegetation, construction type
- **Wildfire Hardening Discounts** available (as of November 2025) for homes meeting defensible space and hardened construction standards

**4. California FAIR Plan (insurer of last resort):**
- Website: cfpnet.com
- Process: Must demonstrate "diligent search" (3+ carrier declinations) before applying
- Coverage: Basic fire insurance only; need separate DIC policy for full protection
- How to apply: Through any licensed insurance broker (3-step process)

**Earthquake Insurance:**
- California Earthquake Authority (CEA) offers residential earthquake policies
- Available as add-on through most homeowner's insurance carriers
- Annual premium: Typically $1,500-$3,000 for Oakland Hills home
- Deductible: Usually 10-15% of dwelling coverage (high)
- Consider: Hayward Fault runs through East Bay foothills; Oakland Hills has moderate-high earthquake risk

**CRITICAL ACTION:** Engage an insurance broker BEFORE making offers. Get a written confirmation that fire insurance can be bound for the specific property. Lenders will not fund without confirmed insurance.
