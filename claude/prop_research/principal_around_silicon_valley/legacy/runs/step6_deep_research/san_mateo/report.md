#### Property Type 2: Baywood Mid-Century (Stretch Budget)
- **Typical listing:** 3-4BR/2BA, 1,800-2,100 sqft, 6,000-7,000 sqft lot
- **Price range:** $1.85M-$2.05M
- **Year built:** 1950-1960
- **Condition:** Many fully renovated; some "original condition" fixer-uppers
- **Renovation needs (if fixer):** Full kitchen/bath remodel, possible foundation work - $100K-200K
- **School zone:** Baywood Elementary / Borel Middle / Aragon High
- **Red flags to check:** Foundation settling on 1950s homes, check for galvanized plumbing, verify not in SFO flight path overlay

#### Property Type 3: Beresford Classic (Middle Ground)
- **Typical listing:** 3BR/2BA, 1,700-1,900 sqft, 5,000-6,000 sqft lot
- **Price range:** $1.7M-$1.9M
- **Year built:** 1948-1958
- **Condition:** Mix of updated and original; many have detached garages with yard space
- **Renovation needs:** Variable - $20K (cosmetic) to $150K (full remodel)
- **School zone:** Beresford Elementary / Bowditch Middle / San Mateo High
- **Red flags to check:** Some parcels near Caltrain tracks (noise), older plumbing/electrical
- **Why it works:** Great dog park (Beresford Park Dog Park), slightly lower prices than Baywood, solid schools, tree-lined streets with high owner-occupancy

#### Property Type 4: San Mateo Park Victorian/Craftsman (Premium)
- **Typical listing:** 3-4BR/2BA, 1,800-2,200 sqft, 5,000-6,500 sqft lot
- **Price range:** $1.9M-$2.2M (many above budget)
- **Year built:** 1910-1940
- **Condition:** Architecturally distinctive, many landmark-adjacent
- **Renovation needs:** Often significant for pre-war homes - $100K-$250K for systems (electrical, plumbing, seismic retrofit)
- **School zone:** San Mateo Park Elementary / Bowditch Middle / San Mateo High
- **Red flags to check:** Historic district restrictions on exterior changes, seismic vulnerability of unreinforced masonry, lead paint
- **Why it works:** Most walkable to downtown (Walk Score 96), beautiful character homes, strong community identity. BUT most are above $2M budget.

#### Property Type 5: Hayward Park (Below Budget)
- **Typical listing:** 3BR/2BA, 1,500-1,800 sqft, 4,000-5,500 sqft lot
- **Price range:** $1.5M-$1.7M
- **Year built:** 1945-1960
- **Condition:** Working-class neighborhood, many original-condition homes
- **Renovation needs:** $50K-$150K for full modernization
- **School zone:** George Hall Elementary (top-rated) / Borel Middle / Hillsdale High
- **Red flags to check:** Closer to El Camino Real traffic, smaller lots, some homes don't meet 1,700 sqft threshold
- **Why it works:** Lowest entry point, George Hall Elementary is highly rated, good value if you find a 1,700+ sqft home. Dog-friendly area with parks nearby.

| **Roof specialist** | For homes 20+ years without re-roofing | $200-$350 |
| **Termite/pest (Section 1)** | Required by most lenders; often seller-paid | $100-$200 |

**Pro tips for San Mateo inspections:**
- 77% of housing stock is pre-1970 - ALWAYS get foundation, electrical, and plumbing add-ons
- Check for cast iron drain pipes (common in 1950s homes, often corroded)
- Test for lead paint on pre-1978 homes (especially with young children)
- Verify water heater strapping (seismic requirement)
- Check for aluminum wiring (fire hazard in some 1960s homes)
- Ask about sewer lateral condition - replacement costs $8K-$25K

### Insurance Brokers (Fire + Earthquake)

**Homeowner's Insurance (Fire/Liability):**
- San Mateo flatland neighborhoods are LOW fire risk (not in VHFHSZ)
- Standard homeowner's premium estimate: $2,500-$4,000/year for a $2M home
- California FAIR Plan: backup if private insurers won't cover (unlikely needed for flatland SM)
- Recommended insurers: State Farm, Allstate, CSAA (AAA), Farmers
- Get quotes from at least 3 carriers

**Earthquake Insurance (California Earthquake Authority - CEA):**
- San Mateo is near the San Andreas Fault (Crystal Springs Reservoir follows the fault)
- CEA earthquake insurance estimated cost: $3,000-$6,000/year for a $2M home
- Deductible options: 5%-25% of dwelling coverage (homes >$1M limited to 15-25% deductible)
- For a $2M home with 15% deductible: first $300K of damage is out-of-pocket
- Coverage includes: dwelling, personal property, loss of use, building code upgrade
- Premium factors: age (1950s homes cost more), foundation type, proximity to fault, soil type

**Recommended insurance brokers:**
- Goosehead Insurance (local Peninsula agents)
- Opes Advisors / Compass Insurance (bundled with real estate)
- Independent broker who can shop multiple carriers

**Budget for total insurance:**
- Homeowner's: ~$3,000-$4,000/year
- Earthquake: ~$4,000-$6,000/year
- **Total insurance: ~$7,000-$10,000/year**

### Well Driller (Not Recommended)

Given San Mateo's excellent SFPUC municipal water supply (Hetch Hetchy + Crystal Springs Reservoir literally in the city), well drilling is NOT recommended:
- Municipal supply is among the most reliable in California
- Urban San Mateo wells require county permits with uncertain approval
- Cost: $15,000-$50,000 for questionable benefit
- Better investment: rainwater collection for garden irrigation ($2,000-$5,000)
