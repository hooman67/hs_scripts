1. **Drive the commute:** Start at target neighborhood at 7:45 AM. Take I-880 South to I-101 South to downtown San Jose. Time it. Note traffic merge points (880/101 interchange in Milpitas). Target: arrive DTSJ by 8:30 AM.
2. **Alternative route:** Try Warm Springs BART to Diridon Station or Milpitas BART + shuttle/VTA. Note total door-to-desk time.

5. **Check construction/noise:** Any active development projects? Highway noise from 880?

### Weekday Evening Checklist (5:30-8:00 PM)

1. **Walk Little Kabul (Fremont Blvd) at dinner time:** Observe crowd, families, atmosphere. Eat at De Afghanan or Salang Pass. Ask servers about the community.
2. **Walk target residential streets at dusk:** Are people outside? Kids playing? Garage doors open? Sense of safety?
3. **Check street lighting and visibility:** Walk as a brown-skinned person at 8 PM. Do you feel safe? Observed? Welcomed?
4. **Dog walk test:** Walk the dog on target streets and at Central Park. Note off-leash compliance, other dog walkers, interactions.
5. **Noise assessment:** Listen for 880 highway noise, airport sounds, industrial hum (Tesla factory in Warm Springs).

### Weekend Checklist (Saturday 9 AM - 4 PM)

1. **Fremont Central Park (10 AM):** Walk around Lake Elizabeth. Observe families — demographics, activities, social interactions. Is this where your family would spend weekends?
2. **Little Kabul Saturday morning:** Afghan bakeries are busiest. Get bolani and chai. Browse Maiwand Market. Does it feel like a cultural home?
3. **Niles District (afternoon):** Walk the historic main street. Note community feel, small businesses, antique shops.

## 6e: Red Flags & Risk Mitigation

### Property Crime
- Fremont's property crime is 32% above national average (primarily vehicle theft/break-ins)
- **Mitigation:** Choose streets away from 880 corridor; look for cul-de-sacs; garage parking; security cameras; neighborhood watch areas

### 880 Highway Noise
- Some Centerville/Irvington streets are within 0.5 miles of I-880
- **Mitigation:** Visit at rush hour; check noise from backyard of any target property; prioritize streets 3+ blocks from freeway

### 1960s-70s Home Issues
- Original plumbing (galvanized → copper conversion: $8K-$15K)
- Original sewer laterals (clay pipe → PVC: $5K-$12K)
- Possible asbestos in popcorn ceilings/insulation
- Small original bathrooms/kitchens
- **Mitigation:** Budget $50K-$100K for updates; get thorough inspection; negotiate credits

### Lot Contamination (rare but check)
- Some areas of Fremont have former agricultural use (orchard pesticide residue)
- Warm Springs area has some industrial history
- **Mitigation:** Check EPA Superfund map; ask inspector to test soil if property has fruit trees/garden planned
