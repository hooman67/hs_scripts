  • Strong community, short commute (12-18 min)
  • Median $1.65M ($350K surplus for upgrades)
  • Highest overall confidence
  • NO MAJOR RED FLAGS

RUNNERS-UP BY PRIORITY:

  If Schools Priority:
    - Los Altos Hills (8/10 schools, best safety; requires full $2M)
    - Almaden Valley (8/10 schools, new construction, $250K surplus)

  If Walkability/Commute Priority:
    - Downtown SJ/SoFA (88 WalkScore, 1-5 min commute)
    - Trade-off: Highest crime, lowest schools

  If Budget/Space Priority:
    - Evergreen ($1.25M, 0.80 ac lots, +$750K surplus)
    - Cambrian Park ($1.35M, 0.65 ac lots, +$650K surplus)
    - Trade-off: Weakest schools (6/10), limited data

CRITICAL CONSTRAINTS (All Neighborhoods):
  • Water Crisis: HIGH risk through 2033 (SWP allocation 40-45% vs 75-100%)
    - Expected: 2-3% annual water rate increases
    - Relief deferred to 2034+ (Sites Reservoir)
  
  • Wildfire Risk (Geographic):
    - HIGH: Los Altos Hills, Almaden Valley, Evergreen (foothills)
    - LOW: Downtown SJ/SoFA, Rose Garden, Willow Glen (valley)
