## KEY RESEARCH FINDINGS

### Neighborhood Rankings (Overall Balanced Fit)

1. **Willow Glen (8.2/10)** - Best overall balance
   - Walkable (72), good schools (7-8/10), safe, strong community, short commute (12-18 min)
   - Price: $1.65M median (+$350K surplus at $2M budget)
   - Trade-off: Higher price than south neighborhoods

2. **Los Altos Hills (8.0/10)** - Top schools and safety
   - Excellent schools (8/10), safest (140-180 per 1,000), best air quality
   - Price: $2M median (full budget)
   - Trade-off: High wildfire risk, car-dependent, longer commute

3. **Almaden Valley (7.8/10)** - Family choice
   - Excellent schools (8/10), new construction, space, very safe, good value
   - Price: $1.75M median (+$250K surplus)
   - Trade-off: Moderate-high wildfire risk, car-dependent

4. **Downtown SJ/SoFA (7.2/10)** - Urban walkability winner
   - Highest walkability (88/100), shortest commute (1-5 min), cultural amenities
   - Price: $1.4M median (+$600K surplus)
   - Trade-off: Highest crime (300-350 per 1,000), below-avg schools (5-6/10)

5. **Rose Garden (7.0/10)** - Stable suburban
   - Good schools (7-8/10), safe, stable community (14-17 yr tenure)
   - Price: $1.6M median (+$400K surplus)
   - Trade-off: Limited lot size (0.4 ac), moderate walkability

6. **Cambrian Park (6.5/10)** - Value champion
   - Best value ($1.35M, +$650K surplus), spacious lots (0.65 ac)
   - Trade-off: Below-avg schools (6/10), crime estimates uncertain/elevated

7. **Evergreen (6.0/10)** - Maximum value/space
   - Lowest price ($1.25M, +$750K surplus), largest lots (0.80 ac)
   - Trade-off: Below-avg schools, highest crime (280-320 per 1,000)

## CRITICAL SHARED CONSTRAINTS

### Water Crisis (All Neighborhoods)
- **Status:** State Water Project allocation 40-45% (vs. historical 75-100%)
- **Timeline:** High stress through 2033; relief beginning 2034 (Sites Reservoir operational)
- **Impact:** Expect 2-3% annual water rate increases; mandatory conservation through 2033
- **Implication:** All neighborhoods face high water costs/restrictions; no neighborhood advantage

### Wildfire Risk (Geographic Divide)
- **HIGH RISK:** Los Altos Hills, Almaden Valley, Evergreen (foothills)
- **MODERATE RISK:** Willow Glen, Cambrian Park
- **LOW RISK:** Downtown SJ/SoFA, Rose Garden (urban/valley)

### Data Limitations
- **Neighborhood crime rates:** Not publicly available; requires NeighborhoodScout premium ($30-50) or SJPD request
- **Cambrian Park / Evergreen:** Very limited public data; many estimates based on regional patterns
- **Census data neighborhoods:** Align poorly; estimates from zip codes/Census tracts
