Check each new cylinder for an intact valve, readable manufacture/certification markings, and no dents, heavy rust, gouges, or other damage. Keep the purchase receipt and record the cylinder tare weight stamped after `TW`.

## Transport and storage

- Keep cylinders **upright, valve closed, and secured against movement** during transport.
- Drive them directly home; do not leave cylinders inside a parked vehicle.
- Store them **outdoors** in the prepared shaded, ventilated location, upright and protected from impact, heat, children, and ignition sources.
- Do not store them inside the house, garage, or a sealed deck box.
- Follow the cylinder label, Coleman manuals, and current local fire rules.

## Connect and test

- Use the Coleman hose only if its package/manual confirms compatibility with the received Triton stove.
- Connect and operate the Triton **outdoors only**.
- Follow the stove and hose instructions for the leak test before lighting.
- Run the planned two- or three-day meal test. Weigh a cylinder before and after; propane remaining equals total cylinder weight minus the stamped tare weight (`TW`).
