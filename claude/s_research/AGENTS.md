# Preparedness Research Agent Instructions

This directory is a long-running household survival-preparedness research project.

Before substantive work:

1. Read `/home/colligo/.codex/RESEARCH_RULES.md` completely.
2. Read `/home/colligo/hs_scripts/claude/s_research/README.md` and every living project-state file listed there.
3. Read the relevant topic's canonical `SUMMARY.md`.
4. Inspect current primary sources before updating recommendations.

Project-specific rules:

- Optimize for the safety of two adults, two young children, and one medium dog in San Jose, California.
- Cover earthquakes, water contamination, power outages, wildfire, supply disruption, and complete utility loss.
- Separate survival-minimum, moderate-conservation, and higher-comfort designs.
- Prefer primary government, medical, standards, and peer-reviewed sources. Retailer and manufacturer material may establish product specifications or current prices, but not independent safety claims.
- Treat infant, medication, supplement, structural-load, chemical-treatment, and food-contact questions as health- or safety-critical.
- Show all calculations, units, assumptions, capacity margins, maintenance intervals, and failure modes.
- Account for San Jose heat, direct sun, seismic anchoring, garage contaminants, pests, access during damage, and the inability to use an unroofed backyard without protective infrastructure.
- Distinguish potable water from non-potable sanitation water. Never imply that untreated or questionably stored water is safe to drink.
- Keep proposals marked as proposals until Hooman confirms them. Never infer a purchase or completed action.
- Do not purchase, contact vendors, publish, or make external changes without explicit authorization.
- Keep credentials and sensitive family information out of tracked files.
