# Long-Term Plans

## Roadmap

1. Water: 90-day quantities, storage architecture, treatment, access, maintenance, and hazard operations.
2. Food: nutrition, infant/toddler needs, dog food, cooking dependencies, rotation, and storage.
3. Medical: first aid, prescription continuity, thyroid medication, pediatric needs, supplements, and evidence-based limits.
4. Tools and utilities: purification, sanitation, cooking, lighting, power, fire safety, communications, and repair.
5. Physical storage: room layout, shelving, floor/seismic safety, inventory, labeling, inspection, and rotation.
6. Integrated hazard playbooks: earthquake, contamination, outage, wildfire, supply disruption, and evacuation versus shelter-in-place.
7. Exercises and maintenance: scheduled inspections, consumption drills, replenishment, and annual plan review.

## Maintenance horizon

Each implemented subsystem will receive explicit inspection, replacement, and drill intervals based on verified manufacturer and authority guidance. "Store and forget" is an optimization goal, not a presumption that stored supplies require no inspection.

## Explicit non-goals

- Individualized diagnosis or medical treatment.
- Unverified improvised chemical dosing.
- Assuming shelter-in-place supplies replace evacuation planning.
- Purchasing products or contacting vendors without explicit authorization.
