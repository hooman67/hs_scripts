# Household Emergency Navigation Plan

**Status:** Proposed; no purchase or completed setup is inferred.  
**Household:** Two adults, two young children, and one medium dog.  
**Home area:** Evergreen neighborhood, San Jose, California.  
**Relocation destination:** Family and shelter in Vancouver, British Columbia.  
**Last researched:** 2026-10-03.

## Purpose

Build a redundant navigation system that works during earthquakes, wildfire evacuations, power or cellular outages, road closures, and extended relocation from San Jose to Vancouver.

The system has four layers:

1. Two independent adults trained to navigate.
2. Magnetic compasses and lightweight local paper maps in the go-bags.
3. Detailed road atlases stored in the vehicle.
4. Offline maps downloaded independently to both iPhones.

A compass is primarily for orientation, map work, and travel on foot when signs or electronics fail. It does not replace detailed road maps or current closure information for the approximately 950-mile vehicle journey to Vancouver.

## Assumptions and boundaries

- Vancouver is a genuine relocation destination with family and shelter available.
- The family expects to make the long-distance journey in an authorized vehicle, not on foot.
- Both adults will learn and practice navigation independently.
- The exact departure route will be selected at the time of travel using current hazard, weather, border, and road information.
- Private home and destination addresses are not stored in this tracked file. They belong on the family's protected route card.
- Vehicle acquisition or recovery must be lawful. This plan does not cover bypassing an ignition or taking an abandoned vehicle without authorization.
- Paper maps and downloaded maps may be outdated. Neither establishes that a road, bridge, border crossing, or evacuation corridor is open.

## Compass selection

### Proposed primary compass

Purchase **two identical Suunto MC-2G Global compasses**, one for each adult.

Select these exact listing options:

- **Configuration:** USGS UTM scales
- **Style:** Global
- **Quantity:** Two

Do **not** select “USGS UTM scales with Military Directions.” Military mil-based direction markings are unnecessary for this household and add avoidable complexity.

The USGS UTM version is preferred because it matches the USGS 1:24,000 maps proposed for local navigation. Bearings themselves are universal, and Canadian topographic maps use UTM grids, so Canadian maps remain usable even when a particular baseplate ruler does not exactly match their printed scale.

Key features:

- Adjustable magnetic declination
- Sighting mirror for accurate bearings and emergency signaling
- Global needle
- Transparent baseplate for map work
- Clinometer
- Luminous markings
- No batteries

Observed price on 2026-10-01: approximately **$95 each**. Price and availability must be rechecked before purchase.

Product and manual:

- https://www.amazon.com/SUUNTO-MC-2-Compass-Scales-Metric/dp/B000FEUCRW
- https://ns.suunto.com/Manuals/MC-2/Userguides/SUUNTO_MIRRORCOMPASSES_UG_EN.pdf

### Alternative

The Brunton TruArc 15 is a strong alternative at an observed price of approximately $84.95. It offers a global needle, tool-free declination adjustment, mirror, clinometer, and 1-degree resolution:

- https://www.brunton.com/products/truarc-15

Using two identical compasses is preferable to mixing models because both adults can learn and troubleshoot the same controls.

### Magnetic-interference precautions

- Take bearings away from the vehicle's steel body.
- Move phones, Apple Watches, speakers, tools, magnetic closures, and MagSafe accessories away from the compass.
- Store the compass away from strong magnets.
- A global needle does not correct declination. Determine current declination from NOAA and set it for the operating area:
  https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/geomag/calculators/magcalc.shtml#declination
- Recheck declination when traveling a substantial distance and before a major trip.

### Go-bag maps

Each adult's bag should contain:

- A local street and terrain map covering the home area and multiple exits from Evergreen
- A regional map covering the greater Bay Area and access to Central Valley routes
- A compact route overview from San Jose to Vancouver
- A protected route card containing the destination address, coordinates, family contacts, and planned meeting points
- Pencil, waterproof notebook, and waterproof document pouch

Do not put the full four-atlas set in a go-bag.

### Free USGS maps

Create maps through USGS topoBuilder:

- One **1:24,000 7.5-minute topo** centered on the home area for walking and terrain detail
- One **1:100,000 topo** for greater San Jose and regional escape planning
- Optionally one **1:250,000 topo** for broader regional orientation

USGS topoBuilder can center maps anywhere and provides free geospatial PDF or TIFF downloads. The 1:24,000 product covers roughly 50–70 square miles, while the 1:100,000 product covers roughly 1,500–2,200 square miles:

- https://www.usgs.gov/programs/national-geospatial-program/topobuilder

Topographic maps complement current road maps; they do not replace them.

## Offline iPhone system

Configure both phones independently. Downloads do not automatically transfer between household members.

### Primary dedicated offline application: Organic Maps

Install Organic Maps and download:

- California
- Oregon
- Washington
- British Columbia
- Nevada and Idaho for major inland detours, if storage permits

Organic Maps provides offline search and offline driving, walking, and cycling navigation using OpenStreetMap data. OpenStreetMap coverage is community-maintained and can contain omissions or errors:

- https://organicmaps.app/

### Secondary application: Apple Maps

Download overlapping offline regions covering the entire corridor on both iPhones.

Settings:

- Enable **Automatic Updates**.
- Disable **Optimize Storage** so the phone does not automatically remove unused preparedness maps.
- Use **Only Use Offline Maps** temporarily during tests.

Apple documentation:

- https://support.apple.com/en-us/105084

### Optional third application: Google Maps

Google Maps may be retained as another backup, but it should not be the primary preparedness application. In offline mode:

- The entire driving route must be within downloaded areas.
- Walking, cycling, and transit directions are unavailable.
- Traffic information and alternative routes are unavailable.
- Maps require periodic refreshes and can expire.

Documentation:

- https://support.google.com/maps/answer/6291838?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DiOS

### Offline validation

After downloading maps on each phone:

1. Enable airplane mode.
2. Disable Wi-Fi.
3. Confirm the home area, multiple local exits, the entire northbound corridor, border area, and Vancouver destination are visible.
4. Test searches and route creation.
5. Confirm the destination address and coordinates are stored outside the mapping applications as well.

GPS reception can work without cellular service, but map display, search, and routing depend on data downloaded in advance.

## Training plan

Both adults should complete the following curriculum.

### Session 1: Foundations

- Identify every compass component.
- Understand true north, magnetic north, grid north, and declination.
- Set and verify declination.
- Orient a paper map to the terrain.

### Session 2: Bearings

- Take a map-to-field bearing.
- Take a field-to-map bearing.
- Follow a bearing between visible landmarks.
- Estimate distance by map scale and pacing.

### Session 3: Terrain navigation

- Recognize contours, ridges, valleys, saddles, waterways, and prominent landmarks.
- Practice handrails, catching features, attack points, and deliberate backstops.
- Practice staying found rather than recovering only after becoming lost.

### Session 4: Failure-mode exercise

- Navigate a familiar park with phones in airplane mode.
- Locate position using two or three recognizable landmarks.
- Compare the paper-and-compass result with GPS only after completing the exercise.
- Repeat with the second adult leading.

Repeat a short practical drill quarterly. Reading without field practice is insufficient.

## Route-decision information

Before departure, check authoritative sources for closures and hazards. These services may be unavailable during an emergency, which is why paper maps are retained.

- California Caltrans QuickMap: https://quickmap.dot.ca.gov/
- Oregon TripCheck: https://tripcheck.com/
- Washington WSDOT real-time map: https://wsdot.com/Travel/Real-time/Map/
- British Columbia DriveBC: https://www.drivebc.ca/

Also check current wildfire evacuation orders, weather, mountain-pass requirements, border conditions, and official emergency instructions. Do not enter a closed or evacuated area merely because a paper or offline map shows a road.

## Maintenance schedule

### Quarterly

- Test every offline mapping application in airplane mode on both phones.
- Confirm all required regions remain downloaded.
- Check the private destination/route card and family contact information.
- Complete a short paper-map and compass exercise.

### Every six months

- Inspect compass capsules for bubbles, damage, sluggish needles, or abnormal behavior.
- Inspect paper maps and waterproof pouches for moisture or damage.
- Confirm compasses have not been stored beside magnets or MagSafe equipment.

### Annually and before long travel

- Review newer road-atlas editions.
- Refresh all offline maps.
- Recalculate declination using NOAA.
- Review primary and alternative corridors without treating either as guaranteed.

### Immediately before an evacuation or relocation

- Check official evacuation orders and road, bridge, pass, wildfire, weather, fuel, and border status.
- Select the route based on current conditions.
- Keep the national overview atlas accessible to the navigator rather than buried under cargo.
