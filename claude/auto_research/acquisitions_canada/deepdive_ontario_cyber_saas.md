## 2. The 6-employees-on-$207K red flag — the central problem

This is the thesis-killer and deserves the hardest scrutiny.

**Do the math (ASSUMED cost model — must be verified against the P&L):**
- CA$207K revenue ÷ 6 employees = **~CA$34.5K revenue per head.** For a healthy software
  business you want **$150K–$250K+ revenue per FTE.** This company is at ~1/5 of that. It is
  either drastically overstaffed, or the "6 employees" are largely **part-time / fractional /
  contract** (common in tiny Ontario startups — a fractional CISO-type founder, a part-time
  content creator, a part-time dev, a part-time sales/CS person).
- **If they are 6 full-time salaried Canadian employees:** fully-loaded cost in Ontario is
  **~CA$70K–$110K each** (salary + CPP/EI/EHT + benefits) = **CA$420K–$660K payroll alone**
  against CA$207K revenue. That is a **deeply negative** operation (burning $200K–$450K/yr) —
  the "profitable" claim would be false or defined creatively (e.g. gross margin, or founder
  working unpaid). **SDE would be sharply NEGATIVE. This is an acqui-hire / distressed-asset
  scenario, not a cash-flowing SaaS. AVOID at any price above the value of the code + contracts.**
- **If they are part-time/contract (the charitable reading):** say 6 people at 0.3–0.5 FTE and
  below-market rates = maybe CA$120K–$180K blended cost. Then SDE could be a **thin positive
  (CA$20K–$60K)** — a lifestyle micro-SaaS where the founder is not really paying themselves.
  Even so, at CA$207K revenue and a fully-loaded team, **honest owner-benefit is small** and
  the business cannot absorb Hooman replacing the founder's labor with a hired GM.

**Honest SDE estimate:** **UNVERIFIED and almost certainly thin-to-negative.**
- **Realistic bracket (ASSUMED): SDE somewhere between −CA$150K and +CA$50K.** The listing's
  refusal to publish any cash-flow number, combined with 6 heads on $207K, is exactly the
  pattern of a business where **SDE does not support a headline price** and the broker is
  hoping a strategic buyer pays for the client list / product, not the earnings.
- **Deal-killer test:** if the broker cannot produce **3 years of tax returns / accountant-
  prepared financials showing positive, owner-adjusted SDE**, treat this as a **software/
  client-list asset buy worth low six figures at most**, not a profitable SaaS.

**Verdict on the flag: CONFIRMED red flag.** 6 employees on CA$207K is prima facie a sub-scale,
likely unprofitable operation. The burden is entirely on the seller to disprove it.

defensible moat is **distribution intimacy in a narrow niche** — e.g. an MSP/MSSP channel, a

**KnowBe4 scale for context (ASSUMED):** ~65,000 customers, ~US$450M ARR, taken private by

## 4. The compliance angle — does a mandate drive stickiness?

**Partly yes — this is the one genuine positive, but it does NOT accrue uniquely to THIS vendor.**
- SAT is **mandated or strongly implied** by **PCI-DSS**, **HIPAA**, **GDPR** (all VERIFIED via
  Wikipedia this session), and by **SOC 2**, **ISO 27001**, cyber-insurance underwriting, and in
  Canada **PIPEDA** + provincial rules (ASSUMED). This creates **non-discretionary demand** —
  companies must buy *some* SAT product to pass audits and get insurance. Good: the category is
  compliance-pulled, not nice-to-have, so churn from "we cut the budget" is lower than typical.
- **BUT the mandate is satisfied by ANY compliant vendor**, including the free/cheap ones. A
  compliance checkbox is the *lowest-differentiation* reason to buy — it explicitly commoditizes.
  The mandate protects the *category's* stickiness, not this company's *pricing power*. A buyer
  facing renewal can switch to Wizer-free or Microsoft-bundled and still pass the audit.
- **Net:** the compliance driver keeps the 35 clients from churning to *nothing*, but gives zero
  protection against churning to a *cheaper competitor*. Stickiness = moderate; pricing power = low.

## 5. Founder-fit — Hooman's edge, and build-vs-buy

- **Software fit: HIGH.** AI/ML + data-eng + cloud/infra is exactly the toolkit to modernize the
  product, cut infra cost, and add a defensible **AI angle** (adaptive/personalized phishing
  simulations, LLM-generated localized training, human-risk scoring) — the direction Hoxhunt is
  taking. He can be the whole engineering org.
- **Sales/channel fit: LOW — and sales is the entire game here.** This category is won on
  distribution (MSP channels, outbound, compliance partnerships), not code. Hooman has **no sales
  track record** (flagged across the whole Canadian search). The one asset that *can't* be rebuilt
  by an engineer — the 35 client relationships and whatever channel exists — is precisely what he'd
  be paying for, and precisely what erodes if the founder leaves and no seller note aligns them.
- **Remote-operability: HIGH.** Fully remote, Ontario-domiciled — runnable from the US on TN,
  no immigration wrinkle (consistent with the category verdict). The 6-employee payroll is the only
  drag on "solo-remote" — he'd likely need to cut heads day one, which is messy in Ontario
  (termination notice/severance under the ESA). **ASSUMED.**
- **Build vs buy:** For a technical founder, **building a gamified SAT product is cheap and fast**
  (weeks, with modern LLM tooling) — the code is NOT the barrier to entry; **customers are.** So the
  only rational reason to *buy* is to acquire the **35 recurring accounts + any channel** at a price
  below what customer-acquisition would cost to replicate. If SDE is negative and the price still
  implies paying a revenue multiple for 35 shaky, low-margin, churnable accounts in a commoditized
  category, **building (or buying a better-positioned target) dominates buying this one.**

## 6. Financing & structure

- **No seller financing → all-cash.** VERIFIED from listing. This is a meaningful negative: it
  removes the seller's skin-in-the-game (no earnout/note to keep them selling and transitioning the
  35 relationships), and it forces Hooman to write a full cheque up front for an asset whose earnings
  are unproven.
- **BDC/CSBFP:** As a Canadian citizen he is eligible, but lenders **cash-flow-underwrite** — with
  thin/negative SDE, **BDC will not lend against this**, and CSBFP intangibles coverage still needs
  demonstrable serviceable cash flow. **Practically this is an all-cash, unlevered buy.** ASSUMED.
- **Only sane structure:** a **low all-cash price justified by asset value** (code + 35 contracts +
  brand), NOT a revenue/SDE multiple, **PLUS a mandatory seller transition/consulting agreement and
  a clawback/holdback tied to 6–12-month client retention.** If the seller refuses both a low price
  and any retention-linked holdback, walk.

## 7. Verdict & deal-killers

**Verdict: PASS / low-priority — pursue ONLY as a cheap asset-buy after the price and P&L are
disclosed. This does not belong ahead of the #1 BC Staffing SaaS.**

The compliance-pulled demand and remote + software founder-fit are real positives, but they are
outweighed by: (a) a **commoditized category with a free/Microsoft-bundled floor** where a 35-client
micro-vendor has **no visible moat and dangerous concentration**; (b) a **6-head-on-$207K cost load
that implies thin-to-negative honest SDE**; (c) **no seller financing / no alignment**; and (d) the
fact that for a technical buyer the **software is trivially buildable — you'd only be buying 35
churnable accounts.**

**Hard deal-killers (any one = walk):**
1. Broker won't disclose price + 3 yrs accountant-prepared financials before serious diligence.
2. The "6 employees" are full-time salaried → SDE is materially negative; it's a distressed acqui-hire.
3. Customer concentration: top 3–5 clients > ~50% of revenue.
4. Price implies a revenue/SDE multiple (any positive multiple on an unprofitable, commoditized,
   35-client base) rather than a modest asset value.
5. Seller refuses BOTH a retention-linked holdback AND a transition/consulting period.

**Single most important next step:** contact **Greg Kells** to (1) get the gated **price**, (2) get
**owner-adjusted SDE + 3 yrs financials**, (3) clarify **FT-vs-contract on the 6 heads**, and
(4) get **churn + top-client concentration.** Until those four land, the honest position is: *likely
sub-scale roadkill; only interesting as a sub-six-figure asset grab, and even then build-vs-buy
probably favors building.*
