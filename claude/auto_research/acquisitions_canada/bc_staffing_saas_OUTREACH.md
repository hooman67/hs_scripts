## THE INFORMATION REQUEST (attach to Note B)

### Tier 1 — the deal-deciders (need these to make an offer)

1. **Financials (3 full years + TTM):** accountant-prepared P&L and balance sheet; monthly
   revenue and MRR export. Reconcile the listing's **"$34K MRR" (headline) vs "$38K MRR"
   (detail)** discrepancy. Confirm the **currency** of both revenue and costs.
2. **Revenue by customer (anonymized ok):** so I can see **customer concentration** — % of
   revenue from the top 1 / top 5 / top 10 accounts.
3. **Logo-level retention / cohort data (36 months):** active accounts by month, logos gained
   and lost per month, and **MRR churn vs. logo churn** — the basis for the stated <2% churn.
4. **New-customer sourcing (last 24 months):** for each new logo, how it was acquired —
   owner outbound / conferences / inbound / referral / partner / paid. (This is the single
   dataset that most affects my offer.)
5. **SDE bridge:** reconcile the US$249K SDE to net income line by line — which add-backs,
   and specifically **which functions the owner personally performs** (sales? development?
   support?) that a new owner would need to hire for.

### Tier 2 — pricing & structure

6. **Team:** the 3 full-time + 2 part-time contractors — role, tenure, cost, location,
   employee-vs-contractor status, and who (if anyone) is the **lead developer** on the codebase.
   Will they stay through/after transition?
7. **Product/tech:** language/framework, hosting stack and monthly infra cost, age of the
   codebase, test coverage, and **IP ownership** (are all contractor contributions assigned?).
8. **"Payroll" scope:** does the product **run payroll / calculate remittances** for placed
   workers, or **export hours** to a third-party payroll provider? (Determines both moat depth
   and any compliance liability I'd inherit.)
9. **Customer contracts:** month-to-month vs. annual; auto-renewal, price-lock, and
   cancellation terms.
10. **Growth trend:** an explanation of the **32% → 10% YoY** deceleration, and the current
    qualified sales pipeline.

### Tier 3 — closing readiness

11. Reason for sale and the seller's desired timeline.
12. Preferred deal structure and the **maximum seller note / earn-out** the seller will carry.
13. Any customer-consent or change-of-control clauses that trigger on a sale.
14. Corporate structure (asset vs. share sale) and whether it's a clean BC corporation.
