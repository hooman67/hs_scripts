**Created**: 2026-07-04. **Owner**: Hooman Shariati. **Status**: separate side-research; does
NOT alter the main funnel or the US-based `acquisitions_500k/` search. This is the **Canadian**
parallel: businesses domiciled in **Ontario / British Columbia** (with a light scan of other
provinces if standout deals appear).

## Why a separate Canadian search
The US search surfaced a financing wall: as a **Canadian citizen on a TN visa** Hooman can't use
SBA today, and TN bars actively operating his own US business (would need E-2 or a green card).
A **Canadian-domiciled** business sidesteps BOTH problems:
- **Financing:** unlocks Canadian leverage — **BDC** (Business Development Bank of Canada), the
  **CSBFP** (Canada Small Business Financing Program), and the big banks' small-biz acquisition
  lending. Roughly analogous to what SBA does for US buyers.
- **Immigration:** no E-2/visa issue — he can legally own and operate in Canada.
