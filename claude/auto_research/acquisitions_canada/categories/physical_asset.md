## Research method & data-quality caveat

Canadian physical-asset businesses are sold two very different ways, and it matters:

1. **Business-only (leasehold) sales** — you buy the equipment + lease + goodwill, landlord keeps
   the building. This is where the sub-$500K deals live (most laundromats, self-serve car-wash
   leaseholds, vending routes).
2. **Business + real estate** — you buy the operating business AND the land/building. These
   dominate car-wash and self-storage listings and run $1.5M–$12M — mostly OUT of a $500K
   all-cash budget, reachable only with heavy BDC/CMHC/commercial-mortgage leverage.

**Sources that actually returned live data (many broker sites 403/JS-block WebFetch):**
- `ontariobizforsale.ca` — Realtor.ca-fed ON commercial listings (laundromat, car-wash categories). WORKS.
- `businessforsaleinbc.ca` — BC laundromat inventory (Macdonald Realty feed). WORKS.
- `soldwell.com` — ON aggregator (403 on detail pages, but counts/titles via search).
- `findbusinessesforsale.com`, `dealstream.com`, `realtor.ca` — via DuckDuckGo HTML snippets.
- BizBuySell Canada (`bizbuysell.com/canada-businesses-for-sale/`) — reachable but thin on these
  categories; ~599 total Canada listings, laundromat/storage/carwash barely represented.
- **Blocked (403/JS/captcha):** businessesforsale.com/canada, sunbeltcanada.com detail pages,
  kijiji.ca (defaults to Ontario CALIFORNIA — beware), loopnet.ca, vrbb.ca (conn refused).

**Universal caveat:** almost NO Canadian physical-asset listing publishes SDE/net income publicly.
Revenue is sometimes given ("$6,500/week coin"), net almost never — "financials available on NDA."
Every SDE below is my ESTIMATE (marked) built from disclosed revenue minus disclosed rent and
typical utility/labor ratios. Treat all cash-flow figures as ASSUMED until verified in diligence.

### Self-storage — Ontario/BC

No sub-$500K operating facility found. Canadian self-storage trades on cap rate (5.5–8.0% on
T12 NOI per CTA Acquisitions / The Storage Brief 2026). A facility with enough NOI to matter is
$2M–$10M+. A $500K all-cash buyer can only touch: (a) a tiny rural facility with heavy BDC/CMHC
leverage, or (b) a portable-container / land-lease micro-storage startup (build, not buy). Effectively
OUT for this budget as an acquisition. Sources: invsty.ca/ontario-storage-report, thestoragebrief.com.

## CATEGORY ECONOMICS (Canadian, honest)

**Laundromats (leasehold).** The realistic sub-$500K play. Typical metro-Ontario/BC coin store:
$120K–$350K/yr revenue, rent $35K–$85K/yr, utilities (gas+water+hydro) 20–35% of revenue, near-zero
labor if unattended. **Realistic SDE $40K–$120K**; sale multiples **~2.5–3.5x SDE** for leasehold,
higher if unattended/card-enabled with long lease. Cash yield on a $300K–$450K all-cash buy: roughly
**15–30% pre-tax IF the disclosed revenue is real** — but that "if" is the whole game, because net is
never published and utility inflation (esp. natural gas / water) is the killer variable. Owner-hours:
5–15/wk if unattended and card-based; 30+/wk if attended with wash-&-fold. This is the closest thing
in the category to a genuinely semi-absentee, financeable, AI/data-founder-tolerant asset.

**Car washes.** Two sub-types. (a) *Self-serve/IBA leasehold* ($345K–$450K here) — low labor, but you
inherit aging pumps/bays and often a short lease; SDE opaque. (b) *Express tunnel w/ real estate*
($1.5M–$12M) — the attractive modern model (membership recurring revenue, 40–55% EBITDA margins) but
far over a $500K all-cash budget; only reachable with a big commercial mortgage + BDC top-up.
Multiples: leasehold ~2.5–3x SDE; real-estate tunnels sold on 6–8% cap. Weather/seasonality and
water-recycling/environmental compliance (esp. ON soil/effluent — note the Hamilton listing's $534K
remediation capex) are real risks.

**Self-storage.** Best long-term asset economics in the category (80%+ gross margins, minimal labor,
inflation-linked rents, sticky tenants) but **not accessible at $500K all-cash** as a going concern in
ON/BC. Cap rates 5.5–8%. This is a "raise money / relocate / build" play, not a solo sub-$500K buy.

**Vending.** Lowest barrier, lowest reward. Sub-$50K routes throw off $10K–$30K SDE; you'd need to
roll up 10+ to matter, each requiring weekly on-the-ground restocking. Poor fit for a remote US-based
owner and low founder-fit for an AI/data engineer.

## FINANCING (BDC / CSBFP / Canadian banks)

- **CSBFP (Canada Small Business Financing Program):** lends against **equipment and leasehold
  improvements** — a natural fit for laundromat washers/dryers and car-wash equipment. Max ~$500K for
  equipment/leaseholds (up to $1M total incl. real property). Lender (a chartered bank/credit union)
  fronts it; program guarantees 85%. **Typically expects ~10–25% buyer equity.** CSBFP does NOT finance
  goodwill — a problem for leasehold laundromat deals where much of the price IS goodwill.
- **BDC:** does acquisition/business-transfer financing and will lend on real-asset businesses; often
  **pairs a real-estate/term loan with a working-capital loan.** For a $300K–$450K laundromat, BDC or a
  bank could plausibly do **20–35% down**, so Hooman's $500K cash could buy 1 solid store outright OR
  put down on a ~$1M car-wash-with-real-estate (leverage into a better asset). BDC likes hard assets and
  real estate; it is cooler on pure goodwill/leasehold.
- **Reality for this budget:** all-cash gets you ONE decent laundromat (best asset-utilization: buy
  business-only, keep cash buffer for utility spikes & equipment). Using leverage, the smarter move is a
  **car-wash-or-small-storage WITH real estate** where the land collateral unlocks a mortgage + BDC —
  turning $500K into a $1.2M–$1.6M asset with amortizing debt and an appreciating property underneath.
- Provincial specifics: **HST 13% ON / 12% (GST+PST) BC**; asset sales can use the **s.167 GST/HST
  election** to avoid tax on the transfer — get an accountant. Business licensing is municipal; car
  washes need **environmental/effluent permits** (MECP in ON) and self-serve sites may carry soil-
  contamination liability (see Hamilton remediation). Laundromats need gas/utility and fire-code sign-off.

## REMOTE-OPERABILITY (from the US, until/unless he relocates)

- **Unattended, card/app-enabled laundromat (e.g. L3 6210 Finch, or any store you convert to
  pay-by-phone):** the ONLY genuinely remote-runnable option. Machines report online; you hire a local
  contractor for cleaning + a technician on call. Hooman's data/cloud skills are a modest edge (dynamic
  pricing, remote monitoring, marketing). Still needs a trusted local pair of hands. SEMI-ABSENTEE at best.
- **Attended laundromat / wash-&-fold:** needs on-the-ground management → ON-SITE-REQUIRED for now.
- **Self-serve car wash (leasehold):** low labor but breakdowns, cash/coin handling, weather, and
  compliance demand a local operator/manager → SEMI-ABSENTEE with a reliable manager, else ON-SITE.
- **Self-storage:** the MOST remote-friendly in theory (kiosk/online rentals, remote gate) — but not
  buyable at this budget.
- **Vending:** requires weekly local restocking → ON-SITE-REQUIRED. Worst remote fit.

Bottom line: from the US, only an **unattended card-based laundromat** is realistically runnable
semi-absentee, and even that wants a local contractor. Everything else pushes toward relocation or a
hired GM eating the thin margins.

## HONEST VERDICT

This category is **asset-backed and financeable but a weak founder-fit and a weak remote-fit** for
Hooman right now. The businesses that fit $500K all-cash (leasehold laundromats, self-serve car-wash
leaseholds, vending) are **low-SDE, opacity-ridden, utility-inflation-exposed, and mostly require local
presence** — precisely the constraints that hurt a US-resident AI/data engineer most. The genuinely
attractive assets (express car washes, self-storage) throw off great cash flow with low labor but sit at
$1.5M–$10M and need real leverage + a relocation or a hired operator.

**If he pursues this category anyway, the single best shape is:**
1. **Best all-cash pick:** a metro Toronto/Vancouver **unattended, card/app-enabled laundromat with a
   long lease** (L3-type profile, but priced ≤$450K) — convert-to-unattended is where his tech edge and
   the only real remote-operability live. Diligence hinges on VERIFYING coin/app revenue (pull the
   payment-processor and utility records) and lease term — never trust "very high net income."
2. **Best leverage pick:** a **car-wash-or-small-storage WITH real estate** (~$1.2–1.6M) using $400–500K
   down + BDC/commercial mortgage — trades remote-operability for a better, appreciating, amortizing
   asset. Requires a hired GM or eventual relocation.

Net: worth ONE serious laundromat swing if he wants a tangible, financeable, inflation-hedged cash
asset — but this category ranks BELOW software/online acquisitions for a remote US-based AI founder.
His diligence-alpha helps (most sellers hide net income), but it can't overcome the physical-presence
and thin-margin realities. **Proceed only for the unattended-laundromat or leveraged-real-estate case;
otherwise deprioritize vs. the digital categories.**
