## Category economics (real Canadian numbers)

- **SDE multiples observed:** owner-operator home-service shops trade **~1.5×–3.3× SDE**; the
  larger, more institutionalized contractors (Sudbury, Barrie, Okanagan) push **3–4× SDE / EBITDA.**
  Sub-$500K deals in ON/BC cluster at **~2–3× SDE**.
- **Cash yield:** at ~2.5–3× SDE, unlevered SDE yield is **~33–40%** of purchase price before debt
  service and before paying a GM to replace the owner. Replacing the owner (mandatory here — see
  remote constraint) with a $90–120K GM typically consumes 40–60% of SDE on the smaller deals.
- **Owner-hours:** HVAC/plumbing/cleaning/fencing are **high-touch, full-time owner-operator**
  businesses (dispatch, quoting, crew/sub management, seasonal swings). The low-voltage integrator
  and painting/detailing franchises are the most "manage-a-team" (least tools-in-hand) shapes.
- **Margins:** SDE/revenue ranged from **~11%** (fence, sub-heavy) to **~35%** (HVAC GTA) to
  **~29%** (cleaning). Recurring maintenance/route revenue (HVAC maintenance co., cleaning,
  exterior detailing) is the durability driver — prioritize it.
- **HST:** all these are HST-registrants (13% ON / 5% GST + 7% PST in BC); factor into working
  capital and diligence of reported revenue.

## Financing notes (BDC / CSBFP / bank — the Canadian advantage)

- **BDC (Business Development Bank of Canada):** the go-to for acquisition financing; more flexible
  than the chartered banks and can finance a **higher % of the purchase** than conventional lenders.
  CFIB/broker sources put **conventional lender equity requirements at 20–35% down**; BDC can go
  lower and often stacks with a seller VTB. No zero-down acquisition loans exist in Canada — buyer
  equity is always required.
- **CSBFP (Canada Small Business Financing Program):** **max $1.15M/borrower**; eligible businesses
  = **≤$10M gross annual revenue** (all these qualify). BUT — **critical for this category** — CSBFP
  finances **hard assets only**: equipment + leasehold up to **$500K combined**, intangibles up to
  **$150K**, working capital LOC up to **$150K**. **CSBFP does NOT finance goodwill or share
  purchases.** Since most of a home-service purchase price is **goodwill/blue-sky** (customer base,
  routes, brand), CSBFP typically covers only the trucks/equipment portion — **BDC + a chartered-bank
  term loan + seller VTB is the realistic stack**, with CSBFP layered onto the equipment.
- **Seller VTBs are common and present in these listings** (low-voltage 20–30% down; London cleaning
  VTB) — the practical path to fit ~$500K cash: **20–30% down + BDC/bank + 10–25% VTB.**
- **Deal structure caution:** an **asset sale** (most of these) sidesteps hidden liabilities and
  lets you step up asset values, but forfeits CSBFP goodwill financing (goodwill never CSBFP-eligible
  regardless). A share sale may carry tax advantages for the seller but heavier diligence for Hooman.

## Remote-operability from the US (the category's core problem)

**This is the weakest category for Hooman's current situation.** Home-service/trades businesses are
**physically rooted**: local crews, trucks, dispatch, same-day/emergency response, in-market sales,
and provincially **licensed trades**. None can be run "from a laptop in the US":

- **ON-SITE-REQUIRED:** HVAC (GTA, Southern ON, Okanagan), plumbing, cleaning (London/Halton),
  fencing, restoration. These need licensed techs and daily local oversight; absentee ownership from
  across the border risks quality/retention erosion.
- **SEMI-ABSENTEE (best available):** the **Richmond low-voltage integrator** (retained Office Mgr +
  Technical Lead), the **painting franchise** (crew-lead model), and **exterior detailing franchise**
  — but even these realistically need frequent on-the-ground presence and a trusted #2, plus for the
  trades a licensed key-man Hooman does NOT hold.
- **Trade-licensing by province:** ON gas work → **TSSA** G1/G2 gas technician + ODP; electrical →
  **ECRA/ESA** licensed contractor + Certificate of Qualification (309A). BC → **Technical Safety BC**
  permits; plumbing/gas/electrical are compulsory-certified trades. Hooman (EE degree, not a
  Red-Seal/CofQ tradesperson) **cannot hold these licenses himself** — he must **retain the licensed
  key-man**, which is the single biggest operational risk in every trades deal here.

Bottom line: **feasible only if Hooman relocates to Canada or installs a strong, licensed local
operator/GM before closing.** Until the green card / relocation, this category is a poor fit vs. the
software/parasitic-SaaS options in his main funnel.
