# Curated Sources

High-quality sources discovered during research cycles.

---

## Tier 1 (High quality, accessible, near-commercial focus)
- **IEEE Spectrum** (spectrum.ieee.org) — Best for validated engineering breakthroughs near deployment
- **New Atlas - Energy** (newatlas.com/energy/) — Good coverage of energy tech at various TRL levels
- **SynBioBeta** (synbiobeta.com/news) — Synthetic biology industry news, commercial focus

## Tier 2 (Good content but access limitations)
- **SingularityHub** (singularityhub.com) — Good overview but article URLs unpredictable/404-prone
- **Refractor** (refractor.io) — Medical tech/devices, good detail
- **New Atlas - Materials** (newatlas.com/materials/) — Mix of early and near-commercial

## Tier 3 (Blocked or limited)
- **Ars Technica** — Blocked by site
- **Wired** — Blocked by site
- **Nature** — Requires auth redirect
- **Science Magazine** — 403 Forbidden
- **Crunchbase** — 403 (competitive intelligence)
- **PitchBook** — 403 (competitive intelligence)
- **G2** — 403 (product reviews)
- **Grand View Research** — 403 (market data)

## Market Data Sources (accessible)
- **MarketsAndMarkets** — Partial access (some reports show summaries)
- **Precedence Research** — Good summaries accessible
- **Mordor Intelligence** — Good summaries accessible
- **Fortune Business Insights** — Partial access
- **Statista** — Paywalled specifics but gives CAGR/size headlines

## Company Research
- Individual company websites are the best source for competitive analysis
- Product Hunt search works for consumer-facing products
- Wikipedia works for some larger companies (Quaise was 404 though)
