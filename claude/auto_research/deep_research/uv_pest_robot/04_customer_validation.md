| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Profile** | 50-120 acre organic strawberry operation, Watsonville/Salinas Valley |

## Discovery Questions (for customer interviews)

### Problem Validation

1. "Walk me through your current pest management program for [crop]. What does a typical season look like from first spray to last?"
2. "What's your total annual spend on pest control — chemicals, labor, crop consultants, application equipment? Per acre?"
3. "Which specific pests cause you the most yield loss or quality damage? What percentage of crop do you lose to pests in a typical year?"
4. "Have you considered organic certification? What's stopping you? What would organic premium be worth to your operation?"
5. "What's your experience with pesticide resistance? Are you having to increase rates or switch chemistries more often?"

### Solution Validation

6. "If a robot could patrol your fields at night and reduce pest pressure by 70%+ without chemicals, what would that be worth to you per acre?"
7. "Have you heard of UV-C treatment for pest/disease control? What's your reaction? What concerns come to mind?"
8. "What would you need to see proven before you'd try this on even 5 acres? What evidence would convince you?"
9. "How do you feel about robots operating in your fields at night unsupervised? What safety/liability concerns arise?"
10. "Would you prefer to own the equipment, or pay per-acre-per-season for a managed service?"

### Operational Fit

11. "What's your row spacing? Row length? Any terrain challenges (slopes, rocks, mud)?"
12. "Is there cellular coverage across your fields? Do you have WiFi infrastructure?"
13. "Who would interact with the robots day-to-day? What's their technical comfort level?"
14. "What's your seasonal calendar? When do pest problems typically start and end?"
15. "Are there other robotic or precision-ag technologies you've adopted? What worked/didn't work?"

## Demand Signals (Evidence of Market Pull)

### Strong Positive Signals

| Signal | Evidence | Confidence |
|--------|----------|------------|
| SAGA raised $44M and deployed 150+ robots | Investors + growers validated UV-C ag-robots | HIGH |
| TRIC oversubscribed seed round ($5.5M) | Strong investor demand for insect + UV-C combo | HIGH |
| Carbon Robotics hit $100M+ revenue | Growers will pay for autonomous chemical-free field robots | HIGH |
| Organic market growing 15%/year | Demand for organic enablement is structural, not cyclical | HIGH |
| EU Farm to Fork 50% pesticide reduction mandate | Regulatory forcing function in largest ag market | HIGH |
| SWD resistance to spinosad documented | Growers losing their best organic tool — urgency for alternatives | MEDIUM |

### Cautionary Signals

| Signal | Evidence | Confidence |
|--------|----------|------------|
| SAGA has NOT expanded beyond 2 crops in 5+ years | Market may be harder to penetrate than expected | MEDIUM |
| No UV-C insect kill product at commercial scale exists | Scientific uncertainty remains about insect efficacy | HIGH |
| Seasonal ag-tech companies struggle with cash flow | Naïo Technologies, Naio, others have had financial difficulties | MEDIUM |
| Farmer adoption curves are notoriously slow | Average farmer age 57; technology adoption lags 5-10 years vs. other industries | HIGH |

## Outreach Plan

### Phase 1: Warm Introductions (Weeks 1-4)

| Channel | Approach | Expected Response |
|---------|----------|-------------------|
| UC Davis Cooperative Extension | Contact small farms advisors; ask for introductions to technology-forward organic growers | 2-3 introductions |
| California Strawberry Commission | Attend events; ask about pest management innovation programs | Industry context + contacts |
| Organic Farming Research Foundation | Present concept; ask for grower advisory panel participation | Credibility + contacts |
| LinkedIn (ag-tech groups) | Connect with farm managers who post about technology adoption | 5-10 conversations |

### Phase 2: Direct Outreach (Weeks 4-8)

| Channel | Approach | Volume | Expected Conversion |
|---------|----------|--------|-------------------|
| Cold email to farm managers | Personalized: reference their specific pest challenges, offer free consultation | 50 emails | 5-10 responses (10-20%) |
| Ag trade shows (attend) | Western Growers Tech Summit, CAPCA, Organic Produce Summit | 2-3 events | 10-15 conversations |
| Crop consultant partnerships | Offer revenue share for referrals; consultants are trusted advisors | 10 consultants contacted | 2-3 partnerships |
| Farm visits (cold drop-in) | Drive Watsonville/Salinas corridor; introduce self to farm managers | 20 visits | 5 conversations |

### Phase 3: Pilot Commitment (Weeks 8-16)

| Action | Target | Offer |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Pilot proposal | 5 qualified growers | Free 10-acre, 4-week trial with data sharing agreement |
| Letter of intent | 3 growers | Conditional commitment to paid season if pilot succeeds |
| Advisor recruitment | 2 growers | Advisory board seat + equity for ongoing guidance |

### Key Metrics for Validation

| Metric | Target | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Discovery calls completed | 20+ | Sufficient sample for pattern recognition |
| "Would pay $800+/acre" responses | >50% of calls | Price validation |
