## Weighted Total: 4.30 / 10.00

## Key Strengths

1. **Extremely low capital requirements.** Can be launched for under $40K, making it a low-risk experiment even if it fails.
2. **Low technical risk.** TEGNet works, COMSOL works, the pipeline is well-understood. A competent ML engineer can build this in 5-8 weeks.
3. **Clear value proposition.** 5 days vs. 3-6 months is a compelling speed improvement, easy to articulate in sales conversations.
4. **No hardware risk.** Pure services/software avoids the capital-intensive hardware trap that killed Alphabet Energy.
5. **Defensible pricing.** At $15K-$50K per project against traditional $60K-$180K consulting engagements, the price/value ratio is strong.

## Key Weaknesses

1. **Negligible market size.** The addressable market is likely $5-20M globally. This caps the business at lifestyle-scale ($500K-$2M/year) regardless of execution quality.
2. **Zero competitive moat.** Open-source core technology means any competitor can replicate the service. Speed advantage is temporary.
3. **Poor founder-market fit.** No thermoelectric domain expertise, no industry network, no credibility with buyers who value PhD credentials and published research.
4. **Consulting trap.** Revenue scales linearly with labor. The "5-day turnaround" promise anchors the business to human delivery, not software scale.
5. **Customer acquisition is expensive.** Conservative industrial buyers with 12-18 month sales cycles. High CAC relative to $15-50K deal sizes. No inbound channel.
6. **Fundamental demand question.** The scarcity of competitors may indicate insufficient demand rather than an untapped opportunity. TEG adoption is limited by economics (5-8% efficiency), not design speed.

## Overall Assessment

**VERDICT: NO-GO for venture-scale. CONDITIONAL-GO as lifestyle business.**

This idea scores poorly on the dimensions that matter most for a venture-backed startup (moat, market size, scalability) but scores well on the dimensions that matter for a lifestyle/consulting business (low capital, low technical risk, clear value prop). 

If the goal is a $200K-$1M/year consulting practice with 50%+ margins and location independence, this could work well. If the goal is a venture-scale company with $10M+ ARR potential, this is not the right opportunity.

**The 4.30/10 weighted score places this idea in the BELOW AVERAGE tier** - viable but not compelling relative to alternatives that score higher on moat, market size, and scalability.
