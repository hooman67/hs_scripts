## AI-Powered Thermal Inspection Tool — Customer Validation Plan

### First 5 Target Customers

#### Customer 1: Solo Home Inspector — "Dave the One-Man Shop"

- **Profile**: Independent home inspector, 5-10 years experience, does 3-5 inspections/week, already owns a FLIR ONE or similar budget thermal camera
- **Location**: Suburban metro area (Phoenix, Dallas, Atlanta — hot markets with thermal-relevant issues)
- **Pain**: Spends 45-60 minutes per report after each inspection. Thermal images require manual annotation. Clients question findings without clear documentation.
- **Budget**: $300-$500 for tools (writes off as business expense). $50/month is acceptable if ROI is clear.
- **Where to find**: InterNACHI forums (100K+ members), ASHI chapter meetings, InspectorNation Facebook group (25K members), local real estate referral networks
- **Why first**: Fastest decision maker (no procurement committee), clearest ROI (time = money for per-inspection billing), already thermal-aware

#### Customer 2: Electrical Contractor (Small Commercial) — "Mike's Electric"

- **Profile**: Licensed electrician running 3-5 person crew, handles commercial maintenance contracts (offices, retail, light industrial)
- **Pain**: Insurance companies and property managers require thermal inspection reports for electrical panels. Currently outsources to thermography consultant ($300-$500/visit) or uses expensive FLIR camera with manual reporting.
- **Budget**: $500-$1,000 for equipment that eliminates outsourcing cost. $50/month trivial for commercial operation.
- **Where to find**: NECA (National Electrical Contractors Association) local chapters, electrical supply houses (Graybar, WESCO), trade shows (NFPA, IEEE IAS)
- **Why second**: High recurring need (monthly/quarterly inspections for contracts), clear cost savings vs outsourcing, potential for multiple units per company

#### Customer 3: HVAC Service Technician — "Comfort Pros HVAC"

- **Profile**: HVAC service company (5-15 techs), handles residential and light commercial service/maintenance
- **Pain**: Diagnosing duct leakage, insulation gaps, and equipment faults currently requires expensive thermal cameras. Younger techs lack experience to interpret thermal images correctly.
- **Budget**: $400-$800 per tech for diagnostic tools. Company already spends $2K-$5K on test instruments annually.
- **Where to find**: ACCA (Air Conditioning Contractors of America) events, HVACR trade shows (AHR Expo), Johnstone Supply counters, HVAC-Talk.com forums
- **Why third**: AI interpretation helps inexperienced techs (levels up entire team), seasonal demand creates urgency (pre-heating-season purchases in Sep-Oct)

#### Customer 4: Property Management Maintenance Director — "Regional Property Group"

- **Profile**: Manages 50-200 residential units or 5-20 commercial buildings. Has in-house maintenance team.
- **Pain**: Reactive maintenance is expensive. Wants to catch issues (moisture, electrical hot spots, HVAC inefficiency) before they become emergencies. Current thermal inspection is outsourced quarterly.
- **Budget**: $1,000-$5,000 for predictive maintenance tools. Strong ROI argument (one prevented pipe burst = $10K-$50K saved).
- **Where to find**: BOMA (Building Owners and Managers Association), IREM conferences, property management software communities (AppFolio, Buildium user groups)
- **Why fourth**: Higher deal size (multiple units, enterprise pricing), longer sales cycle but higher LTV, validates enterprise/fleet use case

#### Customer 5: Insurance Adjuster / Loss Prevention Specialist — "National Carrier Field Team"

- **Profile**: Insurance company field inspector who assesses properties for underwriting or claims
- **Pain**: Needs standardized, defensible documentation. Current thermal inspection is manual and inconsistent across adjusters. Reports must meet specific formatting/evidence standards.
- **Budget**: Company-purchased equipment ($500-$2,000 per adjuster). SaaS pricing trivial at enterprise scale.
- **Where to find**: PLRB (Property Loss Research Bureau) conferences, insurance carrier innovation labs, Claims Journal readership
- **Why fifth**: Largest potential deal size (100+ units per carrier), but longest sales cycle (12-18 months enterprise procurement). Validates "enterprise fleet" endgame.

### Discovery Questions (For Customer Interviews)

**Problem Validation:**
1. Walk me through your last thermal inspection. What tools did you use? How long did the report take?
2. How many inspections do you do per week/month that involve or could involve thermal imaging?
3. What is the most frustrating part of thermal inspection today?
4. Have you ever missed a fault that thermal could have caught? What happened?
5. How confident are you in interpreting thermal images? (Scale 1-10) What would increase your confidence?

**Solution Validation:**
6. If a device could automatically identify the type of fault in a thermal image and tell you the severity, how useful would that be? (Scale 1-10)
7. If that same device generated a complete inspection report with one tap — annotated images, findings, code references, recommendations — how much time would that save you per inspection?
8. Would you trust an AI-generated diagnosis enough to include it in a client report? What would make you trust it more?
9. What is the minimum accuracy you would need from an AI assistant? (e.g., "right 8 out of 10 times")

**Pricing/Purchase Validation:**
10. What did you pay for your current thermal camera? How did you decide on that model?
11. If this AI-enhanced device cost $299-$399 plus $49/month for AI features and unlimited reports, would that be a "yes," "maybe," or "no" for you? Why?
12. Who makes the purchase decision for tools like this in your organization? Is it you, your boss, or a procurement process?
13. Where do you typically buy tools and equipment? (Online direct, Amazon, supply house, distributor, trade show?)

**Competition/Alternatives:**
14. What software do you currently use for report generation? How much do you pay for it?
15. Have you looked at any AI-powered inspection tools? What did you find?
16. If FLIR added AI features to their cameras tomorrow, would you buy that instead? At what price?

### Outreach Plan (First 90 Days)

**Week 1-2: Research & Preparation**
- Join InterNACHI ($49), ASHI associate membership
- Join HVAC-Talk, ElectricianTalk, InspectorNation forums
- Create landing page with product concept + email signup
- Prepare 3-minute demo video (screen recording of mockup showing AI detection + report gen)

**Week 3-4: Online Community Engagement**
- Post in InterNACHI forum: "How long does your thermal report take?" (problem validation)
- Share thermal inspection tips on forums (establish credibility)
- DM 20 active forum members for 15-minute discovery calls
- Target: 10 completed discovery interviews

**Week 5-6: Industry Events & Partnerships**
- Attend local NECA chapter meeting or electrical supply house "lunch and learn"
- Contact 3 home inspection training schools (AHIT, ICA, InterNACHI education) about data partnership
- Reach out to 5 independent inspection companies for beta interest
- Target: 5 more discovery interviews, 2 data partnership conversations

**Week 7-8: Prototype Demonstration**
- Build minimum demo: phone + FLIR ONE + custom app showing AI overlay (even if accuracy is low)
- Show to 10 discovery interview participants for reaction
- Collect feedback on UX, pricing, must-have features
- Target: Go/no-go decision on product direction

**Week 9-12: Beta Recruitment & Pre-orders**
- Launch pre-order page (refundable $50 deposit)
- Post demo video in forums + targeted Facebook/Instagram ads to trade professionals
- Apply to InspectionWorld conference (InterNACHI) for exhibitor booth
- Target: 50+ pre-orders, 20+ beta applicants, data partnership signed

**Week 13-16 (if GO signal): Data Collection Intensive**
- Deploy simple annotation tool to partner inspectors (label existing thermal images)
- Pay $5-10 per quality-labeled image
- Target: 5,000 labeled images across 3 fault categories
- Begin model training in parallel

### Key Validation Milestones

| Milestone | Deadline | Success Criteria | Action if Failed |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|------------------|
| 10 discovery interviews completed | Week 4 | 7/10 confirm report time >30 min as pain point | Pivot problem hypothesis |
| Working demo shown to prospects | Week 8 | 8/10 say "I would buy this" | Re-examine solution approach |
| 50 pre-orders collected | Week 12 | $50 refundable deposits | If <25, product-market fit is not proven; pause |
| Data partnership signed | Week 10 | 1+ inspection company agrees to label images | If zero, training data acquisition is at risk |
| Model accuracy >85% (electrical faults) | Week 20 | Precision >85% on held-out test set | If <75% after 3 iterations, AI thesis is wrong; pivot to assisted/manual |
