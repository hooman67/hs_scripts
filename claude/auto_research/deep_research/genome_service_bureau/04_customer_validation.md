# 04_customer_validation.md — Genome-Scale Engineering Service Bureau

## First 5 Target Customers

### 1. Generation Bio (gene therapy, non-viral delivery)
- **Why**: Developing non-viral gene therapy with large DNA payloads (>50kb). Their approach specifically requires large synthetic constructs that exceed current commercial synthesis limits.
- **Size**: ~200 employees, Series D ($370M raised total)
- **Contact path**: Scientific conferences (ASGCT), LinkedIn outreach to VP of Research
- **Estimated deal size**: $100-200K (2-3 constructs at 50-100kb each)
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM-HIGH (their technology platform inherently needs large DNA)

### 2. Sarepta Therapeutics (gene therapy for muscular dystrophy)
- **Why**: Developing micro-dystrophin gene therapy. Full-length dystrophin cDNA is ~14kb but regulatory cassettes + safety elements push constructs to 50-100kb. Multiple variant testing needed.
- **Size**: ~3,500 employees, public (SRPT), $1.7B+ revenue
- **Contact path**: Through CSO network, scientific advisory board connections
- **Estimated deal size**: $200-500K (multiple construct variants)
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM (large company, may have internal capability or preference for it)

### 3. Colossal Biosciences (de-extinction / conservation genomics)
- **Why**: Explicitly working on synthesizing large portions of extinct genomes (mammoth, thylacine). Publicly stated need for genome-scale synthesis capability. Would be a marquee reference customer.
- **Size**: $10.2B valuation (Jan 2025), well-funded
- **Contact path**: Direct outreach to Chief Science Officer; high public visibility makes them approachable
- **Estimated deal size**: $500K-2M (multiple large genome segments, 200kb-1Mb)
- **Confidence**: HIGH (publicly stated need, well-funded, few alternatives)

### 4. DARPA / BARDA-funded academic lab (government biodefense)
- **Why**: Government biodefense programs need synthetic genomes for vaccine development, threat characterization, and pandemic preparedness. Government labs value US-based, security-cleared vendors.
- **Size**: Individual grants $1-10M, ongoing programs
- **Contact path**: SBIR/STTR proposals, program manager relationships, biosecurity conferences
- **Estimated deal size**: $100-500K per project (but steady pipeline of projects)
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM (long sales cycle, but strong need and budget)

### 5. Synthetic biology startup (metabolic engineering focus)
- **Why**: Companies engineering microbes for biomanufacturing need to swap entire metabolic pathways (50-200kb pathway clusters). Internal assembly takes 6-12 months, killing their speed-to-market.
- **Specific targets**: Lanzatech, Zymergen successor companies, Checkerspot, Genomatica
- **Contact path**: SynBioBeta conference, VC intros (shared investors)
- **Estimated deal size**: $50-150K (pathway cluster constructs)
- **Confidence**: MEDIUM (strong need but budget-constrained if early-stage)

## Discovery Questions (for customer conversations)

### Problem Validation
1. "How do you currently produce DNA constructs above 30kb? Walk me through your process."
2. "What's the longest construct you've successfully assembled in-house? How long did it take?"
3. "How many person-months did your last large-construct project consume?"
4. "What's the failure rate on your large assemblies? How do you handle failed attempts?"
5. "Have you ever abandoned a construct design because assembly was too difficult?"

### Willingness to Pay
6. "If we could deliver a verified 100kb construct in 6 weeks, what would that be worth to your program timeline?"
7. "What's your current budget for DNA synthesis services (Twist/IDT/GenScript)?"
8. "At what price per kb would genome-scale synthesis be an obvious yes for your team?"
9. "Would you prefer fixed-price (risk on us) or time-and-materials (risk on you) for complex constructs?"

### Workflow & Requirements
10. "What delivery format do you need? (YAC, BAC, linear, in specific host strain?)"
11. "What sequence verification standard would you require? (Sanger, long-read, coverage depth?)"
12. "How important is turnaround time vs. cost for your current projects?"
13. "Do you have biosecurity/compliance requirements for your DNA synthesis vendors?"
14. "How many genome-scale projects do you anticipate in the next 12-24 months?"

### Competitive Understanding
15. "Have you evaluated any vendors for genome-scale synthesis? What was the experience?"
16. "What would prevent you from using a new service bureau for this work?"

## Demand Signals (Observable Evidence of Need)

### Strong Positive Signals
- **GP-write consortium existence**: Hundreds of scientists signed on to write genomes, indicating demand (Confidence: HIGH)
- **Sc2.0 project completion (2024)**: Demonstrated feasibility of full synthetic genomes, will inspire commercial applications (Confidence: HIGH)
- **Gene therapy market growth**: $7.4B in 2023, projected $30B+ by 2030. Larger payloads = larger constructs needed (Confidence: HIGH)
- **Colossal Biosciences $10.2B valuation**: Investors believe large-scale genome engineering is commercially viable (Confidence: HIGH)
- **DARPA Safe Genes program**: $65M invested in genome engineering safety, signals government demand (Confidence: MEDIUM)

### Cautionary Signals
- **Ginkgo's revenue struggles**: Despite $17.5B peak valuation, synthetic biology services struggle to generate revenue at scale (Confidence: HIGH)
- **No existing service bureau succeeded yet**: If demand were overwhelming, someone would already be doing this (Confidence: MEDIUM)
- **Falling fragment synthesis costs**: As gene-scale synthesis gets cheaper, more labs may DIY genome assembly (Confidence: MEDIUM)
- **Long sales cycles in pharma**: 6-12 months from first meeting to signed contract is typical (Confidence: HIGH)

### Net Assessment
Demand exists but is concentrated among a small number of high-value customers (probably 50-200 organizations globally that need genome-scale synthesis in any given year). This is a high-value niche, not a mass market.

**Confidence**: MEDIUM-HIGH

## Outreach Plan

### Month 1-2: Warm Introductions
- Identify CSO candidates who have networks in target customer segments
- Attend ASGCT (gene therapy) and SynBioBeta (synthetic biology) conferences
- Cold outreach via LinkedIn to 20 VP-level scientific contacts at target companies
- **Goal**: 10 discovery conversations

### Month 2-3: Design Partner Recruitment
- Offer 3 "design partner" slots: 50% discount on first project in exchange for:
  - Active collaboration on project design
  - Case study permission
  - Testimonial/reference upon delivery
- **Goal**: 3 signed design partner agreements

### Month 3-4: Government Pipeline
- Submit SBIR/STTR Phase I proposals to DoD/DARPA/BARDA
- Attend biosecurity conferences (Gryphon Scientific community events)
- Meet with program managers at relevant agencies
- **Goal**: 1 SBIR award or government contract LOI

### Month 4-6: Pipeline Building
- Publish capability white paper and circulate to VC portfolio companies
- Present at 2+ conferences on genome synthesis capabilities
- Generate 10+ qualified leads with budget and timeline
- **Goal**: $500K+ in qualified pipeline

### Metrics to Track
- Discovery conversations completed (target: 20 in first 3 months)
- LOIs/contracts signed (target: 3 in first 6 months)
- Average deal size (target: >$75K)
- Sales cycle length (benchmark: understand if it's 2 months or 12 months)
- Repeat inquiry rate (do customers come back with more projects?)
