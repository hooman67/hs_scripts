# Customer Validation Plan

## First 5 Target Customers
1. **Sterling Crane** (Houston, 300 cranes) - Gulf Coast, high OSHA activity
2. **TNT Crane & Rigging** (Houston, 200 cranes) - Public parent = compliance pressure
3. **Crane Rental Corporation** (Southeast, 100-150 cranes) - Pure rental = max liability exposure
4. **Bay Crane** (NYC, 200 cranes) - Strictest regulations in US
5. **Deep South Crane** (Gulf Coast, 60-80 cranes) - Ideal design partner, fast decisions

## Key Discovery Questions
- How many cranes lack working LMI? What do you do when LMI fails?
- Recent OSHA citations? Insurance premium changes?
- Would you pay $3.5-5K per crane vs. $20K+ OEM? How many would you retrofit?
- Who decides? What approval process/timeline?

## Outreach Plan (4 phases, $20-31K budget)
1. Warm introductions via industry advisors (weeks 1-4)
2. Trade shows: SC&RA Conference, regional crane events (months 2-4)
3. Cold outreach: 50 messages targeting fleet managers/safety directors (weeks 4-8)
4. Pilot recruitment: free 90-day installation on 3-5 cranes (months 3-6)

## Decision Gates
- Gate 1 (week 4): 4+ companies confirm top-5 pain point
- Gate 2 (week 8): 3+ would pay $3.5-5K, pilot commitments
- Gate 3 (week 12): 2+ signed pilot agreements with deposit
- Gate 4 (week 16): System works reliably in field
