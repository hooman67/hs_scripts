# Product Definition: Sonic Dry-Vacuum Wand

## Problem Statement

**Who has this problem**: People who need to clean their body without water — overlanders, van-lifers, hikers on multi-day trips, festival-goers, hospital patients with mobility constraints, military in field conditions.

**How painful**: Personal hygiene in water-scarce situations is either: (1) baby wipes (leave residue, not truly clean, generate waste), (2) dry shampoo (hair only, chemicals), (3) skip cleaning entirely (common on multi-day backpacking trips). No solution exists that provides a genuinely clean feeling without water.

**What they do today**: Tolerate being dirty, use baby wipes, or carry water specifically for sponge baths (heavy, limited supply in remote areas).

---

## Product Scope: v1 (MVP)

### What it does (3 features max):
1. **Ultrasonic agitation** (25-28 kHz) loosens dirt, oils, and dead skin cells via acoustic cavitation at the skin surface
2. **Micro-vacuum** suction captures loosened debris through a filter pod
3. **Light misting** with volatile carrier fluid improves acoustic coupling and provides clean sensation

### What it does NOT do:
- NOT a shower replacement for daily use (positioning is for water-scarce situations)
- NOT medical-grade sterilization (no antibacterial claims)
- NOT a beauty/skincare device (no cosmetic treatment claims)
- NOT designed for hair (body/face only)

---

## User Workflow (Day 1)

1. Press power button
2. Hold wand against skin (silicone shroud creates light seal)
3. Device cycles automatically: mist → sonic agitation → vacuum → rest (3-second cycle)
4. Move across body areas for 2-5 minutes total
5. Remove filter pod, discard debris, replace for next use
6. Recharge via USB-C
