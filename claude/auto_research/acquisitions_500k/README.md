  be available to him. Agents must FLAG financing feasibility per deal (all-cash within ~$500K vs
  needs leverage he may not qualify for). Canadian small-biz acquisition financing (BDC, banks,

  assuming any US-loan path.
- Skills: AI/ML, data engineering, cloud/infra, EE background, mech-eng friends, strong analytical/
  diligence ability. Edge = diligence alpha + (for data/automation-heavy ops) real value-creation.
