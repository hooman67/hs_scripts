## 0. TL;DR verdict

This is a **strong vein** — arguably the strongest "boring cash flow" category money can buy at
$500K. Multiples are low (2.1x–2.8x SDE), SDE is real owner cash, and route/recurring models are
durable and AI-safe. **BUT two hard caveats for Hooman:**

1. **Trades (HVAC/plumbing/electrical) usually require a licensed master/qualifying party.** Hooman
   (EE degree, no trade license) would need to retain the seller's license, hire a qualifier, or
   pick a state/province where his EE background can bridge — this is the single biggest gating
   risk. **Route businesses (pool, lawn, pest*, cleaning) mostly do NOT require a trade license**
   and are the cleanest founder-fit.
2. **Financing: as a Canadian (non-US person) he likely cannot use SBA 7(a).** Most US home-service
   deals are priced assuming a US buyer puts 10% down + SBA. All-cash at $500K limits him to the
   $150K–$400K SDE tier or forces meaningful seller financing. See §Financing.

Best sub-play for a $500K all-cash Canadian buyer: **a route-based recurring business — pool
service, lawn/landscape maintenance, or residential pest control — with 150+ recurring accounts,
$150–220K SDE, at ~2.0–2.5x, structured with a seller note.**

## 3. Owner-hours reality

- **Trades (HVAC/plumbing/electrical):** owner-operator role is heavy (bidding, scheduling, crew
  management, emergency calls). 40–60 hr/wk unless a strong GM is in place. License-holder must be
  reachable. Best if you retain the seller as licensed qualifier for 12–24 months.
- **Route businesses (pool/lawn/pest/cleaning):** most owner-operator-friendly. 30–50 hr/wk; a lot
  of it is routing, quality checks, sales, and hiring/retaining technicians. Genuinely delegable to
  route techs + a lead. This is where Hooman's software/automation edge (routing optimization,
  CRM/scheduling, review-generation, churn prediction) is a real value-creation lever.
- **Restoration:** on-call, insurance-relationship-driven, lumpy. Not a clean part-time or
  smooth-cash-flow business; project management intensive.

## 4. Financing reality for a CANADIAN citizen (no SBA)

- **SBA 7(a)/504 is almost certainly OUT.** SBA requires the guarantor(s) owning ≥20% to be US
  citizens or lawful permanent residents (green card). A Canadian on a work visa (e.g. TN/H-1B via
  Adobe) generally does **not** qualify. This removes the standard US "10% down + SBA covers 90%"
  leverage that most of these asking prices assume. **FLAG on every US deal.**
- **What that means practically:** with $500K cash, Hooman is a **cash buyer** competing in a market
  where most rivals bring 10x leverage. Upside: cash offers are strong and can win on price/speed.
  Downside: he's capped at businesses whose *total* price he can cover in cash (roughly the
  $150–220K SDE tier at 2–2.5x), OR he must secure **seller financing / vendor take-back** (common
  in this category — several listings above explicitly offer it) to stretch into the $500–700K tier.
- **Seller notes are the key unlock:** e.g. deal #1 (OKC pest, $650K/$317K SDE) works if the seller
  carries ~$200K over 5 yrs — the SDE easily services it. Target structures like 50–60% cash down +
  40–50% seller note, earnout, or holdback.
- **Canadian options if buying in Canada instead:** BDC (Business Development Bank of Canada) and
  the Canada Small Business Financing Program (CSBFP) can fund acquisitions for citizens/residents,
  and major banks do acquisition lending against cash flow + assets. If he's open to buying a
  Canadian home-service business, financing is materially easier than as a foreign cash buyer in
  the US. **Recommend: verify his US visa/residency status before assuming any US-loan path; if he's
  buying in the US, plan around all-cash + seller note.**

## 5. Hooman-fit assessment

**Where his edge helps (real value creation):**
- Route/recurring businesses are **operationally software-improvable**: route density optimization,
  automated scheduling/dispatch, dynamic pricing, churn/renewal prediction, review-generation
  automation, and demand forecasting. His AI/ML + data-engineering background is a genuine margin
  lever here — most incumbents run on paper, spreadsheets, or clunky field-service software.
- His diligence/analytical ability is a direct advantage in a market of unsophisticated sellers with
  messy books — he can find mispriced deals (e.g. the ~1x-SDE wildlife-control and ~1.2x FL-HVAC
  outliers warrant hard diligence: either bargains or hiding something).

**Where his edge is irrelevant / where he's exposed:**
- **Trade licensing** (HVAC/plumbing/electrical) — his EE degree does NOT grant a contractor's
  license; this is the biggest non-fit. Route businesses sidestep it.
- **Local labor management** (hiring/retaining techs, no-shows, turnover) is the real day-to-day
  grind and is not a software problem. This is the operator skill he'd be buying into cold.
- **Local, physical, owner-present** — the opposite of remote SaaS. Requires relocating to / being
  present in the market. He said he's willing to go full-time, so in scope, but it's a lifestyle shift.

**Verdict on fit:** Good fit *if* he targets a **route-based recurring business (pool / lawn / pest /
window / commercial cleaning)** where (a) no trade license blocks him, (b) his automation edge
creates margin, and (c) cash + seller note clears the price. Trades are higher-SDE but the license
barrier makes them a worse fit for a solo Canadian non-tradesperson.

## 6. Category verdict

**Strong vein — top-tier for "best cash flow at $500K."** Cheap multiples (2.1–2.8x SDE), high cash
yield (30–45%), durable/AI-safe demand, and abundant supply of listings. The two things that turn a
"maybe" into a "yes" for Hooman specifically: **(1) pick a no-license route/recurring model, and
(2) structure with a seller note since SBA leverage is off the table.**

**Best single sub-play:** residential **pest control** or **pool-service route** with 150+ recurring
accounts, ~$180–220K SDE, ~2.0–2.5x, seller carrying 40–50%. Pest control edges it out: lowest
recurring-model multiple (2.10x), true recurring contracts, applicator-license attainable by exam,
and the OKC ($317K SDE) / Camden NJ (1000+ contracts) / bed-bug ($192K SDE) listings are concrete
targets. Runner-up sub-play: **HVAC** for raw SDE — but only if he can lock a licensed qualifier and
finance the higher ticket, which is a materially harder path for him.
