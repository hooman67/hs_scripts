**Researched**: 2026-07-03 by Claude (Opus 4.8). Side-quest acquisition search (~$500K cash, full-time OK, Canadian buyer).
**Sources**: Empire Flippers marketplace (live listings), BizBuySell online/technology category (live listings), Acquire.com, Quiet Light (gated). All numbers below are as-displayed on the marketplaces on 2026-07-03; broker fees/verification not applied.

## 1. How this category is priced (the key thing to understand)

- **Amazon FBA & e-commerce sell on SDE / net-profit multiples, NOT revenue.** Empire Flippers quotes a **"listing multiple" in MONTHS of net profit.** Divide by 12 for the annual SDE multiple.
  - EF listings on 2026-07-03 ranged **26x–37x monthly = ~2.2x–3.1x annual SDE.**
  - BizBuySell "asking price ÷ cash flow" on the same listings implied **~1.6x–2.5x SDE** (BizBuySell listings are cheaper/messier and often owner-brokered).
- **Cash yield is very high on paper** (SDE/price = 30–60%), which is exactly *why* multiples are low: buyers demand a huge return because these assets are **fragile and depreciating.**
- **Owner-hours: highly variable.** "Turnkey/automated" FBA stores claim <10 hrs/wk but that is marketing; a real single-brand FBA business with inventory, PPC, supplier management, and reviews is **20–40+ hrs/wk** of genuine operating work. Multi-store operations are effectively a full-time job.

### Typical economics for a ~$500K all-cash buy in this category
| Metric | Typical range |
|---|---|
| SDE multiple paid | **2.0x–3.0x annual SDE** (2.5x–3.5x for clean, aged, diversified brands) |
| Implied annual SDE on $500K | **~$165K–$250K** |
| Implied monthly cash flow | **~$14K–$21K/mo** (pre-tax, owner-operated) |
| Paper cash yield | **33–50%** — but discount heavily for decay risk |
| Realistic owner-hours | 20–40 hrs/wk (single brand); full-time for multi-brand |

## 3. Financing reality for a Canadian buyer (NO SBA)

- **SBA 7(a) is the entire leverage engine of the US FBA-acquisition world** (10% down, 90% financed). Hooman, as a **Canadian citizen, almost certainly cannot use it** → he loses the standard playbook that lets US buyers pay 3–4x SDE with little cash.
- **This is actually an ADVANTAGE for an all-cash Canadian buyer**: SBA-dependent bidders are frozen out of sub-$700K deals when credit tightens, and an all-cash close is attractive to sellers. Hooman can comfortably buy any listing above at **all-cash within $500K** (A is $531K, close negotiable).
- **Seller notes / earnouts are standard and advisable here** — because these assets decay, structure **30–50% as a seller note or earnout tied to trailing revenue** to shift decay risk onto the seller. This is the single most important lever in the category.
- **Canadian bank / BDC financing generally will NOT lend against a US Amazon FBA account** (intangible, foreign-platform-dependent collateral). Treat this as an **all-cash + seller-note** category, full stop.
- Cross-border/tax: buying via Amazon US requires a US entity/EIN and sales-tax handling; solvable but adds ~$5–15K setup + accounting overhead.

## 4. Hooman-fit

- **Where his edge helps a lot:** PPC/advertising optimization, listing/keyword data analysis, demand forecasting, inventory/reorder automation, and building internal tooling — these are exactly the levers that separate a well-run FBA brand from a decaying one. His data/AI skills are genuine value-creation here, not decoration.
- **Where his edge is irrelevant / risk sits:** the moat is NOT technical. It's brand, reviews, supplier relationships, and Amazon's goodwill — all of which can evaporate via an **account suspension, a review purge, an ad-cost spike, a Chinese copycat, or an Amazon policy change.** No amount of ML fixes a suspended Seller Central account.
- **Owner-dependence:** supplier relationships and Amazon account health are the two owner-critical pieces; both transfer with real handover risk. Full-time, hands-on ownership is realistic here.

## 5. Honest category verdict

**Weak-to-moderate vein for the *best* ~$500K acquisition. High cash yield, but structurally fragile and not durable — the low multiples are the market pricing in real decay risk.**

- The 2.0–3.0x SDE multiples look like a bargain vs. SaaS (3–5x) or service businesses, but that discount is *earned*: single-brand Amazon FBA is a **platform-dependent, single-point-of-failure asset** with no financing support for a Canadian buyer and constant margin erosion from rising Amazon fees + PPC.
- **Best sub-play:** an **aged (5+ yr), multi-channel brand with off-Amazon revenue (own Shopify + TikTok Shop), diversified SKUs, and defensible brand/IP** — bought **all-cash at ≤2.5x SDE with a large seller note/earnout** to offload decay risk. Listing **A (giftable, TikTok-diversified, ~2x)** and **C (jewelry, ~1.5x)** are the closest to that ideal in the current inventory.
- **Avoid:** thin-margin (<15% net) single-SKU FBA stores (D, E), "turnkey/automated" dropship-flavored listings (F, G) until proven, and anything at >3.5x SDE (B, H) given no leverage to make the return math work.
- **Vs. other categories in this side-quest:** e-commerce offers the highest *headline* yield and the easiest all-cash fit, but the *worst durability*. It should rank below cash-flowing service/"boring" businesses on a risk-adjusted basis, and is best considered only if a genuinely diversified, aged brand appears at a ≤2x multiple with a seller note.
