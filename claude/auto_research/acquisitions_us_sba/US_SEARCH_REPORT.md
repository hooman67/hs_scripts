## 2. The two winning veins (where to actually spend diligence)

### 🥇 Vein 1 — Non-franchise recurring-route service (pest / pool / commercial landscaping)
**Why it's #1 now:** This is the category the US-person change genuinely rescued. Previously it was
capped at $500K all-cash AND needed an E-2 to operate hands-on — both gone. It has:
- **The most durable, AI-safe moat in the whole project** — 400–1000+ recurring auto-renew contracts
  with high switching cost. A recession- and AI-proof annuity.
- **His engineering as a real EBITDA lever** — route-density optimization (10–20% more stops/tech),
  churn/renewal prediction, dispatch + review automation. Incumbents run on paper/QuickBooks.
- **His diligence alpha** — spotting owner-labor-heavy SDE traps (the sub-1.4x listings are
  owner-sweat disguised as profit; the 2–3x contract-book deals are the real assets).
- **Live in-budget targets:** Camden NJ pest ($500K/$180K SDE, 1000+ contracts) is the cleanest fit;
  OKC pest ($650K/$317K, needs seller note) is the best economics; Elkhart IN landscaping
  ($600K/$208K, 60% recurring) is the best recurring-mix.
- **Deal-killers to check:** pest control needs no trade license (good); HVAC/fire routes need a
  licensed qualifier (friction); avoid sub-1.4x owner-labor listings and franchised routes.

### 🥈 Vein 2 — Laundromats (best passive cash, his diligence edge)
**Why it's the co-lead:** Different risk/return shape — lower absolute cash but genuinely
semi-absentee (5–15 hr/wk, day job stays optional) and the single most diligenceable asset for
Hooman specifically:
- **~30–37% all-cash cash-on-cash** at ~3× SDE, fits the budget with reserve.
- **His exact diligence edge** — regress water/sewer/electric bills against reported revenue to
  catch inflated cash (better than 99% of buyers).
- **Hands-on operation now lets him add value** — card/app payment, remote monitoring, dynamic
  pricing (previously blocked when he had to stay remote/absentee).
- **Live targets:** Fords NJ ($285K/$95K), Allentown PA ($250K/$80K), Houston super-laundromat
  ($650K/$230K, needs seller note + hard utility diligence).
- **The one killer:** the lease. You rarely own the dirt → landlord non-renewal / rent hike is the
  #1 laundromat failure mode. Require 10+ yr lease options or find a rare RE-inclusive laundromat.

## 3. Why everything else is a pass (all-cash)

- **Franchise/multi-unit — PASS.** Two strikes, both financing-independent: (1) the passive
  GM-run multi-unit machines ($734K SDE semi-absentee, 6-unit Subway, 13-unit Jamba) all sit at
  $1.3–2.5M = leverage-only; all-cash pushes you down to single-unit operator jobs. (2) The
  franchisor owns your best lever (software/pricing/margin), stranding your edge. Best-in-category
  (City Wide, ~5.5) still loses to a plain pest route dollar-for-dollar.
- **Self-storage / car-wash-with-heavy-RE — PASS.** They trade at 5–8% cap rates; all-cash the cap
  rate *is* your yield. Every real facility is $825K–$2.9M (over ceiling), and the one high-cap deal
  needs the SBA debt you're declining. (The SBA-504 thesis for this category was explicitly withdrawn.)
- **Niche CNC/fab — sub-band only.** Genuine CutLog domain overlap and AI-safe, but the systematized
  shops with real EBITDA are $1.5–2M (leverage-only). Only sub-$650K listings (Buncombe NC signage/CNC
  $370K/$155K) survive all-cash, and those are full-time operator jobs with equipment-age + customer-
  concentration risk.
- **B2B distribution — sub-band only.** The strong deals (CA building products $2M/$596K, VA print/promo
  $1.1M/$349K) are all over the all-cash ceiling. Genuinely diversified (non-Amazon) product brands
  are rare on public listings; single-platform FBA stays a demoted trap.
- **Vertical B2B SaaS — no clean GO on-shelf.** All-cash at $300–650K clears the delegation-affordability
  threshold (a real improvement over the $150–300K "buy-a-job" tier), but the mandate-grade
  system-of-record moats (the only durable ones) are either leverage-only ($1.5M+) or trade off-market.
  On-shelf listings hide the decisive number (SDE / services-split / Stripe trend). The higher-yield
  path for this shape is **off-market buy-side outreach** to EH&S/SDS/compliance long-tail vendors,
  not marketplace browsing (per the prior `saas_deepdive` finding).
- **Content/media/education — PASS.** AI-melting. Only one all-cash-sized candidate (EF #92346, a
  credentialed-freelancer education + newsletter, $251K) merits even a diligence hour, contingent on
  verifying non-SEO traffic. Leverage would make this category *worse*, not better.

  and his engineering is a genuine margin lever without a sales-rainmaker dependency.

**The honest read:** if Hooman wants **remote + software**, BC Staffing SaaS is still the top pick (and
the outreach is ready). If he'll **operate hands-on** (which he can now), **US recurring-route service
is the better risk-adjusted all-cash asset** — durable moat, fits budget, edge matters, no owner-sales
dependency. These are the two finalists; the choice is remote-software vs. hands-on-durable-cashflow.
