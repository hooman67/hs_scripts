## 0. TL;DR verdict — the constraint change flips the whole thesis

With SBA in play this category jumps from "cheap all-cash cash-cow you can barely afford" to
**the single best-fit path for Hooman's stated goal ($5–30K/mo durable, AI-safe secondary
income).** The reasons:

1. **SBA lets him buy the DELEGABLE tier, not the owner-operator tier.** At $500K all-cash he
   was stuck buying a ~$180K-SDE business where *he* is the route tech. With SBA he reaches
   **$400–650K SDE, manager-run** businesses where a GM + techs run daily ops and his job is
   *systematizing* — exactly where his edge (route-density optimization, churn prediction,
   dispatch/review automation) becomes a **margin lever on a bigger base.** LEARNINGS.md rule:
   *delegation pencils above ~$4K/mo net* — these deals clear that by 3–6x. VERIFIED (internal).
2. **The moat is real and AI-safe.** A book of 400–1000+ recurring contracts with quarterly/
   monthly auto-renew billing and high switching cost is the most durable thing in this whole
   research project. An LLM cannot spray a house, clean a pool, or pass a fire inspection.
3. **His edge SHIFTS from operating to systematizing at this size** (per the task brief). The
   incumbents run on paper/QuickBooks/whiteboards. Route density, tech utilization, churn-save
   campaigns, and Google-review automation are 5–15 pt margin levers he can actually build.
4. **The deal-killer moves to financeability + license, not affordability.** SBA underwrites
   the contract book fine; the two new gates are (a) DSCR after a market-rate manager salary,
   and (b) trade licensing (HVAC/fire = gated; pest/pool/landscape/cleaning = attainable-by-exam
   or none). Pick the un-gated verticals.

**Best sub-play now:** a **manager-run residential pest control or commercial landscaping
business, $350–650K SDE, 2.0–3.0x, ~$1–2M price, 60%+ recurring**, financed 90% SBA, with the
seller on a 12–24 mo transition and a partial seller note behind the SBA loan.

## 1. SBA financing math (the new lens — run this on every deal)

**Assumptions (VERIFIED market conditions, ASSUMED rates for modeling):** SBA 7(a), 10% down,
90% financed, **10-yr term, ~10.5% rate** (Prime ~7.75% + 2.75%). Debt service factor ≈
**$0.162 annual per $1 borrowed** ($1.00M loan ≈ $162K/yr; $1.35M ≈ $218K/yr).

| Price | Down (10%) | Loan | Annual debt svc | SDE needed for 1.5x DSCR* | Owner cash after debt + $90K mgr |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $1.0M | $100K | $900K | ~$146K | ~$310K SDE | ~$74K/yr ($6.2K/mo) |
| $1.5M | $150K | $1.35M | ~$218K | ~$425K SDE | ~$117K/yr ($9.7K/mo) |
| $2.0M | $200K | $1.8M | ~$291K | ~$525K SDE | ~$144K/yr ($12K/mo) |

\*SBA lenders want post-manager-salary DSCR ≥ 1.25–1.5. **The critical adjustment:** at these
sizes you MUST subtract a **market-rate GM/operations-manager salary (~$80–100K)** from SDE to
get true delegated cash flow, because the reported SDE embeds the seller's full-time labor.
The "owner cash after debt + mgr" column is the honest $5–30K/mo number. **Deals where SDE is
mostly one owner's sweat (solo routes) do NOT survive this adjustment** — they're the
all-cash-tier trap, not the SBA-tier prize.

**Down-payment reality:** $100–200K down leaves Hooman **$300–400K reserve** — comfortable
working capital + a cushion the June all-cash plan never had. This is a materially safer
structure than the prior $500K-all-cash-no-buffer Camden deal.

## 4. Where Hooman's edge is REAL vs. IRRELEVANT (at SBA/manager-run scale)

**REAL value creation (the thesis at this size — systematize, don't operate):**
- **Route density optimization** — the #1 margin lever in pest/pool/landscape; incumbents
  hand-build routes. 10–20% more stops/day/tech drops straight to EBITDA. VERIFIED as
  standard industry lever; ASSUMED magnitude.
- **Churn / renewal prediction** — a recurring-contract book lives or dies on retention; a
  model flagging at-risk accounts + auto save-campaigns is a direct LTV lever he can build.
- **Dispatch + scheduling automation + review generation** — turns a paper/QuickBooks shop
  into a modern one; also lifts local SEO (more Google reviews = more inbound = lower CAC).
- **Diligence alpha** — spotting the P8/P9/P10 sub-1.4x-SDE listings that are cheap because
  the SDE is one owner's unreplaceable labor (a trap at SBA scale, not a bargain). His edge is
  *not* getting fooled by owner-labor-heavy SDE.

**IRRELEVANT / exposure (what he's buying into cold):**
- **Hiring/retaining/managing techs** — the real daily grind; not a software problem. At
  manager-run scale he manages the *manager*, which is better fit, but tech turnover is the
  operational risk that no algorithm fixes.
- **Trade licensing** (HVAC/fire) — his EE degree does not qualify; requires seller-as-qualifier
  or a hired qualified party. This is the category's dominant deal-killer for the two
  highest-SDE verticals.
- **Local field operations / physical presence** — the opposite of remote SaaS; he'd relocate
  to / be present in the market during the transition regardless of the software overlay.

## 5. Deal-killers by sub-category (the fast "no" list)

- **HVAC / Fire:** licensed master/qualifying party required. No qualifier path → hard NO.
  (Financeable at SBA scale via salaried qualifier or seller-carry qualifier for 24 mo.)
- **Sub-1.4x-SDE recurring listings (P8/P9/P10, solo pool routes):** SDE is owner sweat-equity;
  fails the post-manager DSCR test. These are all-cash-tier, not SBA-tier. NO for this thesis.
- **Franchises (mosquito/wildlife/window/cleaning franchises):** the franchisor removes the
  software levers (mandated CRM/routing/branding), takes 6–8% royalty off the top, and often
  bans resale software — **his entire edge is neutered.** Also C2 explicitly "NO SBA." Demote.
- **Janitorial:** low switching cost + brutal labor turnover weaken the "contract book moat"
  more than the count implies. Only with unusually sticky (multi-year, specialized) contracts.
- **Key-person / niche (P2 K-9 detection):** moat = the owner or the dog, not a transferable book.
- **Seller-succession / age risk (P3 40-yr, P4 3rd-gen):** verify contracts transfer on sale and
  aren't personal-relationship-dependent; require a 12–24 mo seller transition + non-compete.
