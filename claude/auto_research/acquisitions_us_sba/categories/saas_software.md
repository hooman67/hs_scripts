## 1. All-cash economics (the number that decides every deal)

- At a fair **~3.0x SDE**, **$500K all-cash buys ~$165K SDE ≈ $13–14K/mo pre-tax** owner cash flow
  (~33% cash-on-cash) IF revenue is stable and churn is low. Stretch to **~$650K with a seller note**
  → up to ~$210K SDE ≈ **$17K/mo.** (VERIFIED math; multiple ASSUMED at market ~3x.)
- **No debt service, no personal guarantee → downside is capped at cash-in** (the thesis the prior
  rounds protected). This is the whole reason all-cash was chosen; don't reintroduce recourse risk.
- **Delegation-affordability (carried from LEARNINGS)**: a VA+contractor-dev only pencils above
  **~$4K/mo net.** A $300–650K buy at 3x throws off **~$8–17K/mo** — comfortably above that line, so
  these ARE delegable to ~8–15 hr/wk part-time, unlike the sub-$300K "buy-a-job" tier.
- **The binding constraint is still SUPPLY**, not affordability: a clean, sticky, fairly-priced
  vertical-B2B system-of-record at $300–650K all-cash is rare on the open market (the good ones get
  rolled up off-market before listing — the EH&S/SDS finding from the deep-dive).
