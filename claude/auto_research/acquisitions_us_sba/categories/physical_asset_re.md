  These trade on **~3x SDE / cash flow**, not on cap rate, so all-cash they throw **~25–35%

  are AI-safe, recession-resilient, and — critically — **directly diligenceable with Hooman's exact

**One thing DID improve:** hands-on operating is now allowed (no E-2, no remote-only filter). That

**Bottom line:** the SBA-leverage thesis in the prior draft of this file is **withdrawn.** All-cash,

## 1. All-cash scoring basis

All-cash, the metrics that matter are simple and unforgiving:
- **Cap rate = your yield** (storage/RE-priced deals). 6% cap = 6% yield. No debt to create spread.
- **SDE multiple** (operating deals): all-cash cash-on-cash ≈ **1 ÷ multiple** (a 3x SDE deal ≈ 33%
  gross yield) — **before** subtracting the owner's own labor. For semi-absentee laundromats the
  owner-labor deduction is small (5–15 hr/wk); for full-service car washes it's large.
- **Fit ceiling:** ~$500K all-cash, ~$650–700K with a 20–40% seller note. Anything materially above
  that is **out of reach** regardless of quality — flagged as such below.

No SBA debt-service math anywhere in this version.

Staying all-cash **re-confirms the prior demotion** of the RE-heavy end of this category. The
earlier SBA-504 thesis is **withdrawn.**

**What IS the all-cash anchor from this category: LAUNDROMATS** (plus, as a fair second, self-serve
car washes without heavy RE). They:
- throw **~30–37% all-cash pre-tax yield** at real ~3x SDE (vs. ~6% for storage);
- fit comfortably inside ~$500K all-cash with a large reserve (Fords NJ $285K, Allentown $250K);
- are genuinely **semi-absentee** (5–15 hr/wk) — the day job stays optional;
- are **AI-safe and recession-resilient** (renters always wash clothes) — the most durable, lowest-
  variance income in the whole two-year search;
- are **directly diligenceable with Hooman's #1 edge** — regressing water/sewer/electric utility
  bills against reported revenue to catch inflated cash, better than 99% of buyers;
- and now that he can **operate hands-on**, he can personally capture the value-add (card/app
  payment, remote monitoring to kill skim, dynamic pricing).

### Recommended all-cash shortlist (priority order)

e.g. Van Nuys $799K w/ RE, over budget). This, plus **inflated cash flow** and **machine age**, are
the three things Hooman's diligence must nail.
