## 2. TOP DRY-RUN CANDIDATES (the only ones worth a seller call)

There is **no STRONG-AND-CLEAN listing**. The three below are the best available and are worth

these as the dry-run itself: run Gate 2 on them and expect most to wash out.

### Candidate A — IMS/ISO Management System SaaS (Flippa #11797351) — BEST FIT
- **Why it fits the Buy Box**: This is the archetype — a **compliance / system-of-record** tool
  (ISO/IMS document control, incident & risk mgmt) in boring regulated verticals (mfg, healthcare,
  energy, logistics). **6-year history** (well past the 18-24mo floor). B2B, recurring, sticky
  (compliance records live in it → real switching cost). Flippa data-verified (Stripe/GA connected).
  On-band price at **$139,284** (top of the $75-160K window).
- **Source-verify FIRST (Gate 2, the deal-breakers)**:
  1. **Concentration** — only **9 active subscribers** → near-certain a single customer is >20% of
     revenue. Pull raw Stripe; if the top account is >20%, **walk** (deal-killer #4). This is the
     single most likely kill.
  2. **Churn** — headline **11%** is high and undefined. Build the cohort curve from the raw export;
     confirm it flattens and isn't masked by new logos (deal-killer #3).
  3. Three-way reconcile bank ↔ Stripe ↔ P&L (EUR-denominated); confirm recurring vs one-time/setup.
  4. Tech stack is gated — confirm it's real custom code with a clean IP chain, not no-code.
- **Price-vs-net math**: ~€1,154/mo net ≈ **~$15K/yr SDE**. Fair 4-4.5x = **~$60-68K**. It's asking
  **$139K = ~9x profit** (the card's "6.7x" understates it and even that is above the 4-4.5x fair
  ceiling). **This is ~2x overpriced on earnings** — priced on revenue, not SDE. Only workable with
  a hard negotiation down toward $60-70K, which a 6yr compliance seller is unlikely to accept.
- **DELEGATION-TRAP VERDICT**: At **~$1.2K/mo net, delegation is impossible** — a VA+dev would eat
  100%+ of profit. **Hooman IS the operator** (8-12 hr/wk steady state). But with only 9 customers
  the support load is genuinely light, so <10 hr/wk is plausible *if* it's not one whale needing
  hand-holding. Economics after his time ≈ ~$0.
- **Next click**: free Flippa account → open **https://flippa.com/11797351** → NDA → request
  **read-only Stripe + bank** and ask **"what % of ARR is your single largest customer?"** on the
  first call. If >20% or he won't share Stripe → walk.

## 4. REALISTIC SUPPLY READ — is anything actually buyable?

**No. As of 2026-07-01/02, there is not a single clean, in-Buy-Box, fairly-priced sub-$160K
vertical-B2B SaaS listed across the five marketplaces surveyed.** The scarcity IS the binding
constraint, exactly as the playbook predicted:

- **The affordable end is junk**: Acquire's cheap listings are asset-sales, marketing/agency/UGC
  tools, and platform-dependent Shopify/LinkedIn/Whop apps the Buy Box rejects. Microns and Flippa's
  cheap end is consumer AI wrappers. MotionInvest is entirely SEO-content/YouTube. Latona's floor is
  ~$240K (cheapest SaaS $2.5M) — the whole marketplace is above the ceiling.
- **The good shape is over budget**: the genuinely clean, sticky, growing vertical B2B (Acquire's
  Building-Ops $399K SDE, EF's Amazon-sourcing data-moat SaaS) sits at **$190K-$1.8M** — the
  affordable version would be a 5-10x-smaller sibling that isn't currently listed.
- **The three candidates above are all priced at ~6-9x profit, not the fair 4-4.5x**, and each has
  a live deal-killer (9-16-35 customers = concentration; undefined 11-15% churn; possible AI-wrapper).
  They're worth a *call* precisely because a dry-run should test whether the seller will (a) share
  read-only Stripe and (b) negotiate toward fair value. Expect most to fail one or both.

**Dry-run conclusion**: This snapshot supports the playbook's own verdict — **run the free Gate-1/
Gate-2 dry-run on Candidates A, B, C, and be fully prepared to walk on all three.** If the Stripe
reconcile shows concentration >20% or churn that doesn't flatten (the likely outcome for A and B),
or the seller won't discount from ~6-9x toward 4-4.5x, **the correct move is to keep the capital and
keep the email alerts running.** Nothing here justifies deploying $100-140K today. The economics
even on the "best" candidate (~$1.2-1.6K/mo net, Hooman as sole operator, ~$0 economic profit after
his time) confirm this is a capped-downside part-time job, not passive income — buy only if a clean
asset later clears every gate, not because these three are what's on the shelf.
