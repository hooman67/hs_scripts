- **Technology**: TEGNet AI (publicly available, 10000x faster TEG design)
- **Value prop**: Custom-optimized thermoelectric generator designs in 5 days vs 3-6 months, at 1/10th cost
- **Market**: $10B waste heat recovery, $2B TEG-specific systems

### 2. Precision Pollination Monitoring for Almond/Berry Growers (Feasibility: 7/10)
- **Technology**: mmWave radar (TI IWR6843, $150) + ML species classification
- **Value prop**: See exactly where pollinators are working — verify hive rental ROI, find dead zones, optimize placement
- **Market**: $7B US pollination industry, $2B almond pollination monitoring
- **Capital**: ~$430K to first revenue
- **Key strength**: Zero competition in radar-based pollination monitoring; commodity hardware; massive addressable market in California almonds alone; BeeHero's $40M validates market
- **Key risk**: Seasonal business (bloom = 3 weeks/year for almonds); radar range for bee-sized targets unproven in field; ag sales cycles
- **Revenue model**: Hardware ($4K/unit) + seasonal SaaS ($500/month during bloom)

## Patterns Observed

- **"Publicly available AI tool + domain expertise = consultancy → product" is a powerful model**: TEGNet follows same pattern as NIST alloy research. The AI does the heavy lifting; domain expertise is the moat.
- **Agriculture has seasonal revenue risk**: Pollination monitoring is a great product but revenue concentrates in 3-4 weeks/year. Need to find year-round use cases (pest monitoring, beneficial insect conservation) to smooth revenue.
- **Infrastructure markets (grid, maritime) are too slow for startups**: Even with clear opportunities, sales cycles of 12-24 months to government/utility buyers kill startup economics.
