## Discovery Questions

### Questions to Validate Problem (Ask All 5 Customers)

1. **"Walk me through your current process for building a new multi-gene pathway from scratch. How long does each step take?"**
   - What we're looking for: Confirmation that assembly takes weeks; identification of specific bottlenecks

2. **"How many DNA constructs does your team build per month? Per quarter?"**
   - What we're looking for: Volume data; customers building >50 constructs/month are ideal

3. **"What percentage of your molecular biology team's time is spent on construct assembly vs. other activities (design, characterization, analysis)?"**
   - What we're looking for: >30% of PhD-level time on assembly = strong pain signal

4. **"When a construct fails during assembly, what happens? How long does the re-work take?"**
   - What we're looking for: Failure recovery time; frustration with unpredictable timelines

5. **"If you could build 50+ constructs simultaneously with guaranteed verification, how would that change your R&D strategy?"**
   - What we're looking for: Specific projects they'd attempt; quantified value of speed

### Questions to Validate Solution (After Problem Confirmed)

6. **"Would you prefer a service model (send sequences, receive constructs) or an instrument model (run assembly in your own lab)?"**
   - What we're looking for: Service preference validates our initial model

7. **"What turnaround time would make this transformative for your work? 1 week? 2 weeks? Is 2 weeks fast enough?"**
   - What we're looking for: Acceptable SLA range; urgency of speed vs. cost

8. **"What's your current cost per construct (including labor, reagents, and time)? What would you be willing to pay for a guaranteed, verified construct?"**
   - What we're looking for: Willingness to pay $100-200/construct; validation of pricing

9. **"What construct length and complexity are you typically working with?"**
   - What we're looking for: Confirmation that 1-10 kb is the sweet spot; identification of edge cases

10. **"What's your biggest concern about using an external service for DNA assembly? (Quality? IP protection? Reliability?)"**
    - What we're looking for: Objections to address; trust barriers to overcome

### Questions to Validate Willingness to Pay

11. **"If we offered a 50-construct assembly service at $100/construct with 2-week turnaround and full sequence verification, would you run a pilot with us this quarter?"**
    - What we're looking for: "Yes" or "let me check budget" = strong signal; "maybe next year" = weak signal

12. **"What budget line would this come from? R&D consumables? Service contracts? Is there budget available now or would you need to request it?"**
    - What we're looking for: Available budget NOW vs. "next fiscal year" — indicates urgency

13. **"Would you sign a letter of intent for a beta program at 50% discount?"**
    - What we're looking for: Hard commitment; willingness to put company name on paper

## Demand Signals to Track

### Strong Positive Signals

| Signal | What It Means | Threshold for "Validated" |
|--------|--------------|--------------------------|
| "Can we be your first customer?" | Desperate for solution | 1 unprompted offer |
| LOI signed within 2 weeks of meeting | Budget and authority confirmed | 3 LOIs from 5 meetings |
| Specific project identified for pilot | Real use case, not hypothetical | 3 specific projects described |
| Willingness to pay full price (no discount) | Strong value perception | 2 customers at full price |
| Referral to other teams/companies | Organic demand | 2 referrals received |
| "We'd cancel our Twist order for this" | Switching intent | 1 statement of intent |

### Moderate Positive Signals

| Signal | What It Means |
|--------|--------------|
| "Interesting, send me more info" | Curiosity but not urgency |
| "We'd try it at a lower price" | Price sensitivity; may not value enough |
| "Talk to our lab manager" | Delegation (not top priority for decision maker) |
| "What about IP protection?" | Serious consideration of adoption |
| "How does this compare to BioXp?" | Evaluating alternatives (aware of need) |

### Negative Signals (Market Risk)

| Signal | What It Means |
|--------|--------------|
| "We do this fine internally" | No perceived pain |
| "Our construct needs are only 5-10 per project" | Wrong customer segment |
| "We'd need 6 months to evaluate a new vendor" | Institutional inertia |
| "Twist is launching something similar" | Competitive threat confirmed |
| "We're building our own automation" | Self-serve preference |

## Outreach Plan

### Phase 1: Warm Network (Weeks 1-4)

**Goal**: Get 10 discovery conversations booked

| Channel | Action | Target | Expected Response Rate |
|---------|--------|--------|----------------------|
| LinkedIn | Direct outreach to VP/Director Strain Engineering at 30 synbio companies | 30 messages | 15-20% (4-6 conversations) |
| SynBioBeta Slack | Post about research on assembly bottlenecks; ask for interviews | 1 post | 3-5 responses |
| Academic collaborators | Email 10 principal investigators who publish on DNA assembly | 10 emails | 30% (3 conversations) |
| Cold email (personalized) | Target specific companies with public info about their construct needs | 20 emails | 10% (2 conversations) |

**Message template (LinkedIn)**:
```
Hi [Name], I'm researching the DNA construct assembly bottleneck 
in metabolic engineering. I noticed [Company] is building [specific 
application from their publications/press].

I'm exploring whether a massively parallel assembly service 
(50+ constructs simultaneously, verified, 2-week turnaround) 
would meaningfully accelerate your DBTL cycles.

Would you be open to a 20-minute call to share your perspective 
on the assembly workflow? Happy to share our findings in return.
```

### Phase 3: Content Marketing (Weeks 4-16)

| Content | Channel | Goal |
|---------|---------|------|
| "The Assembly Bottleneck" blog post | Medium / LinkedIn | Establish problem awareness; attract inbound |
| Benchmark study: manual assembly vs. our approach | Preprint (bioRxiv) | Credibility with technical buyers |
| Webinar: "Accelerating DBTL Cycles" | Zoom (invite from Phase 1 contacts) | Convert awareness to intent |
| Design tool demo (open source fragment) | GitHub + Twitter | Technical credibility; community building |

### Phase 4: Pilot Program Launch (Weeks 12-20)

**Pilot program terms:**
- 5 customers selected from Phase 1-2 conversations
- 50% discount on first run (50 constructs at $50/construct = $2,500 per pilot)
- Full support and dedicated communication channel
- Customer provides feedback via structured interview post-delivery
- Agreement to serve as reference/case study (anonymized if preferred)

**Selection criteria for pilot customers:**
1. Clear, immediate need (project identified, budget available)
2. Responsive and engaged (quick email replies, asks good questions)
3. Right size (50-200 constructs needed, not 5 and not 5000)
4. Referenceable (recognizable brand in synbio community)
5. Potential for repeat business (ongoing R&D program, not one-off)

## Competitive Intelligence to Gather During Validation

During every customer conversation, also ask:

1. "Have you heard of any other companies offering parallel assembly services?"
2. "Has Twist (or your current vendor) mentioned anything about assembly services?"
3. "Are you evaluating BioXp or any other automated assembly solution?"
4. "What would make you NOT switch from your current approach?"
5. "Who else in the industry do you think would want this?"

Track answers systematically. If 3+ customers mention a competitor we haven't identified, investigate immediately.
