## Finalists (Step 5 Graduates)

### 1. High-Bandwidth CubeSat Communication Modules
- **Technology**: Origami deployable reflectarray antennas (space-validated April 2026)
- **Value prop**: 1000x data rate improvement for CubeSats ($100K satellites doing $10M satellite work)
- **Market**: $9.35B small satellite market, 28% CAGR, $2B comm subsystem TAM
- **Capital needed**: ~$1.8M to first revenue
- **Feasibility score**: 7/10
- **Key risk**: Manufacturing precision at scale; IP protection
- **Key strength**: Technology already proven in orbit (OrigamiSat-2)

### 2. Massively Parallel Gene Assembly Platform
- **Technology**: Parallel DNA assembly (inspired by Genyro Sidewinder's 320-construct-in-parallel approach)
- **Value prop**: Reduce gene construction from weeks to hours; build 50+ constructs simultaneously
- **Market**: $3.74B DNA synthesis market, 17% CAGR gene synthesis segment
- **Capital needed**: ~$1.2M to commercial launch
- **Feasibility score**: 7/10
- **Key risk**: Developing alternative parallel assembly chemistry (Genyro holds Caltech IP)
- **Key strength**: Clear bottleneck in rapidly growing market; straightforward service model

### 3. Genome-Scale Engineering Service Bureau
- **Technology**: Hierarchical assembly pipeline combining parallel gene assembly + yeast chromosome cloning
- **Value prop**: Build complete synthetic genomes (50 kb – 1 Mb) in weeks instead of months
- **Market**: $24.58B synthetic biology market, 28.6% CAGR
- **Capital needed**: ~$3.5M to commercial operation (18 months)
- **Feasibility score**: 6/10
- **Key risk**: Higher capital intensity; longer time to revenue; fewer customers initially
- **Key strength**: Enables projects currently impossible; winner-take-most dynamics

## Patterns Observed

1. **Energy/materials technologies often fail at Step 1 or Step 2**: Either too early (TRL 4) or already dominated by massive incumbents
2. **Space tech passed all stages**: Less crowded market, high barriers to entry, explosive growth (28% CAGR)
3. **Biotech has the best balance**: High TRL + differentiated approaches possible + large growing markets
4. **IP is the key differentiator at Step 2**: Technologies with strong patent positions (Quaise, Genyro) create moats — both for the holder AND as barriers to new entrants
5. **Hardware at scale is hard to enter**: Sodium-ion batteries, AI servers — even with better tech, manufacturing scale wins
6. **Service models reduce risk**: Both surviving biotech ideas can start as services before building instruments
