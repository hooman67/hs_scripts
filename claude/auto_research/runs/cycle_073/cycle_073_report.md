## ⭐ DOMAIN CLOSE-OUT: NFPA-96 Hood-Cleaning family (cycles 070–073)

**Verdict: the domain does NOT hold a defensible #2 candidate. 0 graduates across 4 cycles / 40
product angles.** This is a valid, high-confidence "no" — the same result 9 of 10 Round-3 domains
returned.

**Why the whole family fails (one root cause, four instances):**
1. **Incumbent gate — already breached everywhere.** Every sub-trade is served by MULTIPLE
   purpose-built apps that already ship the exact thesis (photo-evidence + compliance certificate
   + recurring recompliance scheduling): hood cleaning (7+: HoodOps, HoodClean Pro, MyHoodClean,
   Claver, Guspora, FAT FINGER, Hood Cleaning Report), FOG (5+: PumpDocket, Grease Trap Dispatch,
   FAT FINGER, ServiceCore, FieldTracer), fire suppression/doors (Inspect Point ~15K users,
   ServiceTrade, + 9 fire-door apps), dryer vent (5+: Vent & Duct Pro, Crewara, 360ServicePro,
   LayCor, WePro). The named kill-risk (horizontal FSM adding a template) is ALSO fully realized —
   ServiceTrade, Jobber, and Housecall Pro all cover these trades. There is no "incumbents aren't
   watching" gap; VC-free doesn't mean founder-free, and 2026 saw a wave of niche launches (HoodOps
   Aug-2026).
2. **Moat gate — the fatal one.** The "site-history + photo-evidence register" is real
   switching-cost lock-in but is **per-tenant data, not a flywheel** — my contractor's history
   doesn't improve your jobs, and cloning the UI clones the "moat." Worse, the two mechanisms that
   COULD create a compounding asset both relocate it OFF the founder: (a) the **insurer-accepted
   proof layer** (EvidLY) migrates verification value to the carrier; (b) the **municipal mandated
   portal** (SwiftComply/Truwave in FOG) makes the government's vendor own the record. In every
   variant, the compounding data accrues to someone other than the founder — the precise Round-4
   disqualifier. This is the CutLog-community / PestDoc-workflow failure mode repeating.
3. **SAM gate.** The correct buyer (fragmented private contractor) yields a countable universe of
   low-thousands of firms at $49–300/mo ACV → $9–18M/yr SAM per trade, an order of magnitude below
   $100M. The only size-viable framing (a registry across all restaurants/buildings) flips the
   buyer to the insurer/AHJ → buyer-gate instant-reject.
4. **Growth gate.** All sub-trades grow ~4–9% CAGR (foodservice/construction-linked), below 15%.

**Founder-fit inversion (consistent with LEARNINGS.md):** these are trivially buildable for
Hooman — which is the anti-signal, not a green light. The binding constraint is distribution/sales
to fragmented small contractors against entrenched niche apps and 15K-user incumbents — his
documented weakness, not his infra/ML strength.

**Recommendation**: Retire the NFPA-96 hood-cleaning family (Round-4 domain #5) as a dead end.
Do NOT deep-research it. It slots alongside Fire Sprinkler and Compressed Air as a
false-empty-competition trade. Redirect the remaining Round-4 budget toward the domains where the
operator can UNIQUELY ACCUMULATE proprietary data no portal/insurer holds — the TitlePlant-class
"private plant" theses (well-log / subsurface, ag-drainage as-built, locksmith key-plant), which
are the only structures in the map that survive the moat gate in principle.

Confidence: incumbent existence VERIFIED (live product pages + search across all four sub-trades);
market-size, ACV, and CAGR figures ASSUMED (low-quality secondary sources; IBISWorld/Census
blocked); IKECA membership (~250–400) and Inspect Point (~15K users) VERIFIED from search / prior
