## Domain #12 (Locksmith) — FINAL VERDICT: HONEST ZERO (double-killed)

The domain map called this "the cleanest CutLog analog" on moat *shape* — and it is: a per-facility
keying database is a genuine high-switching-cost, per-account data asset. But it fails on **both**
gates, independently:

1. **Incumbent axis (098):** the exact product already exists and is mature. **ProMaster Key Manager
   8** (WH Software) ships the precise proposed system — locksmith-side master-keying design +
   facility-side issuance ledger + the data handoff between them. **InstaKey / SecurityRecords.com**
   (VERIFIED, 1,200+ cloud users, national-chain customers) owns the enterprise cloud segment. The
   moat exists but was accumulated by incumbents over 20+ years — nothing left for the founder to
   uniquely accumulate.
2. **SAM axis (099):** only ~2,000–5,000 master-key-active commercial locksmiths × a low (~$300–1,200/yr)
   price anchor ≈ **$2.5–6M SAM**, 10–40× below the $100M floor, in a **flat-to-eroding** market
   (electronic access control slowly displacing mechanical master keys).

**No defensible #2 candidate in locksmith key-plant.** Valid, expected honest zero.
