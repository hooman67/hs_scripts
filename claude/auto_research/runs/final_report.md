## The Winning Archetype (Confirmed Across 40 Cycles)

**Software intelligence + commodity hardware + underserved mid-market + no regulatory barriers + immediate measurable ROI**

Now validated with 74 graduates across 40 cycles. The pattern holds.

Refinements from cycles 021-040:
1. **"Software on existing hardware" is even better than "software + commodity hardware"** — ideas that layer AI on equipment customers already own (XRF guns, telematics, press cameras, SCADA systems) score highest because they eliminate ALL hardware risk
2. **"Parasitic SaaS" is the capital-efficient extreme** — connect to existing telemetry APIs (Samsara, Geotab, SolarEdge) without touching the physical world at all
3. **Regulatory compliance is a tailwind, not a headwind** — ideas driven by compliance mandates (IPM, DOT aggregate, food safety) have captive buyers who CANNOT say no
4. **Consumable/chemical optimization has the best ROI story** — savings appear on next month's invoice (vs hardware ROI which requires capital justification)

## What Consistently Fails (Updated)

1. **Novel hardware requiring manufacturing** — vine robots, metallic glass motors, new sensor types
2. **Regulatory-gated markets** — medical devices, pharmaceuticals, nuclear, novel food ingredients
3. **Government/utility buyers** — 12-24 month sales cycles kill startup economics
4. **Markets with $100M+ funded incumbents** — drone inspection, EV fleet, broad smart agriculture
5. **Construction industry** — certification timelines, extreme conservatism
6. **Consumer hardware** — thin margins, marketing costs, Amazon/Chinese competition
7. **Markets that appear empty but are actually dominated by 1-2 well-funded players** (cycles 021-040 finding: many "niche" industrial QC markets have $50M+ European companies already entrenched — SIKORA, Pixargus, GreCon, Precitec)
8. **Printing & packaging inspection** — NEW finding from Cycle 021: this domain is SATURATED with competitors (AVT, Isra Vision, Lake Image, PrintQualityAI, etc.). Reject immediately.
