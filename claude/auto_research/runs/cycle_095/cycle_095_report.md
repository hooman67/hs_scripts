## Step 3: Market Sizing — the binding gate

**Bottom-up seat count (all figures ASSUMED / order-of-magnitude — no clean industry census found):**
- US ag drainage/tile contractors: on the order of **1,000–3,000** active firms (concentrated in the
  Corn Belt — IA, IL, IN, OH, MN, ND). Many jobs are done by the **farmer themselves** (self-tilers,
  per Soil-Max Flow-X positioning), shrinking the paying-contractor pool further.
- Realistic vertical-SaaS ACV: **$1,000–3,000/yr** per contractor (small-business records tool).
- **SAM ≈ 2,000 firms × $2,000/yr ≈ $4M.** Even a generous 3,000 × $3,000 ≈ **$9M.**

**This is 10–25× below the $100M SAM floor.** Same failure mode as Round-2 Wireless TAB (~750 firms /
~$9–18M SAM): a **countable buyer universe is a red flag, not a green field.**

**Growth:** tile drainage acreage is expanding (climate/yield-driven) — a real tailwind — but a
rising tide on a ~2,000-firm buyer base can't lift SAM past a few million.
