## Finalist

### Acoustic Bird Species Monitoring for Wind Farm Compliance (Feasibility: 7/10)
- **Technology**: Commodity microphones ($5) + ML species classifier detects protected bird species near wind turbines; triggers curtailment to avoid kills and regulatory fines
- **Value prop**: Automated compliance monitoring replaces expensive human bird surveyors ($50-100K/year per wind farm) and reduces turbine curtailment waste by targeting only when protected species are present
- **Market**: $140B wind energy; every wind farm needs bird monitoring for permits; ESA violations = $50K/bird fine + project delays
- **Capital**: ~$250K to first revenue
- **Revenue model**: Hardware ($2K per monitoring node, 5-10 per farm) + SaaS ($500/month per farm)
- **Key strength**: Same acoustic ML pattern as CNC tool wear (Cycle 010) but for bird calls. Commodity MEMS mics + species classification ML. BirdNET (Cornell) provides open-source foundation model for bird species ID — can fine-tune for turbine noise environment.
- **Key advantage**: Current human surveys cost $50-100K/year and only cover dawn/dusk. Acoustic monitoring is 24/7 and 10x cheaper.
