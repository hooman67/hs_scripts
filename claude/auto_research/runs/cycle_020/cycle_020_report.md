## Finalist

### AI Lumber Grade Optimization System (Feasibility: 7/10)
- **Technology**: Camera + AI grades rough lumber (knots, splits, wane, grain) to maximize value recovery. Current manual grading misses 10-20% of upgrade opportunities — a board graded #2 that could be #1 = $100+ lost value per board.
- **Value prop**: AI grading + cutting optimization extracts maximum value from every log — the same lumber, 10-15% more revenue
- **Market**: $50B US lumber market; sawmill optimization segment $1B
- **Capital**: ~$350K to first revenue
- **Revenue model**: Hardware ($15K per grading station: cameras + compute + conveyor integration) + SaaS ($1K/month per mill for optimization + grade reporting)
- **Key strength**: Lumber grading is currently NHLA-certified human graders (expensive, subjective, inconsistent between shifts). AI is provably more consistent. Market is highly fragmented: 10K+ sawmills in US, most are small/medium. Existing solutions (USNR/Autolog) are $500K+ systems for large mills.
- **Key insight**: Same "democratize expensive system for small/mid mills" pattern as textile inspection (Cycle 012).
