# Execution Plan: AI Lumber Grade Optimization System

## Summary
- **Value prop**: AI grades lumber more consistently than humans and optimizes cutting to maximize board value
- **Hardware**: 4× machine vision cameras ($3K) + LED lighting ($500) + edge PC ($1K) + conveyor integration ($2K) = $6.5K BOM, sell at $15K
- **SaaS**: $1K/month per mill (grade analytics, optimization reports, market price integration)
- **Capital needed**: ~$350K (vision ML + lumber domain expertise + sawmill pilot, 8 months)
- **Target**: Small/medium sawmills (100-500 MBF/year) — 5000+ in US alone
- **Revenue Year 1**: 30 mills × $15K + 30 × $1K/mo × 8mo = $450K + $240K = **$690K**
- **Key advantage**: Human graders cost $25-35/hr, work 8-hour shifts, and are increasingly hard to find (labor shortage). AI works 24/7 with perfect consistency. At 10% value recovery improvement on $1M/year lumber output = $100K/year ROI per mill.
- **Foundation**: OpenCV + custom CNN trained on NHLA grade standards. Partner with 2-3 mills for training data.
