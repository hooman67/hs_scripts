# Execution Plan: AI Pallet Damage Detection Station

## Summary
- **Value prop**: Catch damaged pallets at the dock door before they cause product damage or injuries
- **Hardware**: 3× industrial cameras ($2K) + LED bar lights ($500) + edge PC ($500) + frame ($500) = $3.5K BOM, sell at $7K
- **SaaS**: $300/month per station
- **Capital needed**: ~$220K (vision engineer + integration + 5 pilot sites, 5 months)
- **Target customers**: Food/pharma 3PLs, grocery distribution centers
- **Revenue Year 1**: 100 stations × $7K + 100 × $300/mo × 8mo = $700K + $240K = **$940K**
- **Key moat**: Pallet defect training data + integration with WMS for automated rejection routing
- **Note**: Simplest product in portfolio — proven ML task (defect detection), clear buyer, obvious ROI
