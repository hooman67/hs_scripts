# Execution Plan: Food Processing Quality Prediction Platform

## Summary
- **Value prop**: Predict off-spec batches 30 minutes early — save $10-100K per batch
- **Architecture**: OPC-UA/Modbus connector → cloud ML pipeline → real-time dashboard with alerts
- **SaaS**: $5K/month per production line + $30K integration onboarding
- **Capital needed**: ~$320K (ML + backend + integrations, 8 months)
- **Target customers**: Dairy processors, beverage bottlers, meat processors with continuous lines
- **Revenue Year 1**: 10 lines × $5K/mo × 8mo + 5 × $30K onboarding = $400K + $150K = **$550K**
- **Key moat**: Per-customer ML models trained on their specific processes + food industry domain expertise
