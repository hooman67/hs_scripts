# Execution Plan: AI Grading Station for Recommerce/Returns

## Summary
- **Value prop**: Grade returned products 10x faster with AI-consistent quality scoring
- **Hardware**: Photo booth ($3K: lights + turntable + 3 cameras) + edge PC ($500) = $3.5K BOM, sell at $10K
- **SaaS**: $750/month per station + $0.05/item processed
- **Capital needed**: ~$350K (ML engineer + full-stack + hardware integration, 6 months)
- **Target customers**: Brand return centers (Nike, Lululemon, Patagonia), recommerce ops (ThredUp, Poshmark)
- **Revenue Year 1**: 50 stations × $10K + 50 × $750/mo × 10mo = $500K + $375K = **$875K**
- **Key moat**: Training data from diverse product categories + configurable grading rubrics per brand
