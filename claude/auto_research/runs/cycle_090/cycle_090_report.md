# Cycle 090 Report — Radon Domain (1/4): Measurement Report-Generation & Tester Workflow

**Date**: 2026-07-09. **Round**: 4. **Domain**: #10 Radon Measurement & Mitigation.
**Cycle focus**: the measurement side — the radon **tester** who runs continuous-radon-monitor
(CRM) cycles and files test reports. Candidate moat (from domain map): own the report/workflow
layer + accumulate an address/zip radon dataset.
**Kill-risk to clear FIRST**: CRM-device OEMs (Radalink, Sun Nuclear/SunRadon, AirThings,
Corentium/Radonova) ship the report/cloud/portal layer FREE, capping standalone price at ~$0
(the Round-3 "OEM ships it free" pattern).

1. **RadonReport** — auto-generate the NRPP/state-format test report from CRM data (charcoal/CRM),

**Step 1: 5 PROMOTED** (bias to action; kill on incumbent/moat gates below).

## Step 2: MANDATORY Incumbent / OEM Check — the decisive gate

**The OEM-ships-it-free pattern is CONFIRMED, hard.**

- **Radalink (VERIFIED, direct from radalink.com):** its CRM lease ("OnePrice Program") **bundles
  at no extra cost**: (1) "easy-to-read, customizable" **test-report generation**, e-mailed within
  ~1 hour of upload; (2) a **free customer portal** for "account info, testing documentation, hot
  leads, and QA records"; (3) **free mobile device-control software** on Windows/Android/iOS; and
  (4) **geographic radon aggregation** — "check the radon levels that our inspectors have found in
  your area." This single vendor already ships angles #1, #2, #4 AND #3 for $0 to its testers.
- **Sun Nuclear / SunRadon (ASSUMED — site 403'd; well-documented elsewhere):** the 1028 / Lucas
  CRM line ships with AnalyzeRadon / results software; report generation is part of the device
  ecosystem, not a paid third-party layer.
- **AirThings (VERIFIED, airthings.com/business):** pro/business line ships its own dashboard +
  reporting; Corentium Pro is sold WITH its reporting software.
- **Radonova (ASSUMED — 429'd):** lab-based model returns the analyzed report itself; the report
  IS the product they sell.

**Every device path a tester can buy already includes free report generation and a cloud portal.**
A standalone report/workflow SaaS competes against $0 bundled software AND must re-ingest each OEM's
proprietary data format. Competitive density **5/5**.

**Moat gate:** the address/zip radon "plant" (#3) is the only genuinely interesting asset — but
**Radalink already aggregates and displays it** from its installed CRM fleet, and no solo software
founder can out-accumulate the device OEMs who see every reading at the source. The data accrues to
whoever owns the device feed, NOT to the founder. Moat gate **FAILS**.

**Step 2: ALL REJECTED.**
