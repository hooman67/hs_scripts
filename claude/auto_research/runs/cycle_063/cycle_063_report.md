## TL;DR (honest outcome)

**GRADUATES: 1 (conditional, ~5.0/10) — the best in this domain block, but still soft.**
GSHP borefield drilling is the one water-adjacent drilling trade with (a) a **real growth
tailwind** (electrification + IRA 30% tax credit; installs growing ~double-digit %/yr, VERIFIED
~10%/yr historical + policy acceleration) and (b) **subsurface/thermal data that is NOT state-
filed** — Thermal Response Test (TRT) results, formation thermal conductivity, grout thermal
performance, and as-built borefield geometry. A loop-drilling/design contractor *does* uniquely
accumulate this, and no state re-publishes it. That clears the two gates that killed 061/062.

**BUT it graduates only conditionally (~5.0)** because: (1) the reachable buyer universe is
SMALL — GSHP-loop drilling is a specialized subset of the ~8,085 groundwater firms plus some HVAC-
geo installers, likely low thousands, so **SAM clearing $100M is a stretch** (needs high ACV
via design-tooling, not just $/mo logging); (2) design incumbents exist — the industry-standard
**GLD (Ground Loop Design) / GLHEPRO / Gaia Geothermal** borefield-design tools already model
loops from TRT inputs; (3) the value data (TRT/conductivity) is partly generated by a
$5–15K TRT rig — a mild hardware/analog element on the edge of founder-fit. It is a *legitimate*
own-data flywheel, but on a small trade against existing design software.

## Step 2 — Incumbent/OEM + Moat gate

- **Growth (VERIFIED, Wikipedia GSHP + policy knowledge):** ~1M+ units installed worldwide,
  historically **~10%/yr growth**; **~80,000 US installs/yr**; IRA §25D gives a **30% federal
  tax credit** for residential geothermal through 2032 (domain knowledge, ASSUMED at the
  precision). Vertical closed-loop systems require professional borehole drilling (e.g. 3 bores
  80–110 m for a 10 kW house, VERIFIED). This is the growth tailwind the water-well core lacked.
- **Design incumbents:** GLD (Ground Loop Design, Gaia Geothermal), GLHEPRO (Oklahoma State),
  Ground Loop Design by Celsia — established borefield-sizing tools that model from TRT/
  conductivity inputs (ASSUMED, domain knowledge). They are **design calculators, NOT a growing
  proprietary regional thermal-data plant** — they don't accumulate every contractor's local
  TRT results into a compounding map. That gap is the wedge.
- **Moat gate on #1 — PASS (conditional).** TRT results + measured formation thermal
  conductivity + as-built loop performance are **not state-filed and not consultant-owned** (the
  loop contractor generates and keeps them). Density on a *data-plant* product is low (1–2); the
  existing tools are non-accumulating calculators. This is the one genuine founder-accruing
  flywheel in the whole domain block. → **Promote to Step 3.**
- #2–#4 collapse into #1; #6 grout advice is free from grout suppliers; #7 is horizontal FSM;
  #8/#10 have instant-reject or ERP-owned buyers. REJECT.
