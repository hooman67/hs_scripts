## Step 2: Competitive Analysis + ⭐ Incumbent Check + Moat Gate

### Idea 1 — Legionella / WMP service-provider app
- **Incumbent check**: The WMP-software space is already populated: **LAMPS (HC Info / Environmental Safety Technologies)** — cloud WMP builder with editable policies, sampling docs, analytics; **Special Pathogens Technology** — auto system diagrams + Legionella control mapping; **IWC Innovations, AATLS, iFactory**. Critically, the big **water-treatment chemical majors — Nalco Water (Ecolab), ChemTreat, ChemAqua, Veolia, LiquiTech** — run the actual service and provide their customers proprietary compliance/reporting portals *bundled with the chemical program* (the razor-and-blades pattern; the report is free to sell the chemistry). [VERIFIED for LAMPS/SPL/IWC/AATLS; ASSUMED for the chemical majors' bundled portals based on the industrial-water-treatment norm]
- **Moat gate**: The lab/analytical result (the valuable compounding data) is generated by the accredited lab and the chemical-treatment provider, and the WMP-of-record software (LAMPS) already aggregates it building-by-building. A solo software founder cannot uniquely accumulate the sampling/result data — the treatment provider and the lab own the feed, and LAMPS owns the plan-of-record. This is the round-3 "OEM/service-provider owns the data" pattern. FAIL. Density 4/5.
- **Status**: REJECTED.

### Idea 2 — Certified irrigation-audit app
- **Incumbent check**: **AuditFlow** is already the named incumbent — computes distribution uniformity, generates CLCA-compliant PDFs, tracks system health scores, estimates water savings; free trial; targets landscape contractors, HOAs, water agencies. [VERIFIED] Generic form apps (Fulcrum, iAuditor) also cover it.
- **Moat gate + market**: (a) The seat is already taken by AuditFlow. (b) The audit is a *one-shot* event tied to a rebate, not a recurring annual mandate for most sites → weak renewal flywheel; the per-site history rarely compounds because audits aren't repeated frequently. (c) Buyer/WTP is weak — the property owner commissions it once to claim a rebate. (d) SAM questionable: certified auditors are a modest population and audits are episodic. FAIL on moat (data doesn't compound) + occupied seat + soft SAM.
- **Status**: REJECTED.

### Idea 3 — Cooling-tower compliance micro-tool
- **Incumbent check**: Same water-treatment-provider ownership as Legionella (Nalco/ChemTreat run tower programs + reporting); jurisdiction registration is filed to the health dept (e.g., NYC portal). Also very narrow (only jurisdictions with tower mandates).
- **Moat gate + buyer**: Data owned by the treatment provider; registration filed to a government portal (buyer/mandate = government). FAIL + micro-SAM.
- **Status**: REJECTED.

### Competitive Density: Legionella 4/5, irrigation 4/5, cooling-tower 4/5.
