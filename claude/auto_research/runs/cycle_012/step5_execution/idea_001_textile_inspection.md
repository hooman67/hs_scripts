- **SaaS**: $400/month (model updates, cloud analytics, defect reporting)
- **Capital needed**: ~$280K (vision ML engineer + hardware integration + textile partnerships, 7 months)
- **Target customers**: Small/medium textile mills (100-500 employees) in India, Bangladesh, Turkey, Vietnam, Mexico
- **Revenue Year 1**: 100 systems × $8K + 100 × $400/mo × 8mo = $800K + $320K = **$1.12M**
- **Key moat**: Defect classification ML trained on diverse fabric types + multi-language support for global market
