**Verdict: CONDITIONAL GO (6.30)**

---

## Key Strengths

1. **Clear market gap**: No productized AI inspection exists at $5K-$10K for small mills. The 10-20x price gap vs. incumbents is real and verified.

2. **Proven technology, novel application of cost reduction**: This is not an invention problem -- it is an engineering + distribution problem. Machine vision for textiles works. The breakthrough is doing it at 1/20th the cost with commodity hardware + modern AI.

3. **Large TAM with strong tailwinds**: Rising labor costs in Bangladesh (+56% in 2023), tightening buyer quality requirements, government incentives (India PLI scheme), and AI compute cost reductions all converge.

4. **Favorable unit economics**: 65-75% gross margin on hardware; SaaS adds recurring revenue with near-100% gross margin. At 100 units/year + SaaS, business is profitable.

5. **Capital efficiency**: $280K to first revenue is unusually low for a hardware startup. No custom silicon, no regulatory approval, no multi-year R&D cycle.

---

## Key Weaknesses

1. **Founder-market fit gap (CRITICAL)**: An AI/ML infrastructure engineer lacks three essential capabilities: hardware design, textile domain expertise, and emerging market sales. This venture requires a founding team, not a solo founder. Without a co-founder who has textile industry experience AND local market access, execution risk is very high.

2. **Hardware business scaling challenges**: Each unit requires manufacturing, shipping, installation, and support. Unlike pure SaaS, cannot grow 10x without proportional operations investment. Inventory risk, warranty cost, and field service are real.

3. **Payment collection risk**: Small mills in India and Bangladesh are notorious for delayed payments (60-120 day cycles common). Need to design business model that mitigates this (prepaid, lease with remote disable, or work through channel partners who absorb credit risk).

4. **Moat is time-limited**: Chinese manufacturers are rapidly improving AI capabilities. A well-funded Shenzhen company could copy the hardware in 12-18 months. Data moat takes 2-3 years to become meaningful. Must move FAST to establish market position.

5. **Customer education burden**: Small mill owners in developing markets may not understand or trust AI. Sales cycle requires in-person demos, free pilots, and local language communication. CAC could be higher than projected if education takes longer.
