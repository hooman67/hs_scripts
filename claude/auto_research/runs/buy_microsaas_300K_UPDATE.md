## 2. WHAT THE $300K CEILING UNLOCKS (revised Buy Box)

The ceiling doubling ($150K → $300K) changes the math in one decisive way: **it moves the
delegation threshold from "unreachable" to "reachable."**

| Dimension | OLD ($150K ceiling) | NEW ($300K ceiling) |
|---|---|---|
| **Price band** | $75-160K (buy $80-120K) | **$150-300K (buy $180-260K)** |
| **Net/profit band buyable at fair 4-4.5x** | ~$1.5-2.5K/mo | **~$4.2-6.25K/mo** (see math) |
| **Delegation** | Impossible — VA+dev eats 100% of profit; Hooman IS the operator | **PENCILS** — $4-6K/mo assets clear the ~$4K/mo threshold; VA+dev leaves ~$1.5-4K/mo |
| **Operator posture** | "Hooman is always the operator" (8-12 hr/wk indefinitely) | **Delegable to part-time (~8-12 hr/wk) via VA+contractor**, not always sole operator |

**The core math — what monthly net $300K buys at a fair 4-4.5x SDE:**
- **$300K ÷ 4.5x = ~$66.7K/yr SDE = ~$5,555/mo net** (conservative multiple).
- **$300K ÷ 4.0x = ~$75K/yr SDE = ~$6,250/mo net** (aggressive multiple).
- Leaving a ~$15-25K reserve (buy at ~$275K): **~$5,090-5,730/mo net.**
- **Range he can buy: ~$4.2-6.25K/mo net** (the low end if he pays up for a cleaner/growing asset).

**Does that clear the delegation threshold? YES.** The playbook's rule is **delegation (VA+dev)
only pencils above ~$4K/mo net.** A $300K budget buys **$4.2-6.25K/mo**, which is **at or above**
that line. Concretely at ~$5.5K/mo net: after a ~$1.5-2.5K/mo VA+contractor-dev spend, **~$3-4K/mo
net remains** while owner-load drops toward the delegable 8-12 hr/wk — i.e. it becomes a
**~$3-4K/mo part-time-managed asset**, not a full-time job that nets ~$0 after his time. This is the
single most important unlock: **the old "Hooman is always the operator" conclusion no longer holds
at the top of the new band.**

**What does NOT change:**
- **Supply is still the binding constraint.** Doubling the ceiling widens the *net band* but the
  clean, sticky, fairly-priced **vertical-B2B SoR at $4-6K/mo still isn't on the shelf today.** The
  two deep-dived assets are (A) 5-6x over even $300K, and (B) affordable but Amazon-dependent with a
  decaying moat. **The perfectly-clean $4-6K/mo vertical SoR is the sibling that isn't listed.**
- **Fair multiple discipline (4-4.5x, zero growth credit)**, the 8 deal-killers, and the "no live
  Stripe = walk" gate all still apply verbatim.
- **AVOID SBA / personal-guarantee financing** — it is the ONLY way to reach Listing A and it
  breaks the capped-downside thesis. The $300K unlock is meaningful **only as all-cash headroom.**

## 3. BOTTOM LINE — is buy-SaaS now a real path to $5-30K/mo?

**Honest answer: NO to $5-30K/mo from one buy; YES to a real, delegable ~$3-6K/mo — a genuine
improvement over the $150K verdict.**

- **$5-30K/mo passive income from a single ~$300K acquisition: still not realistic.** To *net*
  $5-30K/mo after delegation you'd need to *buy* ~$6.5-40K/mo gross, i.e. **~$350K to $2M+** at a
  fair multiple. $300K buys the **bottom edge (~$5.5K/mo gross)** of that — and after delegation
  the *take-home* is ~$3-4K/mo. Reaching $5-30K/mo take-home requires either a bigger check than
  $300K, a roll-up of several assets over time, or customer-acquisition growth (**not Hooman's
  edge** — do not pay or plan for it).
- **What DID change (the real win of the $300K ceiling)**: The ceiling now reaches the level where
  **delegation finally pencils.** At $150K he was buying a ~$1.5-2.5K/mo *job* that netted ~$0 after
  his own time. At $300K he can buy a **~$4.2-6.25K/mo asset that survives paying a VA+dev**, drop to
  **~8-12 hr/wk**, and keep **~$3-4K/mo of genuinely part-time income** plus resale optionality.
  That flips the prior "always the operator / ~$0 economic profit" conclusion into a plausible
  **part-time-managed cash-flowing asset.**
- **On the two specific listings**: **Listing A (Building-Ops) = PASS** — it is the ideal shape and
  proof the model works, but it is **still 5-6x over $300K** and only reachable via a
  thesis-breaking SBA PG. **Listing B (Amazon-Sourcing) = CONDITIONAL** — the first thing that is
  both **affordable AND delegable AND a real fit for Hooman's data-eng value-creation edge**, but it
  **breaks two Buy Box rules** (Amazon single-platform dependency; decaying-scraper moat) and the
  cheap 2x multiple flags possible decline. **Worth a seller call and Gate-2 diligence; walk fast on
  a down MRR trend or a pure ad-fed/scraper moat.**
- **Recommendation**: **Keep the $0 dry-run running with the revised Buy Box** ($150-300K price,
  $4-6K/mo net, delegation-affordable). **Run Gate-2 on Listing B** (pull the 24-mo Stripe trend
  and the paid-ads mix first — those two kill or keep it). **Do NOT chase Listing A via SBA.** The
  $300K ceiling makes buy-SaaS a **credible path to a delegable ~$3-6K/mo part-time asset** — a
  real improvement — but **not, from one deal at this budget, the $5-30K/mo passive machine.** Buy
  only when a clean asset in the new band clears every gate; otherwise keep the capital.
