## Finalist

### Automated Fire Sprinkler Inspection Documentation (Feasibility: 7/10)
- **Technology**: Tablet app + AI camera identifies sprinkler head types, checks for obstruction/corrosion/paint coverage, auto-generates NFPA 25 compliant inspection reports
- **Value prop**: Cut fire sprinkler inspection time 50% and eliminate paper-based documentation errors
- **Market**: $10B fire protection services; 1M+ commercial buildings need annual sprinkler inspection; 15K+ fire protection companies in US
- **Capital**: ~$200K to first revenue
- **Revenue model**: SaaS ($200-500/month per inspector) — pure software, no hardware
- **Key strength**: PURE SOFTWARE on existing iPads/tablets. No hardware to build. Every fire protection company does inspections — it's their bread and butter. Current tools: paper forms or basic apps without AI. AI that recognizes sprinkler types and auto-flags deficiencies (paint, corrosion, obstruction distance) would save hours per building.
