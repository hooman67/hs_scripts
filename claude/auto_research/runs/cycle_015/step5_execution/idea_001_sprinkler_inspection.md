# Execution Plan: AI Fire Sprinkler Inspection App

## Summary
- **Value prop**: AI-powered inspection cuts documentation time 50% and catches deficiencies human eyes miss
- **Platform**: iPad/Android tablet app (camera + AI + NFPA 25 knowledge base)
- **Capital needed**: ~$200K (iOS/Android dev + ML + fire protection domain, 6 months)
- **Target**: 15K+ fire protection companies doing annual inspections
- **Revenue**: SaaS $300/month per inspector; 500 inspectors Y1 = **$1.8M ARR**
- **Key features**: (1) Photo → auto-identify sprinkler head type/orientation, (2) AI flags paint/corrosion/obstruction/wrong temperature rating, (3) Auto-generate NFPA 25 report with photos, (4) Integration with inspection scheduling + customer portal
- **Pure software play**: Zero hardware cost. Inspectors already carry iPads.
- **Go-to-market**: Partner with NFPA for credibility. Demo at NFSA annual convention. Offer free trial month.
