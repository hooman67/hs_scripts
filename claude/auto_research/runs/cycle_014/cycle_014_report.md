## Finalist

### AI Weld Quality Monitoring from Electrical Signatures (Feasibility: 7/10)
- **Technology**: Monitor welding machine current/voltage waveforms with commodity current sensor ($10) + ML to detect defective welds in real-time WITHOUT post-weld inspection
- **Value prop**: Catch bad welds AS THEY HAPPEN — no waiting for X-ray or ultrasonic testing. 90%+ of weld defects produce detectable electrical signature anomalies.
- **Market**: $20B welding market; $3B weld inspection/testing
- **Capital**: ~$250K to first revenue
- **Revenue model**: Sensor kit ($500/welding station) + SaaS ($150/month per station)
- **Key strength**: Non-invasive (clamp-on current sensor), no modification to welding process, works on any MIG/TIG machine, replaces $50K+ inspection equipment and personnel time
- **Key insight**: Xiris, HKS, and some academic groups have published on this but NO affordable product exists for small fab shops. The waveform data is there — the AI interpretation layer is missing from affordable tools.
