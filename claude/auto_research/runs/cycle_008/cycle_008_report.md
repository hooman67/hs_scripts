## Finalist (Step 5 Graduate)

### AI-Powered Thermal Inspection Tool for Trade Professionals (Feasibility: 7/10)
- **Technology**: Commodity thermal sensor (FLIR Lepton 3.5, $180) + on-device AI anomaly detection
- **Value prop**: Point, scan, know — AI identifies electrical faults and moisture issues at 1/5th cost of pro thermal cameras
- **Market**: $5B inspection tools, 1.2M US electricians + 500K home inspectors
- **Capital**: ~$525K to first revenue
- **Key strength**: FLIR's $300-500 products have NO AI; $2K+ products are overkill for trades; the $400 AI-enhanced gap is wide open
- **Revenue model**: Hardware ($399-499) + pro SaaS subscription ($30/month)
