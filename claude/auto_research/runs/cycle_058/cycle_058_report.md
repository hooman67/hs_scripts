## Why this angle

The strongest non-deferrable demand in septic is the **advanced-system operating permit**: EPA notes most states mandate "annual operation, maintenance, monitoring and reporting as a requirement of the homeowner's septic permit" for ATUs [VERIFIED]. This is recurring (unlike a one-time transfer inspection) — the best shape for a data flywheel. Test whether the monitoring data accrues to the founder or to the ATU/panel OEM.

## Step 2: Competitive Analysis + ⭐ Incumbent/OEM Check + ⭐ Moat Gate

### ⭐ OEM check — the decisive kill (this is the "OEM ships it free / owns the data feed" pattern)
The ATU market is dominated by manufacturers whose **control panels already stream telemetry to the OEM's own cloud**, and who sell/mandate that cloud to their service-provider network:
- **Orenco VeriComm (VCOM)** [VERIFIED]: "web-based telemetry control panels enabling operators to remotely monitor and control each individual site's wastewater systems." The panel, the data pipe, and the provider dashboard are all Orenco's. Service providers operate *within* VeriComm.
- **RMSYS Inc** [VERIFIED]: wastewater/aerobic telemetry + alarm systems for service providers.
- **Septilink** [VERIFIED]: IoT septic monitoring (homeowner-oriented — wrong buyer).
- Broader ATU OEMs (Orenco, Clarus/Zoeller, Norweco, Delta Whitewater/regional franchises) each run their own O&M/monitoring stack tied to their equipment.

**The monitoring data is generated by, and lands in, the OEM's control-panel cloud.** Any founder analytics layer sits on the OEM's data feed — the exact Round-3 "moat accrues to whoever owns the device feed" failure (cf. radon CRM OEMs, XRF guns). A third-party analytics SaaS is one firmware/dashboard release away from being free.

### ⭐ Moat Gate — FAIL
- The compounding effluent/uptime time-series accrues to the **panel OEM** (Orenco/RMSYS), not the founder's platform.
- The compliance *report* accrues to the **county/state permit portal** (OnlineRME and equivalents accept the annual O&M report — cycle 056/060).
- The operator cannot uniquely accumulate the data; it originates in hardware the founder doesn't own and terminates in a portal the founder doesn't own. **Two-sided leak.**

### Provider-side compliance-only wedge (no telemetry)
Even stripped to "just track permit due-dates + file the annual report," this collides head-on with the cycle-056 incumbents (National Onsite, Summit, ServiceCore, SepticMind all do scheduling + reporting) and adds nothing they don't already do.

**Competitive Density: 5/5** (OEM-owned telemetry + FSM incumbents own the workflow). **Moat Gate: FAIL. REJECT at Step 2.**
