# Microns.io — Live Micro-SaaS Listings (captured 2026-07-02)

**Marketplace**: https://microns.io/ (curated micro-SaaS, typically on-budget for a $25–150K buyer)

**Buy Box reminder**: vertical B2B system-of-record / compliance / workflow SaaS in an unglamorous
trade; price $75–160K (sweet $80–120K); net $2–5K/mo (realistic $1.5–2.5K/mo); ≥18–24mo history;
B2B recurring; churn flattens; <20% concentration; owner <10 hrs/wk. REJECT consumer apps,
SEO/content, extensions, thin AI wrappers, single-platform add-ons.

## Access notes

Microns exposes an unusually **large amount publicly** on each detail page. The homepage cards show
title + niche + asking price + annual revenue + multiple. The individual
`/startup-listings/{slug}` detail pages additionally show (public, no login): **profit/net income,
profit margin, MRR, customer count, launch year, full tech stack, churn, business model, pricing
tiers, expense breakdown**. **Gated behind a free account/login**: founder contact info, the live
startup website URL, the traffic-metrics chart, the revenue-metrics chart, and the "how it works"
video. So the paper-screen can be done fully public; only source-verification contact + traffic/rev
charts require signup. NOTE: many listings on the site are marked **"Already Sold"** — those are kept
as historical examples, not buyable. Only a subset are live.

## Verdict for Hooman

Only **one** current Microns listing fits the Buy Box: the **Healthcare Exercise Management SaaS
($120K, ~$1.5K/mo net, 3.8x, B2B, 2022, real stack)**. It is a legitimate paper-screen pass and worth
a closer look, but two things must be source-verified before an LOI: (1) **customer concentration** —
35 subscribers is thin, so a single clinic/chain could blow the <20% rule; and (2) the **"15% churn"**
definition plus a self-built cohort curve (a PT-exercise tool competes with Physitrack/Medbridge, so
confirm the switching-cost moat is real). Everything else on the marketplace right now is either a
consumer/AI-wrapper/content/ecommerce reject or already sold. Microns' inventory skews small and
consumer-AI; expect to check it periodically rather than as a primary channel.

**Gated for buyer account**: founder contact, live site URL, and the traffic/revenue charts — i.e.
you can fully paper-screen public, but must sign up (free) to reach the seller and to see the
traffic/revenue trend charts; true diligence still requires read-only Stripe+bank off-platform.
