# Micro-SaaS Listings — Tiny Acquisitions + Latona's + MotionInvest

**Sourced**: 2026-07-02 by Claude (Opus 4.8) for Hooman. **Buy Box**: vertical B2B system-of-record /
compliance / workflow SaaS, price $75–160K (sweet $80–120K), net $2–5K/mo, ≥18–24mo, B2B recurring,
churn flattens, <20% concentration, owner <10 hrs/wk. REJECT consumer/SEO-content/Chrome-ext/thin-AI/
single-platform add-ons.

## Headline verdict (honest)

**None of these three marketplaces produced a single in-Buy-Box listing.** All three are structurally
mismatched with Hooman's mandate, for different reasons:

- **Tiny Acquisitions (tinyacq.com)** — the domain given in the task **does not resolve** (DNS
  `ENOTFOUND` on tinyacq.com, tinyacq.io, tinyacquisitions.com). Tiny Acquisitions was an early
  sub-$100K micro-acquisition marketplace that appears defunct / rebranded; it no longer serves live
  inventory at that URL. The live equivalents in that price band today are **Acquire.com, Little Exits
  (littleexits.com), and Microns.io** — but their actual listings are **gated behind buyer login/
  verification** (Acquire `/marketplace` and `/business-for-sale/saas` returned 404 to anonymous fetch;
  Little Exits shows "750+ verified projects" only after signup). **Zero public listing data captured.**
- **MotionInvest** — live and public, but it is a **content-site / YouTube / AdSense marketplace, NOT
  a SaaS marketplace.** Every visible listing is an ad-monetized website or YouTube channel priced
  $750–$5,600. These are the exact archetype the Buy Box REJECTS (SEO/content sites). No B2B SaaS at all.
- **Latona's** — live and public, a legitimate brokerage, but its **entire visible SaaS inventory is
  far above budget** (cheapest SaaS = $2.5M; cheapest listing of any kind = $240K). Latona's operates
  in the $250K–$13M band. Nothing within $75–160K exists here.

Net: for Hooman's $75–160K vertical-B2B Buy Box, **the correct venue is a gated buyer account on
Acquire.com / Microns.io / Little Exits / Flippa / Empire Flippers** (per playbook Days 1–7), not these
three public pages. The public data below is what genuinely exists.
