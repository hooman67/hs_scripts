**Filters used**: `?monetization=SaaS`, `?monetization=Subscription`, `sort=price-asc`, and individual listing pages.

## Access reality (what is public vs gated)
Empire Flippers shows a LOT publicly on the listing card + listing page: niche, monetization type,
asking price, monthly revenue, monthly net profit, multiple, launch date/age, tech stack, owner
hours/week, subscriber count, churn, revenue-model split, and the risk/opportunity narrative. That is
unusually generous vs Acquire/Flippa.

**Gated behind a (free) buyer account / verification**: the actual domain URL, the detailed
earnings + traffic proof charts, Semrush/backlink analytics, the seller Q&A/interview, and social
links. For real diligence you still need read-only Stripe + bank three-way reconcile (Gate 2) — never
the EF proof screenshots alone.

## CRITICAL multiple note (do not misread)
**EF quotes the multiple as MONTHLY** (e.g. "32x" = 32x monthly profit ≈ **2.7x annual**). The playbook
targets 4-4.5x ANNUAL. So EF's "32x monthly" ≈ 2.7x annual = actually CHEAP relative to the playbook's
fair-value band — but EF listings this cheap-on-annual are typically that way because of decline,
high churn, or short history. Verify why before celebrating. (35x monthly ≈ 2.9x annual; 46x ≈ 3.8x;
41x ≈ 3.4x; 25x ≈ 2.1x.)
