## Step 2 — Competitive density + MANDATORY Incumbent/OEM + Moat gates

### 1. WDO/termite (NPMA-33) inspection report + per-property history — **Density 4, REJECT**
- **Incumbent check (VERIFIED):** **PestPac (WorkWave)** ships a dedicated "Termite Inspection Software" module with custom forms, mobile field capture, and chemical tracking. **FieldRoutes, Briostack, GorillaDesk, ServSuite** all serve pest control with inspection/graphing. NPMA-33 report generation and the termite graph are standard modules.
- **Moat gate FAIL:** the per-property inspection & treatment history is the pest-control SoR's core record. A standalone WDO tool is an absorbable feature; the flywheel accrues to the ERP the PCO runs their business on. Reject (the cycle-079 / PestDoc pattern again).

### 2. Termite-warranty / re-treat scheduling + per-structure history — **Density 4, REJECT**
- Warranty and renewal scheduling on the structure's treatment history is exactly what PestPac/FieldRoutes recurring-service modules do. No founder-owned data asset. Reject.

### 3. Tree-preservation-ordinance compliance (protected-tree permits/mitigation) — **Density 2, but REJECT on buyer + moat**
- **Buyer check FAIL (decisive):** protected-tree permitting, mitigation-fee calculation, and the protected-tree register are administered by **municipalities via e-permit portals** (the AHJ owns the record). Selling into that = the municipal-procurement instant-reject. The private-side users (developers, landscape architects) touch it once per project — no recurring per-asset flywheel accrues to a private operator's platform. Reject on buyer + moat.

### 4. Construction tree-protection monitoring documentation — **Density 2-3, REJECT on moat + market**
- **Incumbent check:** the consulting arborist who does construction monitoring writes geotagged-photo compliance letters; PlanIT Geo TreePlotter and SingleOps can already hold the site tree inventory + work log (VERIFIED they serve private tree care). Civica ArborSafe (AU) shows the "ArborSite" multi-site assessment product is exactly this (VERIFIED).
- **Moat gate FAIL:** project-scoped documentation (weeks–months per job) does not compound into a durable per-asset history that accrues to the founder; the tree inventory that WOULD compound is already the SoRs' feature. Small, project-bound buyer universe. Reject.

### 5. Per-property landscape/irrigation as-built + service-history plant — **Density 4, REJECT**
- **Incumbent check:** LMN (Granum), Arborgold, Aspire (ServiceTitan-owned), Jobber, Service Autopilot all hold per-property landscape service history + notes; irrigation as-builts sit in the same FSM record. This is a mature landscape-FSM category (Aspire alone is a large funded player under ServiceTitan). Density 4-5. The as-built "plant" doesn't uniquely accrue to a new entrant. Reject.
