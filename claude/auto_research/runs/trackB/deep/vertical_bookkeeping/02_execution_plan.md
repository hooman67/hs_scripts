**Owner:** Hooman Shariati · **Date:** 2026-07-01 · **Status:** conditional-go, sales-gated
**Companion:** `01_research_validation.md` (verdict: PASS-as-pitched; only one narrow form survives)

> **Read this first.** The research says a from-scratch bookkeeping firm is a PASS for Hooman
> as a *secondary* stream, for two reasons the plan cannot engineer away: (1) it needs his
> scarce personal SALES time — his known weakness, already claimed by CutLog; (2) the exact
> billable task is being eaten by incumbent AI (Pilot, QuickBooks Live). Bench raised $100M+
> and died. **Therefore this plan is deliberately front-loaded with a cheap $0–2K, ~40-hour
> "kill gate" that runs in parallel with CutLog and is designed to say NO fast.** Do not spend
> real money, hire anyone, or take a paying client until Gate 1 passes. The realistic upside if
> it fully works is **~$4.5–8K/mo net at 15 clients after a 12–24-month sales ramp** — modest,
> and only worth it if the Gate-1 validation shows selling is *easy* in the chosen niche.

## 2. How It Coexists With CutLog (the real constraint)

CutLog is the priority and already owns Hooman's builder hours. This idea must **never** compete
for the same deep-work block. Rules:

1. **CutLog first.** If in any week CutLog needs the hours, bookkeeping pauses. No exceptions
   during the Gate-1 validation phase.
2. **This is a "sell + delegate" business, not a "build" business** — its time cost is *sales &
   review*, which is different muscle from CutLog's product work but competes for the same
   scarce total hours. Budget **max 5–7 hrs/wk** to it during validation, rising to ~8–10 hrs/wk
   only *after* it earns revenue and a delegate is doing the labor.
3. **Distribution synergy, not distraction:** the same indie-SaaS/CutLog network is the lead
   source for both. Selling finance-ops to CutLog-adjacent founders can be done in the same
   conversations. Exploit that overlap; do not build a separate sales motion.
4. **Capital+delegation over founder-focus:** the moment it has ≥5 clients, push ALL labor to a
   contractor and Hooman's role shrinks to sales + weekly QA review (~4–5 hrs/wk). If it can't
   get to delegated-QA-only by ~month 9, it is failing the "secondary/part-time" test — kill or
   sell it.
5. **Hard stop:** if serving clients ever pulls Hooman below the CutLog hours he needs, the
   bookkeeping clients get transitioned to the delegate or handed off — CutLog wins.

## 3. Cheapest-Possible Validation BEFORE Any Real Money (the kill gate)

**Total spend to reach the first go/no-go: $0–200. Total time: ~30–40 hrs over 4–5 weeks.**
This exists because the research's #1 kill-risk is "can Hooman actually sell this?" Answer that
for ~$0 before touching QuickBooks certs, insurance, or contractors.

- **Offer test, not product test.** Sell the *promise* to 15–25 indie-SaaS founders in his
  network / Indie Hackers / relevant Slacks / X before doing any bookkeeping. Pitch: "I'm
  starting a finance-ops service for SaaS founders — clean books + Stripe reconciliation +
  board-ready MRR/ARR + rev-rec — $900/mo. Would you want this?"
- **Success = commitment, not compliments.** The bar is **≥3 founders who say "yes, I'd pay
  $800+/mo" and 1 who will start a paid pilot within 30 days.** Verbal "sounds great" counts as NO.
- Cost: a landing page (Carrd/free), calendar link, ~20 outreach conversations. $0–100.

## 5. Months 4–12 — Path to Target (part-time, delegated)

Only if Gate 2 passes. The whole game now is: **sell steadily, keep Hooman out of the labor.**

| Month | Clients (retained) | Hooman's role | Delegate | Gross/mo | Net/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 (end P-B) | 2–3 | full delivery + sales | trialing | ~$2.4K | ~$1–1.5K |
| 6 | 5–7 | sales + weekly QA only | 1 contractor (labor) | ~$5–6K | ~$2.5–3.5K |
| 9 | 8–11 | sales + QA (~4–5 hr/wk) | 1 contractor scaling | ~$7.5–10K | ~$4–6K |
| 12 | 12–16 | sales + QA + a senior lead for overflow | 1–2 contractors | ~$11–14K | **~$4.5–8K** |

- **Hiring/delegation plan:** contractors only, paid from client cash, never W-2 employees.
  One offshore bookkeeper carries ~15–40 clients depending on complexity (research §4, flagged
  UNVERIFIED — confirm in Phase B by measuring real hours). Add a second/senior contractor for
  QA-assist only when >12 clients so Hooman's review load stays ≤5 hrs/wk.
- **Sales:** ~1 net-new retained client/month via network + referrals + niche communities.
  This is the fragile assumption. Referrals from happy SaaS founders are the cheapest channel;
  paid ads are a last resort (bad CAC in this space).
- **Churn:** assume ~2–3%/mo baseline (clients get acquired, go in-house, or leave over one bad
  close). Selling ~1/mo net *includes* out-running churn.
- **Part-time guarantee:** if at any point Hooman's weekly hours exceed ~10 or CutLog suffers,
  the response is *more delegation / slower sales / raise prices*, NOT more of Hooman's time.

## 6. Go / No-Go Decision Gates

- **GATE 1 (end Week 4) — the sale.** GO only if **≥3 founders say they'd pay $800+/mo AND ≥1
  commits to a paid pilot within 30 days**, gathered without begging. If not → **KILL. Total
  cost <$200, ~25 hrs.** This is the whole point of the plan: fail cheap on Hooman's weakness.
- **GATE 2 (end Week 13) — the unit + delegation.** GO only if: (a) client accepts the monthly
  package and renews; (b) per-client delivery ≤6 hrs *and* successfully handed to a contractor
  under QA; (c) net margin per client ≥40%; (d) at least 2–3 paying clients; (e) sales felt
  *repeatable*, not soul-crushing. If any fails → freeze at current clients, do not scale, or exit.
- **GATE 3 (month 9) — the part-time test.** GO to keep scaling only if Hooman's personal time
  is ≤5 hrs/wk (labor fully delegated, QA-only) AND net ≥$4K/mo AND CutLog unharmed. If Hooman is
  still doing labor or sales is stalling → this is not a viable secondary stream; **hand clients to
  the delegate as a lifestyle micro-book, or sell the small book, and return focus to CutLog.**

## 8. Revenue Model + Realistic Ramp

- **Model:** monthly recurring subscription ($800–1,500/mo) + one-time setup/catch-up
  ($750–1,500) + optional advisory upsell (board-deck / fundraise-prep, $500–2K project).
- **Ramp curve (retained clients):** 1 (mo 2) → 2–3 (mo 3) → 5–7 (mo 6) → 8–11 (mo 9) →
  12–16 (mo 12). ~1 net-new/mo after out-running churn.
- **Steady-state (mo 12–18, ~15 clients @ ~$900):** ~$11–14K/mo gross → **~$4.5–8K/mo net**
  (offshore-delegated, CAC tapered). This is the honest ceiling for a part-time operation; the
  pitch's "$10–15K/mo" is best-case (20+ retained + cheap offshore + zero CAC), not expected.

## 9. One-Paragraph Recommendation

Run **only Phase A** now, in the seams around CutLog, for <$200 and ~25 hours. It directly tests
the single thing that kills this idea for Hooman — whether *he* can sell finance-ops to SaaS
founders without a grind. If Gate 1 fails (the likely outcome given his profile and the AI-
compression / commoditization headwinds), he has lost almost nothing and confirmed CutLog is the
right sole focus. If Gate 1 surprisingly passes — meaning selling to his own indie-SaaS network is
*easy* — then and only then invest the weekend cert + first client, and scale strictly via
delegation so it stays a ≤5-hr/wk, capital-light, ~$4.5–8K/mo side stream that never steals CutLog's
hours. Do **not** start with the cert, insurance, or a contractor; start with the sale.
