# Track B — Boring / Micro Software in Unglamorous Low-Growth Niches

**Shape:** Micro-SaaS, vertical software, directories, data products, internal-tool-as-product
in un-sexy niches VCs ignore (NOT the crowded AI/consumer/dev-tool arena). Solo-maintainable
software earning $5–30K/mo profit.

**Researched:** 2026-07-01 by Claude (Opus 4.8). Owner: Hooman Shariati.
**Method:** Track-B funnel (research-plan-lifestyle.md). Real web research via WebFetch against
Indie Hackers, Empire Flippers, Acquire.com, TinySeed, SaaS Ranger, Baremetrics Open Startups,
Starter Story–class founder writeups.

## 0. The sobering base rate (read this first)

The single most important number for calibrating this shape, from an analysis of 1,000+
micro-SaaS products (SaaSRanger, "Micro-SaaS Revenue Reality"):

| MRR band | % of micro-SaaS |
|---|---|
| under $500/mo | 70% |
| $500–$1K | 12% |
| $1K–$5K | 10% |
| **$5K–$20K** | **5%** |
| $20K–$50K | 2% |
| $50K+ | <1% |

Source: https://saasranger.com/blog/micro-saas-revenue-reality-what-1000-founders-actually-earn/

**Implication:** the $5–30K/mo target sits in the top ~7% of outcomes. This is achievable but
NOT the median. It requires either (a) buying a business already in that band, or (b) building
in a niche where the *reason* competition is thin is structural (boring, hard to reach the
buyer, regulated, relationship-driven) — not just "nobody built it yet." Cheap-and-easy-to-build
is an ANTI-signal (echoes LEARNINGS.md Round-3: low build cost attracts clones).

The corollary Hooman should internalize: **for a part-time operator with $25–150K, BUYING a
proven $8–12K/mo vertical SaaS is a far more reliable path to the target than BUILDING one from
zero and hoping to be in the 5%.** Both paths are covered below; the acquisition path scores
higher on "proven path to success."

## 2. Durability & crowding read for the shape

- **AI is mostly a TAILWIND, not a threat, for boring-vertical SaaS.** The moat in these
  businesses is NOT the code (AI can now write CRUD apps) — it's the *distribution* (you know
  how to reach 200 tattoo shops / 400 septic haulers), the *workflow depth* (10 years of edge
  cases baked in), the *data/system-of-record lock-in*, and the *switching cost* of a business
  running its daily ops on you. AI lowers YOUR build/maintenance cost. The risk: AI also lowers
  a competitor's build cost, so "thin CRUD in a reachable niche" is fragile. Defensibility comes
  from being the system-of-record and owning the customer relationship.
- **Crowding: LOW at the niche level, by design.** These niches are too small ($10–200M
  addressable) and too unglamorous for VC-scale players; incumbents are often 15-year-old
  desktop/on-prem software or spreadsheets. The swarmed arena (horizontal AI dev tools, generic
  productivity, chatbots) is exactly what this shape *avoids*.
- **Structural reasons competition stays low:** hard-to-reach fragmented buyers (you must know
  the trade shows / FB groups / associations), unsexy work, sometimes light regulation/compliance
  (a moat, not a barrier — see LEARNINGS.md #11), and system-of-record lock-in once installed.

## 3. Fit for Hooman

Strong fit overall: he can build/maintain solo (or now, with AI, faster than ever), his infra/AI
background is an *operational cost advantage* (cheap to run, can add genuinely useful AI features
competitors can't). **The gap is distribution, not engineering** — reaching septic haulers or

BUY-AND-IMPROVE path fits him better than pure build-from-zero: he inherits customers and
cashflow and applies his engineering/AI edge to grow and automate. Time fits 10–20 hrs/wk once
either (a) an acquired business is stabilized, or (b) a built product crosses ~$3K MRR and can
support a part-time VA/support contractor.

## 4. The opportunities (6–10, funnel-scored below in the structured output)

1. **Acquire a $8–12K/mo bootstrapped vertical micro-SaaS** (Empire Flippers / Acquire.com /
   FE International). Highest "proven path" score — you buy INTO the target band. Real listing
   evidence above (#94153: $11.8K/mo net at 32× monthly).
2. **Compliance / mandated-recordkeeping SaaS for a regulated trade** (e.g. septic/grease-trap
   pumping manifests, backflow-test reporting, food-safety logs, DOT/ELD-adjacent driver
   compliance). Regulatory mandate = non-optional demand (LEARNINGS.md #11).
3. **Vertical field-service / shop-management SaaS for a fragmented un-sexy trade** (the TinySeed
   pattern: tattoo shops, pet-care, jewelry repair, auto-collision, small-engine repair).
4. **Paid niche B2B directory / lead-marketplace** in a trade with real deal flow (e.g. connect
   commercial-property managers to specialty contractors; charge for listings + leads).
5. **Data product / "database-as-a-subscription"** — scrape/aggregate/clean a public-but-painful
   dataset a specific profession pays monthly to access (permit data, lien/UCC data, liquor
   license renewals, expiring-contract signals).
6. **Micro-SaaS built AROUND a large platform's ecosystem** (WordPress plugin, Shopify app, QBO
   app, Jobber/ServiceTitan add-on) serving a specific vertical's gap on that platform.
7. **"Internal tool as a product"** — productize the spreadsheet/Airtable/script a specific
   profession hacks together (e.g. commission reconciliation, route optimization, quote builders
   for a trade). Echoes Empire Flippers #94153 (reimbursement auditing).
8. **Niche calculation / spec / quoting engine SaaS** — Hooman's EE + engineering strength:
   e.g. HVAC load calc, electrical panel/service sizing, structural/beam quick-checks, solar
   string sizing — priced tools for tradespeople who currently use error-prone spreadsheets.

Detailed scoring, economics, 90-day plans, and risks for each are in the structured output.

## 5. Shape verdict

**A good vein for Hooman — but the reliable version is BUY-AND-IMPROVE, not build-from-zero.**
Building hits the brutal 5% base rate and a 12–24mo distribution grind. Buying a proven
$8–12K/mo bootstrapped vertical SaaS at 3–4× annual profit ($300–450K enterprise value; his
$25–150K as down payment + seller note / small SBA-style structure, or an outright cheaper
$5–8K/mo asset) puts him in the target band on day one and lets him apply his real edge —
engineering + AI to cut costs and add features — to grow it. Best single sub-play:
**acquire a compliance- or system-of-record-anchored vertical micro-SaaS in a fragmented trade,
then use AI to widen the moat and delegate support.**
