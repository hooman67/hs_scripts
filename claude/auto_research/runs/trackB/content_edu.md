# Track B — Business Shape: Content / Media / Community / Education

**Researcher:** Claude (Opus 4.8, 1M). **Date:** 2026-07-01. **Owner:** Hooman Shariati.
**Mandate:** secondary/lifestyle income, $5–30K/mo profit, part-time (10–20 hrs/wk or
delegable), $25–150K capital, AI-safe (avoid crowded AI/SW/VC hype), clear proven path.

This report covers the shape: **niche paid newsletters, professional communities, courses,
and info products with a durable audience moat in a specific vertical.** Focus is on
BORING, PROFESSIONAL, NON-CONSUMER niches where the audience is a defensible asset and the
income is recurring — not general-interest content that races to the bottom.

---

## Why this shape fits the Track-B mandate

- **Audience is the moat.** Once you own the attention of N professionals in a vertical, a
  clone can't copy the relationship. This is exactly the "defensible asset accrues to the
  operator" property the venture track kept failing to find (LEARNINGS round-3).
- **AI is a tailwind, not a threat, IF the niche is trust-based / relationship-based.** AI
  makes it cheaper to produce drafts, moderate communities, and repurpose content. It does
  NOT replace a named human's curation, judgment, and community in a professional vertical.
  (The threat is only to generic SEO content farms — which we explicitly avoid.)
- **Recurring revenue, low capital, delegable.** Paid subscriptions / memberships are MRR.
  Ops (moderation, editing, customer support) delegate cleanly to a $1.5–4K/mo contractor.
- **Boring = good.** VCs don't chase a $250K/yr newsletter for HVAC contractors. That's the
  point.

**The key durability rule (from the data below):** pick a niche where subscribers have
**professional/economic motivation** (their job, license, or business depends on it), not
hobby/entertainment motivation. Professional verticals show 5–20x higher paid-conversion
and far lower churn than lifestyle/entertainment ones.

## The economic reality (verified benchmarks)

**Paid-newsletter economics (beehiiv State of Paid Newsletters 2026):**
- Median free→paid conversion: **0.62%** (~6 paying per 1,000 free). Top-decile Technology
  5.55%, Economy 30.80%. Finance/Investing top-decile **18–20%** (≈26–30x median).
- Median lifetime value: **$83 (community) → $230 (investing)**. Subscriber lifetime 6–20
  months; Money churns ~16.7%/mo, Food & Drink ~5%/mo.
- Median price: **$10/mo or $100/yr**. By vertical (monthly): Investing $27, Finance $20,
  Business $15, Lifestyle/Travel $7–9.
- Source: beehiiv.com/blog/the-state-of-paid-newsletters-2026.

**Composite newsletter income profiles (influencerskit.com income-report analysis):**

  @ $19 = $3.8K, consulting $1.5K).
- 30,000 subs, creator-economy niche → **$29,500/mo gross (~$25.5K net)**.
- Source: influencerskit.com/blog/newsletter-income-report-creator-case-studies-real-numbers.

**Courses / info products:**

## Opportunities (6–10 concrete plays within the shape)

### 1. Paid B2B trade/vertical newsletter (e.g. a specific contractor trade)
**What:** A weekly paid email for one boring professional vertical (HVAC, roofing,
commercial pest control, dental-practice owners, ag/precision-farming, marine surveyors,
freight brokers). Curated market intel, regulation changes, pricing/labor benchmarks,
vendor reviews. Monetize via paid tier ($15–30/mo) + sponsorships + jobs board.
**Evidence:** beehiiv 2026 — Business-vertical top-decile conversion and $15 median price;
influencerskit profile #3 (12K subs, professional niche) = $10.7K/mo. Stacked Marketer Pro
proves a pure-paid B2B newsletter at **$99/mo** works (DuckDuckGo result). B2B newsletters
priced up to $300/yr.
**Realistic profit:** $6–20K/mo at 8–15K subs after 18–30 months.
**Why durable/AI-safe:** trust + curation in a niche AI can't build a reputation in; boring
enough that VCs/clones ignore it. AI helps you produce faster (tailwind).

### 2. Certification / licensing exam-prep course (regulated professions)
**What:** A course + question bank for a licensing exam in a growing regulated field
(drone Part 107, HAM radio, HVAC EPA 608, NASM personal training, real-estate license,
insurance license, boiler operator, food-safety manager). One-time price $99–499.
**Evidence:** Drone Launch Academy — 30,000+ students, $50K+/mo, FAA Part 107. This is the
single cleanest proven playbook in the whole shape: a mandated exam = non-optional demand.
**Realistic profit:** $5–25K/mo once ranked, largely passive (evergreen + updates).
**Why durable/AI-safe:** the credential is legally mandated; the moat is the pass-rate
reputation + updated question bank. Boring, regulator-gated demand.

### 3. Paid professional community (Skool/Circle) for a specific operator role
**What:** A recurring-membership community ($39–250/mo) for one occupation to trade tactics,
templates, and peer support (e.g. med-spa owners, self-storage operators, court reporters,
short-term-rental managers, ecom-agency owners, government-contract bidders).
**Evidence:** profitable.app/skool live tracker — Wealthy Insurance Agent (14.4K @ $97),
Business Lending Masterclass (4.8K @ $250), War Dogs Academy government-contract (2.6K @
$4,997/yr, only 16 months old). $5–30K/mo needs only ~50–300 members.
**Realistic profit:** $5–25K/mo at 100–400 members after 12–24 months.
**Why durable/AI-safe:** peer relationships and accountability are un-clonable; recurring MRR.

### 4. Niche paid data/benchmark product delivered as content
**What:** A subscription report/dashboard of proprietary benchmark data one vertical will
pay for — labor rates, material prices, salary bands, ad-rate benchmarks, insurance-claim
data — packaged as a recurring paid newsletter/portal.
**Evidence:** beehiiv — Finance/Investing top-decile 18–20% conversion, $27 price, $230 LTV
(highest of any vertical); investing 5K-list ≈ $13.6K/yr at median vs $1.3K for travel.
Data/finance is the highest-monetizing content vertical by a wide margin.
**Realistic profit:** $8–30K/mo; smaller list needed because willingness-to-pay is high.
**Why durable/AI-safe:** the moat is the proprietary dataset you collect over time (a data
flywheel — the exact archetype LEARNINGS praises for CutLog/TitlePlant). AI can't scrape
what you gather firsthand.

### 5. Buy-and-operate an existing cash-flowing newsletter/content site
**What:** Acquire a $700–4,200/mo-net newsletter or niche content site (Flippa,
Empire Flippers, Acquire.com) inside the $25–150K budget and grow it via a paid tier +
sponsors.
**Evidence:** Empire Flippers live listings trade content/subscription assets at ~36–46x
monthly net; a $50–120K spend at 30–40x buys a $1,500–4,000/mo-net asset, then you add a
paid tier to lift it into $5–15K/mo.
**Realistic profit:** buy $2K/mo-net → grow to $6–12K/mo in 12–18 months.
**Why durable/AI-safe:** skips the 0→1 audience-build (the hardest part); you inherit the
moat. Risk: buying a decaying/SEO-dependent asset (mitigate with traffic-source diligence).

### 6. Productized "info product + templates" for a technical profession
**What:** A paid template/tool library + short course for a technical role Hooman can
credibly serve (e.g. ML-infra playbooks, data-engineering system-design interview prep,
cloud-cost-optimization runbooks, "AI adoption for [boring industry]" guides). One-time
$149–999 + a $29/mo update membership.
**Evidence:** Skool SAT Masterclass ($229/mo test prep) and general course profile ($50K
over 14 mo) show priced info products sell; technical-interview-prep and system-design
courses are a documented six-figure category (e.g. the broad "tech interview prep" market).
**Realistic profit:** $4–15K/mo; strongest FOUNDER-FIT of all six — Hooman IS the expert.
**Why durable/AI-safe:** authority + specificity; a named practitioner's judgment doesn't
commoditize. Caveat: adjacent to the crowded "AI/tech creator" space — pick a boring sub-
niche (e.g. cloud-cost/infra for non-tech enterprises) to stay AI-safe.

### 7. Sponsored niche podcast/newsletter combo in an un-sexy vertical
**What:** A podcast + companion newsletter for one professional community, monetized purely
via 2–4 recurring sponsors who want that audience (equipment vendors, SaaS, insurers).
**Evidence:** influencerskit profiles show sponsorships at $2,500 each → $10K/mo at 30K subs;
niche B2B sponsor rates are high because the audience is qualified.
**Realistic profit:** $5–20K/mo once you have 3–4 recurring sponsors.
**Why durable/AI-safe:** sponsor relationships + audience trust; low competition in boring
verticals. Downside: sponsor concentration risk (mitigate by diversifying to 4+).

## Track-B funnel notes

**Crowding/durability:** The SHAPE is popular (many creators), but the DURABLE sub-niches
(regulated exam prep, boring-trade B2B intel, proprietary-data products, occupation-specific
communities) are NOT swarmed by VCs or AI clones — they're too small and unglamorous.
Reject the crowded lanes: general-interest newsletters, "make money online / creator
economy" content, faceless AI-content YouTube/blogs, and anything that lives or dies on
Google SEO (AI Overviews are actively killing SEO content — a real threat, avoid it).

**Path to success:** Every opportunity above has a named operator making $5–30K+/mo, cited.
The cleanest proven playbooks are #2 (exam prep — mandated demand) and #3 (occupation
community — recurring MRR). The best FIT for Hooman is #6/#4 (he's a domain expert with a
data-flywheel instinct).

**Downside:** Capital risk is LOW for build plays (you spend time + <$5K tools before you
know it works). The real cost is TIME to build an audience (12–30 months). Buy-and-operate
(#5) risks the purchase price if the asset decays — bounded at the check size, diligenceable.

**Fit/feasibility:** All learnable by a smart generalist. #6 leverages Hooman's unfair
advantage directly. All delegable (editing/moderation/support → contractors) once past 0→1.
The 0→1 audience build is the part that genuinely needs Hooman's ~10–20 hrs/wk and is not
delegable early.
