## Finalists

### 1. Compressed Air Leak Detection System (Feasibility: 8/10)
- **Technology**: Ultrasonic microphone array ($200 per unit) + AI identifies leak location and estimates cost
- **Value prop**: Compressed air leaks waste 20-30% of compressor energy ($billions/year globally). Acoustic detection finds leaks continuously without manual surveys.
- **Market**: $40B compressed air market; 70% of manufacturers have compressed air; typical plant leaks waste $20-50K/year in energy
- **Capital**: ~$180K to first revenue
- **Revenue model**: Hardware ($500 per monitoring node, 20-50 per plant) + SaaS ($200/month per plant for leak analytics/reporting)
- **Key strength**: Simplest possible product — ultrasonic mic + edge ML + cloud dashboard. No moving parts. Clear ROI: find $30K/year in leaks with $10K system + $2.4K/year SaaS.
- **Key insight**: This is the BEST ARCHETYPE example found yet — commodity sensor ($10 ultrasonic transducer), pure software value, universal customer base (every manufacturer), immediate measurable ROI.

### 2. AI-Powered Crane Load Monitoring Upgrade (Feasibility: 7/10)
- **Technology**: Retrofit load cells + wireless + AI for older cranes lacking modern load moment indicators
- **Value prop**: Bring OSHA-compliant load monitoring to the 500K+ unmonitored cranes in the US for 1/5th the cost of new systems
- **Market**: $3B crane safety market; 500K+ cranes in US without modern load monitoring; OSHA fines $15K-150K for violations
- **Capital**: ~$300K to first revenue
- **Revenue model**: Retrofit kit ($2-5K per crane) + monthly monitoring SaaS ($100/month)
