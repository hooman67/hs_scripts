## Finalist

### AI-Powered Asphalt Paving Quality System (Feasibility: 7/10)
- **Technology**: Thermal camera + GPS on paver captures continuous temperature profile of hot-mix asphalt as it's laid. ML detects temperature segregation (cold spots that cause premature failure). Commodity: FLIR Lepton ($200) + GPS ($10) + edge compute.
- **Value prop**: Identify paving defects in real-time before the roller compacts them — preventing $50K+ road failures that show up in 2-3 years. DOTs increasingly require thermal profiling for acceptance.
- **Market**: $50B US road paving; DOT acceptance testing segment $2B
- **Capital**: ~$300K to first revenue
- **Revenue model**: Hardware kit ($5K per paver) + SaaS ($300/month for thermal maps, QC reports, DOT submission)
- **Key driver**: FHWA mandating Intelligent Compaction and thermal profiling on federal road projects. States following.
- **Key insight**: Same FLIR Lepton thermal sensor as Cycle 008's trade inspection tool, but applied to paving QC. Federal mandate creating pull (vs push).
