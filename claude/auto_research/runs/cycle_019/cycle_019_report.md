## Finalist

### AI Laser Cutting Parameter Optimization SaaS (Feasibility: 8/10)
- **Technology**: Collect sensor data from fiber laser cutters (power, speed, gas pressure, focus, cut quality feedback) and ML-optimize cutting parameters for new materials/thicknesses. PURE SOFTWARE — connects to existing laser cutter controllers.
- **Value prop**: Optimal cut parameters for any material in minutes instead of hours of trial-and-error. Reduces scrap, speeds setup, improves edge quality.
- **Market**: $7B laser cutting market; 200K+ fiber laser cutters installed globally; growing 10%/year
- **Capital**: ~$200K to first revenue
- **Revenue model**: SaaS ($300-500/month per machine) + parameter library marketplace (community contributes, premium access)
- **Key strength**: PURE SOFTWARE on existing machines — no hardware needed. Same pattern as Cycle 002 alloy software (NIST research → software on existing machines). Every job shop wastes 1-2 hours per new material finding cut parameters. 200K+ machines × 250 working days × 30 min saved/day = massive productivity gain.
- **Feasibility: 8/10** — highest score this cycle because: zero hardware, zero regulatory, clear buyer (laser operators), immediate measurable value (less scrap, faster setup).
