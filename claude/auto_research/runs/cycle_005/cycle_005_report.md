## Patterns Observed

- **Application-on-infrastructure is the winning play**: Both winners use someone else's breakthrough (SpinMagIC's chip, Hubble's satellites) as a platform to build vertical products.
- **Vertical SaaS + hardware bundle** is the emerging archetype: generic platform tech + deep domain expertise in a specific industry = defensible moat.
- **Timing matters**: Satellite IoT infrastructure became operational in 2025 — applications built on it in 2026 catch the first wave before 6G commoditizes everything ~2030.
- **InchFab-class opportunities are too niche**: A market of ~50-100 customers globally is not venture-scale even if each pays well. Need >1000 potential customers minimum.
