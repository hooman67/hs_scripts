# Cycle 092 Report — Radon Domain (3/4): The Geographic Radon "Plant" as a Standalone Data Asset

**Date**: 2026-07-09. **Round**: 4. **Domain**: #10 Radon.
**Cycle focus**: Isolate the strongest single thesis from the domain map — the **address/zip-level
radon dataset + mitigation-outcome history** as a TitlePlant-class data asset sold to
downstream buyers (real-estate, insurance, appraisal, mitigation lead-gen). Test whether the
DATA (not the workflow tool) can be a company.
**Kill-risks**: (a) OEMs already aggregate readings at the source (Radalink); (b) public/free radon
data (EPA radon zones, state programs) commoditizes it; (c) buyer for the data may not pay.

---

## Step 1: Candidate Angles (4)

1. **RadonPlant data API** — sell zip/parcel radon-risk scores to inspection-ordering platforms,
   appraisal, and iBuyers. TRL 7.
2. **Mitigation lead marketplace** — high-radon results → warm mitigation leads sold to contractors.
   TRL 8.
3. **Radon-risk underwriting feed** — for home-warranty / disclosure-liability products. TRL 6.
4. **Aggregated benchmarking** — "your home vs. neighborhood" consumer report. TRL 8.

**Step 1: 3 PROMOTED** (drop #3 — insurance-underwriting buyer = slow enterprise/regulated sale,
poor founder fit).

---

## Step 2: Incumbent / OEM + Data-Source Check

- **Radalink already IS a radon plant (VERIFIED):** it aggregates and publicly displays "the radon
  levels our inspectors have found in your area" from its installed CRM fleet — a nationwide,
  continuously-fed dataset the founder cannot match without owning the devices. The single most
  valuable data feed already accrues to the OEM.
- **Public data commoditizes the floor (VERIFIED, general):** the **EPA Map of Radon Zones** (county
  Zone 1/2/3) is free; most state radon programs publish county/zip test aggregates; USGS geologic
  radon potential is public. A paid "radon-risk-by-zip" API competes against free government data
  that's "good enough" for the low-stakes disclosure use case.
- **Lead-gen (#2):** Radalink's portal already routes "hot leads" from measurement to mitigation —
  the referral funnel is owned by the party that sees the test result, i.e. the device OEM again.

**Moat gate:** the founder cannot uniquely accumulate the data — the device OEMs see every reading
first, and a free public floor exists. The data asset accrues to whoever owns the device feed.
Moat gate **FAILS** decisively — this is the *same* failure as 090, just one layer downstream.

**Step 2: ALL REJECTED.**
