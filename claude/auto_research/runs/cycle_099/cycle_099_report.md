# Cycle 099 Report — Locksmith Key-Plant (2/3): SAM Check + Modernization-Wedge Escape

**Date**: 2026-07-09. **Round**: 4. **Domain**: #12 Locksmith Master-Key "key plant".
**Cycle focus**: two tests. (1) The **SAM gate** the domain map flagged as the primary risk. (2) A
possible escape from the "already occupied" verdict of 098: could a **modern cloud/mobile-first**
tool win against aging desktop incumbents (ProMaster is Windows-desktop-rooted)?

---

## Step 1: The modernization-wedge candidate

**KeyPlant Cloud** — mobile-first, cloud master-keying + issuance ledger for the small independent
commercial locksmith who finds ProMaster's desktop UX dated and InstaKey enterprise-only. Pure
software, excellent founder fit. TRL 8.

---

## Step 2: Does the wedge survive?

- **Incumbents move to cloud too (ASSUMED):** ProMaster offers a hosted/multi-user path and InstaKey
  is already cloud. "Better UX on a mature category" is a feature advantage, not a moat — exactly the
  Round-2/3 "cheap + trivial to build + no flywheel = absorbable feature" failure. A weekend cloner or
  the incumbent closes a UX gap in one release.
- **No NEW data flywheel:** the keying data still accrues per-account the same way it does for
  ProMaster; the founder isn't uniquely accumulating anything the incumbents aren't. Moat gate FAILS again.

**Step 2: REJECTED** (feature, not moat).

---

## Step 3: SAM Gate — the independent kill

**Buyer universe (figures ASSUMED — no clean census of commercial-master-key locksmiths found):**
- US locksmith businesses: on the order of **~15,000–20,000** (BLS/industry rough), but only a
  fraction do **commercial master-key system** work (most revenue is auto keys, residential rekey,
  lockouts, safe work). The master-keying-active subset is plausibly **~2,000–5,000** shops.
- Realistic SaaS ACV for a small locksmith records tool: **$300–1,200/yr** (ProMaster is famously a
  modest one-time/low-cost license — the segment's price anchor is LOW).
- **SAM ≈ 3,500 shops × ~$700/yr ≈ $2.5M.** Generous: 5,000 × $1,200 ≈ **$6M.**

**10–40× below the $100M floor.** And the enterprise/multi-site segment that *could* carry higher
ACV (facility-side, à la InstaKey) is **already owned by InstaKey** and is an enterprise-procurement
buyer anyway.

**Growth:** flat — mature trade; electronic access control / smart locks are slowly *eroding*
mechanical master-key work at the high end (a headwind, not a tailwind).

**Step 3: REJECTED on SAM (and negative growth trend).**
