## Step 2: Competitive / Incumbent Check

- **Generic FSM (VERIFIED, category-level):** ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceM8 all
  serve home-service contractors and cover quote→schedule→invoice→job-photos→warranty. A radon
  mitigator is a small trade that fits their existing templates. Density 4/5 for the FSM core.
- **Radalink (VERIFIED):** its free portal already carries "hot leads" (measurement→mitigation
  referral funnel) + area radon data — it sits upstream and could extend downstream into mitigation.
- **Niche radon-specific FSM:** none dominant found — BUT the horizontal FSMs already suffice, the
  classic "absorbable feature, not a company."

**Moat gate (decisive):** the per-property ASD record + before/after retest outcomes are the only
candidate flywheel, but (i) a mitigator touches a property ONCE — no compounding annual revisit like
backflow testing, so the dataset never densifies per account; (ii) no external buyer pays for it;
(iii) any FSM already stores job photos + warranty. **No compounding, founder-owned, monetizable
data asset.** Moat gate FAILS.

**Step 2: REJECTED** (density 4/5 + moat-gate fail).
