## Finalists (Step 5 Graduates)

### 1. Custom Alloy Software Plugin for Metal 3D Printers (Feasibility: 8/10)
- **Technology**: NIST's laser-stirring technique (public domain) — software-only, no hardware
- **Market**: $12B metal AM market growing at 24% CAGR to $102B by 2035
- **Capital**: ~$990K to commercial launch
- **Key strength**: Pure software play on existing $35-100K machines; no IP to license; deepest moat is metallurgy expertise
- **Revenue model**: $50-100K/year per machine license + alloy recipe marketplace

### 2. Legacy IC Recovery-as-a-Service (Feasibility: 7/10)
- **Technology**: Computer vision + thermal robotic desoldering of ICs from e-waste PCBs
- **Market**: $77.4B e-waste management + $15B semiconductor aftermarket
- **Capital**: ~$1.12M to first production cell deployed
- **Key strength**: Unit economics are exceptional ($360K-1.2M revenue per $69K cell per year); addresses chip shortage + e-waste + right-to-repair simultaneously
- **Revenue model**: Revenue-share with recyclers + chip resale marketplace
