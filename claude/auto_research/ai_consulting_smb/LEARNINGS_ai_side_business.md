# Learnings — "Where can Hooman enter the AI space for an on-the-side business?" (Aug 2026)

**Context:** After the 5-agent SMB-AI-consulting research, Hooman asked the sharper question: given
everything this project learned AND his specific profile, is there anywhere in AI he can enter for a
*side* business? This file captures the strategic reasoning + his decisions. Companion to the 5
research files and `SMB_AI_CONSULTING_PLAN.md` in this folder.

---

## The decision (CONFIRMED BY HOOMAN, 2026-08-29)
- **Pursue: consulting FIRST → find the repeatable pattern → productize it.** The consulting is not
  the business; it's de-risked customer-discovery + distribution for a vertical micro-product.
- **Stay AWAY from image/video generation** — that's exactly what he does at Adobe, so avoiding it is
  how he stays legally clean. Lead with agentic/ops/workflow automation instead.
- **On Gate 0 (Adobe conflict):** Hooman is comfortable proceeding, reasoning detection risk is low
  and he'd quit before it's big enough to matter. (See the correction below — the agent flagged that
  the real risk is IP *ownership*, not detection; Hooman accepts the risk but chose the gen-media
  avoidance as the compliance hedge, which materially reduces the exposure.)

## The reframe of Gate 0 that changed the plan (important)
- The risk that bites is **IP ownership, not detection.** Standard IP-assignment agreements can vest
  work *related to the employer's business* in the employer **whether or not they ever find out.**
- **"Stay quiet until it's big enough to quit" inverts the risk:** big-and-valuable is exactly the
  trigger — an acquirer/investor/co-founder runs due diligence, finds the Adobe agreement, and the
  ownership question surfaces on the *asset itself*, not on conduct. The more successful it gets, the
  more exposed it is.
- **The cheap fix that preserves the plan:** operate in a domain ADJACENT to Adobe's business (NOT
  gen-media), on his own time/equipment. This is why "avoid image/video" is both the legal hedge AND
  points at the strongest option anyway. Hooman adopted this.

## Why "consulting → vertical micro-product" is the best shape FOR HIM (the core insight)
This is **"the informed CutLog."** CutLog failed because he built the product FIRST, then had to find
fragmented buyers (his documented weakness) and a moat he didn't have. This inverts every failure:
1. **Routes around his ONE weakness (distribution to fragmented buyers).** He never cold-sells a
   product to strangers — the consulting relationship IS the distribution channel.
2. **Uses his ACTUAL edge** (enterprise delivery judgment + agentic architecture + diligence alpha),
   not commodity code anyone can wire in n8n.
3. **Starts tiny** (one client, near-zero capital) — fits the capital-light, no-cofounder constraint.
4. **Produces a validated product spec:** the same painful manual workflow repeating across 2–4
   clients IS the spec — validated, with paying customers already in hand before a line of product code.
5. **Becomes an ASSET that compounds** (vertical workflow tool + accumulated usage data = a real
   moat), not an hourly grind.

## The ranked AI-entry landscape (for his profile)
| Shape | Fit | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Consulting → vertical micro-product** (informed CutLog) | routes around distribution weakness, uses edge, starts small, becomes an asset | **★ Best** |
| Pure narrow consulting (stop at step 1) | real demand, but time-for-money, focus-heavy, doesn't scale | on-ramp only, not the destination |
| Sell to developers / AI builders (agent infra, eval tools) | distribution via his own peer community; literally his day-job expertise | strong distribution BUT **maximally Adobe-conflicted** — avoid while employed |
| Gen-media production (Firefly-grade ad creative at scale) | his most unique skill | **most Adobe-conflicted** — skip while employed |
| Buy a small AI-adjacent cash-flowing tool | capital-led not focus-led (fits buy>build) | AI-asset froth + fast decay; doesn't USE his skill (he wants to) |

**The governing pattern:** the shapes that use his skill BEST (dev-tools, gen-media) are his MOST
Adobe-conflicted; the shape cleanest on both conflict AND distribution is the **vertical
consulting→product** play in a NON-Adobe domain (the trades / home & field services).

## How this reconciles with the whole project's prior learnings
- **Time/focus is the binding constraint** (not capital or ideas). This shape starts at ~one client,
  scales focus-demand only as revenue justifies → the least-bad focus profile of any BUILD shape.
- **Build-from-zero is his trap** (Rounds 1–4 = zero winners; distribution weakness). This defers the
  build until distribution + moat + spec are already solved by the consulting phase.
- **"The shape that looks most like his day job is the seductive trap"** — still true, and it's why we
  DON'T do generic AI consulting or dev-tools; we do a *vertical* he has no Adobe overlap with, using
  the day-job skill only as the delivery capability.
- **BUY > BUILD for this founder** was the acquisition-track conclusion; this is the one BUILD shape
  that borrows the best property of BUY (inherited customers) by acquiring them via consulting first.

## Open risks to keep honest about
- It's still a BUILD, and still consumes evening focus — the failure mode is stalling at "busy
  consultant, never productizes." The discipline is to treat every engagement as R&D toward a product.
- The Adobe IP-ownership exposure is reduced (non-gen-media, own equipment) but not zero — revisit if
  the product ever approaches gen-media or real value/liquidity.
- Vertical pick must be a domain where the manual pain is REAL, repeats across clients, and the buyer
  is reachable — home/field services ranked #1 on exactly these axes (see `01_target_segments.md`).
