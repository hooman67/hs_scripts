## NOTES FOR HOOMAN (do not include in the sent version)
- **Pricing:** start at **$2,500** for the first 2–3 clients to lower their risk and win case studies;
  move to $4,000 once you have proof. It's an easy-yes on purpose.
- **The scope line that prevents creep:** the engagement = ONE opportunity map + ONE built automation.
  Everything else is the retainer. Say that explicitly on the call.
- **The IP clause** (in the contract, not this sheet): client owns their config/data; you retain the
  reusable methods/tools. This is what lets you productize later.
- **Keep it gen-media-free.** If a client's biggest pain is content/creative, note it in the Pain Log but
  do NOT sell it as your deliverable — steer to the ops/agentic automation. (Adobe conflict zone.)
- **The real goal of the engagement is R&D:** you're getting paid to discover the repeatable workflow.
  The Automation Opportunity Map doubles as your Repeatable Pain Log input (see `03_repeatable_pain_log.md`).
