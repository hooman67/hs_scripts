# Outreach Templates — Home & Field Services Owners

> **Voice rules:** short, peer-to-peer, specific, zero jargon, dollars over features. Lead with the
> credibility (builds AI for huge companies) but NEVER name Adobe's clients. Every message ends with a
> tiny, low-friction ask (a reply or a 20-min call), never a hard pitch. NO mention of image/video gen.
> Fill [BRACKETS]. Personalize the first line of every cold message — that's what lifts reply rates from
> ~3% to ~18% (see `03_finding_and_contacting_clients.md`).

---

## A. WARM outreach (do these FIRST — highest conversion)

### A1 — Warm intro request (to a friend/ex-colleague who knows owners)
> Subject: quick favor — know any home-service owners?
>
> Hey [Name], I'm starting something on the side: I help home-service companies (HVAC, plumbing, roofing,
> that kind of thing) automate the manual work that's quietly costing them — leads getting lost, quotes
> and invoices done by hand, that sort of thing. It's the same AI work I do for big companies, just sized
> for a 20–200-person shop.
>
> I'm looking to do a few first projects at a friendly rate to build case studies. Do you happen to know
> any owners or GMs in that world I could buy a coffee / do a free 20-min teardown for? Even a "you should
> talk to [name]" would mean a lot. No worries if not!

### A2 — Referral super-node (accountant / bookkeeper / supplier / trade-group contact)
> Subject: the manual-work problem you see in your [clients / customers]
>
> Hi [Name] — you work with a lot of [home-service businesses / contractors], so you see this: how much
> of their day is still manual — re-typing leads, rebuilding quotes, chasing follow-ups.
>
> That's exactly what I automate now (I build AI systems for large enterprises; I'm now doing it for
> smaller service companies). I do a fixed-fee "operations teardown" that finds where they're losing money
> to manual work and automates the biggest one in a few weeks.
>
> If any of your [clients] gripe about that stuff, I'd be glad to do a free teardown call — and I'm happy
> to make it worth your while for a good intro. Worth a quick chat?

## B. COLD email (personalize line 1 every time)

### B1 — Cold email, owner of a home-service company
> Subject: the manual work at [Company]
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> [PERSONALIZED LINE — e.g. "Saw [Company] runs [3 crews across the East Bay] — congrats on the growth"
> / "Noticed you're hiring a [dispatcher] — usually a sign the manual load is piling up."]
>
> Quick question: how much of your team's day still goes to manual work — leads typed into the CRM after
> the fact, quotes and invoices rebuilt by hand, follow-ups that rely on someone remembering? For most
> shops your size that's easily $[30–60K]/yr in lost jobs and wasted hours.
>
> I build AI systems for some of the largest companies in the world, and I now do the same — sized down —
> for home-service businesses. I've got a fixed-fee "operations teardown": in 3 weeks I map exactly where
> you're losing money to manual work and build the #1 automation live in your tools.
>
> Worth a free 20-minute call to see if there's anything here for [Company]? If I don't spot at least a
> few thousand a year worth fixing, I'll tell you straight.
>
> — Hooman
> [phone] · [booking link]

### B2 — Follow-up #1 (3–4 days later, value-add, not a nudge)
> Subject: re: the manual work at [Company]
>
> Hi [First name] — following up with something useful either way. The single fastest win I see at most
> [HVAC/plumbing] shops: auto-capturing every inbound lead (phone, text, web form) into the CRM with an
> instant first response. Shops that do it stop losing the [20–30%] of leads that quietly slip through.
>
> Happy to show you what that'd look like for [Company] on a quick call — or just reply and I'll send a
> 2-minute walkthrough. No pressure.
>
> — Hooman

### B3 — Follow-up #2 (final, ~1 week later)
> Subject: last note — [Company]
>
> Hi [First name], I'll stop here so I'm not cluttering your inbox. If automating the manual side of
> [Company] ever gets to the top of your list, I'd love to help — the free 20-min teardown offer stands
> anytime: [booking link]. Wishing you a strong [season]. — Hooman

## NOTES FOR HOOMAN (don't send)
- **Order of operations:** exhaust A (warm) before B (cold). Warm converts many multiples higher.
- **Volume math:** ~100 personalized cold touches → ~10 replies → ~3 calls → ~1 client. Send in small
  batches so you can personalize line 1 genuinely (use your own AI to draft, but eyeball each one).
- **The credibility line, safe form:** "I build AI systems for some of the largest companies in the world."
  True, potent, and names no one. NEVER write "Coca-Cola/Home Depot/Ford/Adobe client" — that's the
  confidentiality line.
- **Every reply routes to the same place:** the free 20-min call → the Teardown offer (one-pager 01).
- **Log every conversation's pain points** in the Repeatable Pain Log (03) even if they don't buy — the
  discovery value is in the pattern across all of them.
