# Repeatable Pain Log — the instrument that finds your product

> **Purpose:** one running log across ALL engagements + discovery calls. Every manual workflow you see
> becomes a row. After ~3–4 engagements, the workflow that repeats AND scores high on the 6 signals is
> your product. This is the whole point of the consulting phase — treat it as R&D, fill it after every
> client interaction (even ones who don't buy).
>
> **How to use:** (1) Log each workflow you observe as a row in Section 2, tagged with the client.
> (2) After each engagement, update the 6-signal scores in Section 3. (3) When one workflow clears the
> gate in Section 4, that's your build. Keep it in this markdown file, or move to a spreadsheet if you
> prefer — the structure is what matters.

---

## Section 1 — The 6 signals (score each workflow 1–5; definitions)

| # | Signal | 1 (kill) | 5 (green) |
|---|--------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | **Repetition** | seen at 1 client, bespoke | same shape at ≥3 of ~4 clients |
| 2 | **Dollar-countable pain** | vague "efficiency" | owner names a hard $ number |
| 3 | **Productizable core** | needs a rebuild each time | one config'd tool, ≤10% per-client tweak |
| 4 | **Data moat / compounding** | trivially cloned | gets better with usage (data/benchmarks/library) |
| 5 | **NOT incumbent-owned** | ServiceTitan/Jobber/QuickBooks already ships it | sits in the gap between their tools |
| 6 | **Retainer-shaped** | one-time fix | ongoing monthly need |

**Weighting:** #1, #4, #5 are the tiebreakers — they're what killed every prior idea in this project.
A workflow that scores 5 on repetition + pain but 1 on "not incumbent-owned" is NOT a product (the SoR
will bundle it). Walk from those.

## Section 4 — The productize gate (the decision)

**Build the FIRST workflow that meets ALL of these:**
- [ ] Repetition (signal 1) ≥ 4 — appeared, near-identical, at ≥3 clients
- [ ] Not-incumbent-owned (signal 5) ≥ 4 — their existing SaaS does NOT already ship it
- [ ] Data-moat (signal 4) ≥ 3 — a copycat can't trivially clone it
- [ ] Total ≥ 22/30
- [ ] **At least 2–3 of your existing engagement clients would pre-commit to pay for it** (the real test)

**If a workflow clears the gate →** productize it: narrow tool, first 2–3 clients pre-committed, priced as
setup + monthly. That's the asset.

**If NOTHING clears after ~4 engagements →** either (a) run 1–2 more engagements (the pattern may still be
forming), or (b) the vertical's pain is too fragmented / too incumbent-owned → switch vertical. Do NOT
build a product that failed the gate just because you've done the consulting work.

## NOTES FOR HOOMAN
- **This file is the product-discovery engine.** The temptation is to skip it and just consult. Don't —
  the whole thesis (invert CutLog: distribution + validated spec BEFORE building) lives or dies here.
- **Fill it after every client touch,** including lost deals — a pain that shows up on calls you *lost*
  is still a real market signal.
- **Signal 5 (not-incumbent-owned) is the one this project got burned on most.** Before you get excited
  about any workflow, spend 30 min checking whether ServiceTitan / Jobber / Housecall Pro / QuickBooks
  already ships it. If they do, it's their feature, not your product.
- **Keep it gen-media-free** — if content/creative pain shows up, log it (it's real data) but don't make
  it your product (Adobe conflict zone).
