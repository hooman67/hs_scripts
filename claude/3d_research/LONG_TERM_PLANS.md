# Long-term plans

## Current checkpoint — 2026-09-28

The laundry-sink feasibility POC and customer-workflow mockup are complete. The next uncompleted
decision gate is a matched evaluation of generation accuracy and total human authoring effort versus
the incumbent motion-bank workflow. Hooman has not yet confirmed that this should be the next batch;
do not treat it as authorized execution.

## Phase 1 — Understand and audit

Reconstruct the proposal, inspect its evidence, identify hidden labor and assumptions, and explain
it in plain language without accepting its conclusions.

## Phase 2 — Astra capability experiments

Design free or near-free tests of whether Astra can author correct, readable blockouts from
non-technical intent. Separate geometry correctness, visual readability, iteration burden, and
model autonomy.

## Phase 3 — Generation validation

After explicit approval for each paid run, test whether validated blockouts improve Seedance
accuracy using controlled paired comparisons and a fixed rubric.

## Phase 4 — Alternative workflows

Compare blockouts against simpler controls and hybrids, including storyboard frames, ordered
keyframes, generated synthetic guides, constraint extraction, and reusable procedural templates.

## Phase 5 — Adoption decision

Rank candidates by accuracy, customer effort, technical skill, latency, cost, safety, robustness,
and integration complexity. Propose implementation only after user confirmation.
