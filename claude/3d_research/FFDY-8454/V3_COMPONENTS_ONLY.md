## Native Blender editing

1. Select a component directly in the viewport or the `CABINET_COMPONENTS_DIRECT` collection.
2. Press `G` to move it. Press `X`, `Y`, or `Z` afterward to restrict movement to an axis.
3. Press `R` to rotate it. Press an axis key afterward to restrict the rotation.
4. Press `I` and choose `Location & Rotation` to save its pose at the current frame.
5. Move to another frame, reposition the component, and insert another key.
6. Repeat for any component in any order.

The floor and camera are optional scene conveniences. They do not control the cabinet components.
