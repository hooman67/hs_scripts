~$17.5k) than the higher-quality one (LLY ~$10.8k).** For a **quality-first** investor, the weights should
arguably be **flipped** — more of the compounder, less of the value-trap-risk name. The barbell also **misses
the cheapest, highest-ROE corner of healthcare entirely (managed care)**, which the prior research identified
as the actual value opportunity.

defensive, aging-tailwind sector, and it diversifies a portfolio that holdings_snapshot.md shows is **~70%
commodities/real-assets** — healthcare is one of the few genuinely diversifying, defensive sleeves he has. **Do
not cut healthcare exposure; improve its shape.**
