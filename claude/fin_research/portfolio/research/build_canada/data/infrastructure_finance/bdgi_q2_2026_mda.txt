OVERVIEW OF BADGER
Badger is North America's largest provider of non-destructive excavation and related services, serving a broad 
customer base across a diverse range of construction, utility, transportation, industrial and other critical 
infrastructure related markets. Badger's customers typically operate near high concentrations of underground 
power, communication, water, gas and sewer lines, where safety and economic risks are high and where non-
destructive excavation provides a safe alternative for certain customer excavation requirements.  
Badger's key system is the Badger Hydrovac TM, which uses a pressurized water stream to liquify the soil cover, 
which is then removed with a powerful vacuum and deposited into a storage tank. Badger operates a vertically 
integrated business model, designing and manufacturing its Badger Hydrovac fleet at its facility in Red Deer, 
Alberta, which has an annual production capacity of more than 350 units and enables Badger to produce units at 
a lower cost compared to purchasing from third parties. This approach provides Badger with direct control over 
equipment specifications, fleet availability, and lifecycle management, supporting operational consistency across 
its Canadian and U.S. operations. To complement the Badger Hydrovac and expand Badger's service offerings, 
Badger has a select number of specialty units, mainly combo trucks, sewer and flusher units, dump trucks, rock 
slingers, and airvacs. 
QUARTERLY OPERATIONAL HIGHLIGHTS
Badger delivered solid operating and financial performance in the second quarter, generating $257.1 million of 
revenue, up 23% from the same quarter in 2025. Revenue growth reflected broad-based strength across all of our 
geographic regions and market segments, including utilities, power generation, transportation and data center 
construction. This growth was supported by additions to our fleet, along with gains in our utilization and pricing.
Adjusted EBITDA (1) improved by 25% over the same quarter in 2025, to $66.1 million for the quarter. Adjusted 
EBITDA margin(1) for the quarter was 25.7% compared with 25.3% for the same quarter in 2025. Starting in 2025 
and continuing into 2026, Badger has made targeted investments to increase long-term scale and operational 
efficiency, including:
• opening new branches to further capture end market growth in key markets and a corresponding increase 
in certain operational and commercial leadership positions to ensure capacity for continued growth and 
scale.
• the roll-out of Badger's Operational Excellence program through multiple pilots. We expect this program to 
drive efficiencies across all of our field locations; and

BUSINESS OUTLOOK
Looking to the remainder of 2026, Badger expects a continuation of the strong growth in our end markets and 
customer demand that we experienced over the last twelve months. Badger's industry-leading footprint, well- 
established commercial and pricing strategies, comprehensive safety program, and plans for continued 
investments to expand our branch network in key strategic markets leave the Company well positioned to support 
our customers' growing needs.  
As a result of continued, increased demand, we expect our 2026 fleet growth to be on the high end of the planned 
7% to 10% range. Concurrent with the higher truck build rate that started in the fourth quarter of 2025, we 
continue to focus on the hiring and training of new operators to serve our growing fleet capacity.
Badger's Operational Excellence program, which was introduced in early 2025, is being rolled out to all of our 
branches throughout the remainder of 2026 and 2027. As our core hydrovac service business grows, we have 
identified opportunities to expand Badger's service offering into complementary and adjacent market service lines. 
We are underway with the launch and expansion of these new offerings which includes spending approximately 
$15.0 million to $25.0 million of capital in 2026..
Badger is conducting a fulsome process to develop a second manufacturing facility, located in the U.S., to meet 
the growing demand for hydrovacs and hydrovac services. Upon completion of our due diligence, we plan to 
move as quickly as possible to secure a location and work towards opening this second facility as early as the 
second half of 2027 or first half of 2028.
These investments and initiatives are expected to capture market opportunities and to expand profitability over the 
longer term to create sustained shareholder value across the organization.

Non-destructive Excavation Service Revenue
Non-destructive excavation service revenue was $ 245.5 million, $48.1 million or 24% higher than  $197.4 million 
in the same quarter of the prior year as we have been able to meet the continued growth in customer demand 
across all of our markets through our continued investment in our fleet combined with the ongoing benefits from 
our sales and marketing investments.
Other Service Revenue
Other service revenue was $11.6 million, $0.8 million or 8% higher than $10.8 million in the same quarter of the 
prior year. 
Revenue per Truck per Month
RPT was $47,731, a 14% increase from $41,867 in the same quarter of the prior year. The growth in revenue 
outpaced the increase in the average number of hydrovac units largely as a result of improved unit utilization 
combined with higher pricing. As at June 30, 2026, Badger had 1,822 hydrovac units in its fleet compared to 1,682 
as at June 30, 2025. 
Direct Costs
Direct costs were $179.6 million, or 70% of revenue compared to $144.6 million, or 69% of revenue in the same 
quarter of the prior year. 
Direct costs increased due to the higher volume of work performed to meet customer demand, greater costs 
associated with the larger fleet size, higher fuel prices, as well as increases to our operations support functions to 
meet the growth opportunities across our markets. Direct costs also increased as we continued to make  
investments in our Operational Excellence program and the launch of new service lines. Direct costs increased as 
a percentage of revenue as these investments, initially, have led to costs outpacing the increase in revenue.   
Gross Profit
Gross profit was $77.6 million and gross profit margin was 30.2%, compared to $63.6 million and 30.5% in the  
same quarter of the prior year, primarily driven by higher direct costs outpacing the revenue increase. As 
discussed in the Quarterly Operational Highlights and as expected, the Company's targeted investments in growth 
have had a near-term impact on gross profit margin but position the Company to capture growth opportunities as 
the year unfolds.

General and Administrative Expenses
G&A expenses were $11.4 million or 4% of revenue compared to $10.8 million or 5% of revenue in the same 
quarter of the prior year.  The decrease in G&A expenses as a percentage of revenue compared to the same 
quarter of the prior year was due to the increase in revenue and the overall stability in G&A functions.
Depreciation and Amortization of Property, Plant and Equipment, Right-of-Use and Intangible Assets
Depreciation and amortization expense was  $26.1 million compared to $21.7 million in the same quarter of the 
prior year. Depreciation expense, which is recorded on a straight-line basis, increased compared with the prior 
year primarily due to our continued growth in the h ydrovac fleet and increased right of use assets, principally our 
branch real estate and light duty vehicle fleets. Depreciation and amortization expense was stable as a percent of 
revenue. 
Share-Based Compensation 
Share-based compensation expense was $21.5 million compared to $9.2 million in the same quarter of the prior 
year. During the quarter, Badger's share price increased from CAD $62.04 per share at March 31, 2026 to 
CAD$93.02 per share at June 30, 2026 , compared to the increase in Badger's share price from CAD $38.86 per 
share at March 31, 2025 to  CAD $47.58 per share at June 30, 2025 . Share-based compensation expense 
fluctuates based on the effects of the movement in Badger's share price, combined with the impact of normal 
course vesting of previously issued long-term incentive plan grants and the issuance, if any, of new long-term 
incentive plan grants. 
The Company has entered into total return swap contracts to manage the exposure to share price market risk on 
its cash-settled long-term incentive plan programs. All total return swap contracts are recorded at fair value. The 
net gain on the total return swaps in the quarter was $20.0 million compared to a $6.6 million gain in the same 
quarter of the prior year. This is due to the greater increase in the share price of Badger's shares in the second 
quarter of 2026. For a discussion of the risks and risk management strategies associated with financial 
instruments, see "Critical Accounting Policies and Estimates" and "Risk Factors to Badger" in the Annual MD&A.

Senior unsecured notes
On May 14, 2026, the Company issued CAD$300.0 million of senior unsecured notes (the "Notes"), which mature 
on May 14, 2031 and bear interest at a fixed annual rate of 5.375%. Interest is payable semi-annually in arrears 
on May 14 and November 14, commencing on November 14, 2026. The net proceeds from the issuance were 
used to fully repay the Company's $100.0 million syndicated, secured, non-revolving term loan (the "Term Loan"), 
and repay a portion of the outstanding borrowings under the Credit Facility. The Notes were issued at par. The 
issuance of the Notes extended the Company’s debt maturity profile and reduced its reliance on the Credit Facility 
for term financing, while preserving capacity for operational flexibility and growth investment.
At any time prior to May 14, 2028 the Company may redeem (a) up to 40% of the aggregate principal amount of 
the Notes at a redemption price equal to 105.375% of the principal plus accrued and unpaid interest, subject to 
certain conditions being met; or (b) all or any part of the Notes at a redemption price equal to 100% of the 
aggregate principal amount plus an applicable premium and accrued and unpaid interest.
At any time on or after May 14, 2028, the Company may redeem all or any part of the Notes during the twelve-
months period beginning on May 14 of the following years at the following redemption prices, expressed as 
percentage of principal amount: 102.688% in 2028, 101.344% in 2029, and 100% in 2023 and thereafter, in each 
case plus accrued and unpaid interest.
