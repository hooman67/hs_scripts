5.1 Full Description of Material Change 
On October 13, 2026 , Enerflex announced the award of a major E S project. The Company has been 
contracted to design, engineer, fabricate, and assemble over one gigawatt of low emission behind -
the-meter natural gas -fired power generation units for a large -scale North American data center 
development. Combined with additi onal project awards, including approximately 450 megawatts 
announced on October 1, 2026, Enerflex’s backlog of behind -the-meter natural gas -fired power 
generation now exceeds 1.75 gigawatts, with deliveries extending through 2028.  
The projects will be executed through Enerflex's North American manufacturing operations. To 
support the execution of the Company’s behind-the-meter natural gas-fired power generation backlog 
and expected growth across Enerflex’s core markets, the Company is investing in expanding the 
capabilities of its ES business line. As previously announced, Enerflex has authorized approximately 
$100 million of investments in its ES facilities and capabilities, $15 million of which is included in 
Enerflex’s capital exp enditure guidance for 2026. These investments, combined with on -going 
initiatives to enhance collaboration, leverage scale, improve operational efficiency, and solidify 
capabilities, are expected to significantly increase revenue capacity of Enerflex’s ES business line. 
Beyond the initial equipment scope, the Company’s backlog of low -emission behind -the-meter 
natural gas -fired power generation units provides opportunities for future commissioning, 
installation, and after-market services support including digital solutions  with Enerflex’s ReliaCore TM

support the full lifecycle requirements of behind-the-meter power generation development.
