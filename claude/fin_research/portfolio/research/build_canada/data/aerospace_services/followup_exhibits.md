# Direct-source exposure exhibits — Calian and FTG

Cutoff/retrieval date: 2026-10-20. All amounts are C$ unless stated otherwise.

## Calian: defence backlog is disclosed; Canadian-defence backlog is not

Primary exhibit: Calian Q3 FY2026 combined release, statements and MD&A, published 2026-08-12, period ended 2026-06-30.

URL: https://investors.calian.com/app/uploads/sites/2/2026/02/Q3-FY26-Combined-PR-FS-and-MDA.pdf

Verbatim supporting passages:

> Ending backlog of $1.4 billion, including one billion in defence

> The Company’s realizable backlog at June 30, 2026 was $1,448 million with terms extending to fiscal 2030.

> Contracted backlog $1,400,530; Option renewals $227,780; Management estimate of unrealizable portion $(180,162); Estimated Realizable Backlog $1,448,148.

> Most fee-for-service contracts provide the customer with the ability to adjust the timing and level of effort throughout the contract life and as such the amount actually realized could be materially different from the original contract value.

> The amounts disclosed below represent the value of the firm orders only. Such orders may be subject to future modifications that might impact the amount and/or timing of revenue recognition. The amounts disclosed below do not include unexercised options or letters of intent.

Firm remaining performance obligations were C$1,248.338 million: C$699.858 million (56.1%) expected within 24 months and C$548.480 million (43.9%) thereafter. This firmer IFRS-note figure is C$199.810 million below management's C$1.448 billion realizable-backlog measure.

The issuer discloses approximately C$1 billion of **defence** backlog, about 69% of C$1.448 billion realizable backlog. It does **not** disclose that this is Canadian defence backlog. The claim that more than 60% of backlog is Canadian defence cannot be verified from the filing. Separate evidence must not be conflated:

- Nine-month revenue by customer location was Canada 63%, US 22%, Europe 13%, other 2%.
- The largest customer, across multiple contracts committed through 2030, was 40% of nine-month total revenue. The filing does not name that customer in this passage or state that the 40% is all defence backlog.
- The broad Defence & Space segment was 66.7% of nine-month revenue; it includes defence training, manufacturing and healthcare, space/terrestrial communications, defence IT/cyber, and other specialized offerings.
- Named Q3 signings included more than C$8 million of healthcare solutions with defence agencies in Canada, but most other signing categories mix customers or regions.

Primary annual corroboration: Calian FY2025 annual report, published 2025-11.

URL: https://investors.calian.com/app/uploads/sites/2/2025/02/Calian-AR-2025-FINAL.pdf

Verbatim supporting passage:

> Reached a record backlog of $1.4B, of which two-thirds is in defence

Again, the annual report says defence, not Canadian defence.

## Calian: completed/delivered AI projects, but no disclosed AI revenue

Primary exhibit: Calian AI Innovation page, published 2025-06-12 and modified 2025-12-11.

URL: https://www.calian.com/ai-innovation/

Verbatim supporting passages:

> Calian completed a project for the CSA that uses AI to automate anomaly detection capabilities for satellite telemetry data. The solution used operational satellite telemetry data to train an ensemble of deep neural networks (LSTMs) to learn to detect anomalies. It’s estimated to save about 65 per cent of an operator’s time spent on FDIR process.

> Calian partnered with NATO’s Joint Assessments Division to deliver a cutting-edge AI engine that automatically generates the realistic data and insight reports analysts need during training exercises.

> Calian has developed AI assistant functionality in MaestroEDE to automate the development of exercise content for any exercise and integrates with the Virtual Command and Control (VCCI) toolset.

These statements establish a completed CSA project, delivery to NATO, and a developed product capability. The page and financial reports disclose no AI-specific revenue, margin, contract value or backlog. The 65% figure is estimated operator time saved, not revenue growth or customer savings realized in audited results.

## FTG: customer geography is disclosed; defence/commercial mix is not

Primary exhibit: FTG Q3 2026 report, published 2026-10-07, period ended 2026-08-28.

URL: https://www.ftgcorp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/FTG-Q3-2026-Report_FINAL.pdf

Verbatim supporting passages:

> FTG continues to strive to balance its sales between commercial aerospace and defence customers.

The filing does not provide a defence-versus-commercial revenue percentage. Segment revenue cannot supply that split because both Circuits and Aerospace serve commercial and defence programs.

Customer-location revenue for the first nine months of 2026 was:

- Canada C$11.602 million, 7.1%
- United States C$116.344 million, 70.9%
- Asia C$23.221 million, 14.1%
- Europe C$11.429 million, 7.0%
- Other C$1.539 million, 0.9%

Q3 alone was Canada 6.6%, US 71.8%, Asia 13.6%, Europe 7.1%, other 0.9%. Management says Canadian and US growth came mainly from increased shipments to existing customers; Asian growth came mainly from China's C919 commercial-aircraft program. Customer location is not the location of manufacturing or the ultimate government/program funding source.

Relevant defence-demand passage:

> FTG’s plants in the USA and Canada are certified vendors for the US defence supply chain.

This supports US/allied defence participation and cautions against treating FTG's defence demand as primarily Canadian.

Temporary-pricing passages:

> [Circuits] revenue increased primarily due to operational improvements at several U.S. sites, short-term pricing premiums and $0.9 million driven by favourable exchange rates.

> Gross margin rates increased due to operational improvements as well as short-term pricing premium in the quarter.

FTG does not quantify the revenue or gross-profit contribution from pricing premiums and provides no roll-off date. The Oct. 7 report therefore supports treating the 42.9% Q3 gross margin as partly temporary but does not support a numerical normalized margin.

Primary annual geography corroboration: FTG FY2025 AIF, published 2026-02-18.

URL: https://www.ftgcorp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/AIF_FINAL-FTG-2025.pdf

FY2025 customer-location sales were Canada 6.2%, US 69.9%, Asia 14.3%, Europe 8.4%, other 1.2%. The AIF says about 50% of operations are located in Canada while FTG competes primarily in US dollars; this manufacturing footprint must not be confused with Canadian end demand.
