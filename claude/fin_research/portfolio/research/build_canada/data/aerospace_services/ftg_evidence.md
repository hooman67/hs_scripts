## AI and thesis fit

- Searches of the Q3 report and FY2025 AIF found no issuer disclosure of artificial-intelligence revenue. There is plausible manufacturing/avionics optionality, but no quantifiable AI thesis.
- Moat evidence: high-reliability, customer-qualified complex printed circuit boards and cockpit electronics, certifications, customized engineering, and long aerospace-program lives.
- Bear evidence: current valuation assumes sustained exceptional margins; short-term pricing premiums and classified-program ramps are poorly transparent; customer concentration, USD/CAD, tariffs, technology change, and acquisition integration remain material.
