## AI and thesis fit

- Searches of the financial reports found no issuer disclosure of AI products, contracts, or attributable revenue. AI is not an evidence-based investment thesis for Magellan.
- Build-Canada exposure is plausible through sovereign munitions, fighter and submarine opportunities, but current revenue remains majority commercial and globally diversified. Conditional MOUs/teamings should remain outside base-case backlog.
- Bear evidence: concentrated control and weaker minority-shareholder protections, high customer concentration, asset-heavy reinvestment, negative trailing after-capex cash flow, working-capital build, commercial-cycle and execution exposure, and uncertain conversion of Canadian announcements into funded revenue.
