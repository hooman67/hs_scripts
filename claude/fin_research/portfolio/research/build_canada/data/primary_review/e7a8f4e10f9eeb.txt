How it works
Identifying projects of national interest
We work with proponents, federal departments, provinces and territories, Indigenous Peoples, and industry to help identify projects that offer the greatest benefits for Canadians.
Submitting project information
We accept proposals for major projects including ports, railways, energy corridors, critical mineral developments, and clean energy initiatives, among others, to be considered as projects of national interest.
Evaluating projects
We assess projects against the five factors  outlined in the
Building  Canada Act
, among others, and provide recommendations and advice to  federal ministers and the Prime Minister.
MPO
supports
We provide varied support to projects based on the project type, status and unique requirements.
Projects listed under the
Building Canada Act
In addition to other  support, the
Building Canada Act
is one option we may  recommend for projects that would benefit from regulatory streamlining.
Listing under  the
Building Canada Act
will help advance select major projects  deemed to be of national interest by conditionally approving them to further  streamline federal review and approval processes.
