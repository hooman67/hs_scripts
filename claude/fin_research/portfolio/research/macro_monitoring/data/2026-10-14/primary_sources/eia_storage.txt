Summary
Working gas in storage was 3,500 Bcf as of Friday, October 2, 2026, according to EIA estimates. 
This represents  a net increase of 85 Bcf from the previous week. Stocks were 130 Bcf less than last year at this time and 68 Bcf above the five-year average of 3,432 Bcf. 
At 3,500 Bcf, total working gas is  within the five-year historical range.
