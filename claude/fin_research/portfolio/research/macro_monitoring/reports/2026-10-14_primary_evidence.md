## Preliminary integration findings

- **ASSUMED interpretation, medium confidence:** Stagflation **risk strengthened** with September payroll growth of only 29,000, unemployment rising to 4.2%, and a 0.65% monthly headline CPI increase. Positive Q2 GDP and August real spending remain contrary evidence to a claim of already established economy-wide stagnation. September core CPI was 2.56% year over year, lower than its short-run monthly acceleration might suggest. A mixed slowdown/supply-shock regime fits the opened evidence better than certainty about either stagflation or a benign soft landing.
- **VERIFIED forecast revision:** EIA increased 2027 Brent from $74 to $84; normalization is forecast later, at a higher price than the prior report.
- **REPORTED newer demand evidence:** September gold ETF inflows contradict use of historical Q2 outflows as the current flow trend.
- **DERIVED:** Losing 70% of domestic purchasing power over ten years requires prices to multiply 3.333 times, equivalent to `(1/0.30)^(1/10)-1 = 12.794%` annual inflation. Losing 70% against an FX comparator is a different claim: `0.30^(1/10)-1 = -11.343%` annual currency depreciation. Gold-relative loss is different again. Current reserve shares or above-target CPI alone establish none of those paths.

Global oil prices. Although we expect oil production and flows from the Middle East will increase, we raised our global oil price forecast compared with last month.
