# AI infrastructure quality candidates and silver vehicles

## User decision

Compare LITE, MRVL, LRCX, ANET, ARM, COHR and KLAC as possible constituents of the existing Quality
sleeve, using JQUA as the default implementation and MSFT/GOOGL as context. Direct-company positions
may not exceed 2% of the total portfolio. Separately compare direct silver implementations for an
Ameritrade account or OneGold, allowing either long-term or tactical use.

## Decision questions

1. Which companies have the strongest current operating quality, balance sheets and per-share cash
   conversion?
2. Which valuations require the least optimistic AI-growth assumptions at October 30, 2026 prices?
3. Which exposures diversify the household and existing portfolio rather than duplicating Adobe,
   GOOGL/JQUA, grid and commodity risks?
4. Does any direct name improve the Quality sleeve enough to displace diversified JQUA?
5. Which silver vehicle best balances physical backing, fee, spread, custody, tax complexity and
   liquidity, and is there room inside the accepted silver allocation?

## Evidence hierarchy and method

- Latest issuer filings and earnings releases control financial facts and company guidance.
- Issuer ETF/trust documents control fees, custody and redemption mechanics.
- Reproducible market-price and enterprise-value calculations use the October 30 close and latest
  disclosed balance-sheet/share data; vendor forward estimates are labeled secondary.
- AI attribution, cyclicality, customer concentration, dilution and capex are explicitly separated
  from headline revenue growth.
- Final judgments use `VERIFIED`, `ASSUMED` and `UNVERIFIED` labels and preserve contrary cases.

## Stopping rule

Stop after the latest filings, current valuation arithmetic, portfolio overlap and vehicle documents
are sufficient to rank the choices. Do not infer undisclosed AI revenue or fair value from promotional
language. No trades or live portfolio edits are authorized by this research.
