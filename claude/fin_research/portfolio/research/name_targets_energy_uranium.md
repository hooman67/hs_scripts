## 3. Is the Uranium/AI-power sleeve too crowded? — **YES, and it's really two themes.**

7 names for a 3.5% target forces sub-0.5% "confetti" positions that add complexity without moving the
book. It is **not one theme** — it mixes:

- **Nuclear fuel & reactors:** CCJ, BWXT, URC, SMR, and CEG (nuclear-fueled AI-power).
- **Electrification / grid:** GEV, ETN — a *different* factor (grid capex, data-center power build-out),
  not uranium.

**Recommendation:** SPLIT into (a) a **Nuclear** sleeve — concentrated in **CEG + BWXT + CCJ** (drop URC,
keep SMR only as a tiny option) — and (b) a small **Electrification** sleeve for GEV/ETN with its own
target, where the discipline is "trim the +87% winner (GEV), add quality (ETN) only on valuation." As one
blended 3.5% sleeve, hold **CEG, BWXT, CCJ** (74%) and let the other four be tokens or zeros.
