# OneGold Swiss Gold and Gold-Vehicle Review — 2026-09-08

## Decision

Keep the existing approximately five ounces of OneGold Switzerland Gold, but do **not** put the
entire remaining approximately $43,000 gold allocation into OneGold. Use a liquid, physically
backed U.S.-listed trust for the new purchase; **GLDM is the preferred implementation**. IAUM is
an equally defensible near-tie if its execution price is better at the time of purchase.

**Status:** Accepted by Hooman on 2026-09-08. This records the intended vehicle choice only; no
holding, trade, execution price or live portfolio change is asserted.

This creates useful custody and jurisdiction diversification across the existing OneGold Swiss
position, the existing Royal Canadian Mint MNT position, and a liquid U.S.-listed bullion trust.
It also avoids concentrating the entire 15% gold sleeve in one private platform and avoids paying
OneGold's materially wider exit spread on the additional $43,000.

## OneGold Assessment

OneGold Switzerland Gold is a credible **allocated pooled physical-gold account**, not a paper
futures product. OneGold says the metal is stored with MKS PAMP in Switzerland, insured against
physical loss through Lloyd's, reconciled daily, inventoried monthly and independently verified.
Its user agreement says customers have title to an allocated, pooled interest in tangible metal.

Important limitations:

- The customer does not own an identified retail bar that can simply be removed from the Swiss
  vault. The position is a fractional interest in larger pooled bars.
- Normal physical redemption sells the vaulted position and uses the proceeds to purchase a new
  APMEX retail product. It is not delivery of the same Swiss bar.
- The account relationship remains with a U.S. company, is governed by U.S./Delaware law and can
  be subject to account, KYC/AML, court-order and sanctions restrictions. Swiss vaulting is
  geographic diversification, not immunity from government action.
- Insurance covers physical theft/loss, not gold-price loss, platform access, legal restrictions or
  all possible counterparty outcomes.
- OneGold may change fees and reserves broad rights over account closure and buyback availability.

Verdict: **reasonable for a minority custody-diversification position, but not the best sole vehicle
for the entire gold sleeve.**

OneGold's redemption page says sales receive the current bid minus 0.30%. The public sell quote
appears to be the executable quoted proceeds, so the 0.30% should not automatically be added a
second time without checking the order preview. On $43,000:

- Published immediate spread: approximately **$716**.
- Three years of storage at 0.12%: approximately **$155**.
- Indicative three-year spread plus storage: approximately **$871**, or 2.0%.
- If the transaction preview charges another 0.30% outside the displayed sell quote, the total
  would instead be approximately **$1,000**.
- Physical redemption adds the premium of the selected APMEX coin/bar and may add international
  shipping, customs and tax costs.

Therefore the user's `$100/oz including storage` estimate is directionally reasonable over a
multi-year holding period, especially if eventual physical redemption is included, but the exact
amount depends on the exit date and whether the displayed sell quote already includes the 0.30%.

At roughly five ounces, the existing position's 0.12% percentage charge is above the $20 annual
minimum, so the minimum fee is not an economic concern.

## Can the Gold Be Delivered After Moving to Canada?

**Not confirmed strongly enough to rely on.** OneGold says international redemption is available
to countries supported by APMEX and that international shipping fees, customs and duties can apply.
The terms also require an accurate, verified shipping address. The public terms do not promise that
a U.S.-registered account can remain open without restriction after its owner becomes a Canadian
resident, nor do they promise delivery to every Canadian address/product combination.

Before adding more, obtain written answers from OneGold covering this exact scenario:

1. Can the existing account remain open after the residence and verified address change from the
   U.S. to Canada?
2. Can Switzerland Gold continue to be held and sold after that change?
3. Can an APMEX redemption be shipped to the verified Canadian address, and which products qualify?
4. What shipping, insurance, customs, GST/HST and product-premium costs would apply?
5. Can sale proceeds be wired directly to a Canadian USD bank account?

Even if the answer is yes, the customer receives newly purchased APMEX bullion, not the exact bars
held at MKS PAMP.

## Government-Seizure Claim

The claim that OneGold Swiss is meaningfully seizure-proof versus a physically backed ETF is too
strong. Foreign vault location reduces domestic-vault concentration and may complicate a purely
local physical seizure, but the ownership record and redemption channel remain controlled through
a U.S. platform. Courts and governments can freeze accounts, compel intermediaries, impose sanctions
or capital controls, and seize assets under lawful process. Personally held bullion can also be
seized under lawful process and creates theft, insurance and border-transport risks.

The useful distinction is therefore:

- **OneGold:** title to pooled allocated metal plus a private-platform redemption claim.
- **GLDM/IAUM/SGOL:** a security representing a fractional interest in a trust that owns allocated
  physical bullion; ordinary retail investors sell shares rather than withdraw bars.
- **Direct bullion:** possession and no platform redemption dependency, but higher transaction,
  storage, insurance and transport burdens.

OneGold improves custody diversification; it does not eliminate sovereign or intermediary risk.

## Vehicle Comparison

| Vehicle | Physical structure | Annual cost | Retail physical delivery | Fit for this portfolio |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| OneGold Swiss | Allocated, pooled Swiss bullion; MKS PAMP | 0.12% storage plus wide buy/sell spread | Via sale and new APMEX purchase | Keep existing five ounces; do not concentrate further |
| GLDM | Allocated physical bullion; 100% allocated at FY2025 year-end | 0.10% | No; creation units are institutional | **Best new purchase: deep liquidity and 0.01% recent median spread** |
| IAUM | Physical gold held for the trust, allocated by end of each business day | 0.09% | No; only authorized participants redeem baskets | Near-tie; choose if its live all-in execution is better |
| SGOL | Primarily allocated physical bullion | 0.17% | No practical retail redemption | Swiss custody, but redundant with existing OneGold |
| BAR | Allocated London Good Delivery bars | 0.1749% | No practical retail redemption | Sound, but no advantage over GLDM/IAUM here |
| OUNZ | Allocated physical gold | 0.25% | Yes, subject to delivery application and fees | Optional only if retail delivery is worth the extra cost |
| MNT/MNT.U | Direct beneficial interest in RCM gold; Canadian government-backed Mint obligation | 0.35% | Minimum 10,000 ETRs and at least 10 oz; fees apply | Existing diversification; do not add without U.S./Canada tax review |
| PHYS | Fully allocated RCM bullion | 0.39% MER | Roughly one 400-oz London Good Delivery bar minimum | Impractical redemption size; PFIC filing burden for U.S. persons |

All the U.S. physical-gold grantor trusts reviewed treat long-term gains attributable to gold as
collectibles gains, subject to a maximum 28% U.S. federal rate for individuals. Direct bullion is
generally in the same collectibles category. OneGold does not remove this tax issue. Canadian-listed
vehicles can introduce additional U.S. cross-border classification/reporting questions and should
not be added in a U.S. taxable account without specialist advice.

## Recommended Implementation

1. **Keep the existing approximately five ounces of OneGold Switzerland Gold.** It is already a
   meaningful Swiss-vault/custody-diversification position.
2. **Use GLDM for the remaining approximately $43,000.** At the current 0.10% fee, three years of
   sponsor fees on $43,000 are roughly $129, versus roughly $871 of OneGold spread plus storage at
   the current public quote, before physical-redemption costs.
3. Use a limit order during normal U.S. market hours and stage the purchase if desired for gold-price
   timing; staging does not change the vehicle decision.
4. Hold it in the U.S. taxable brokerage if this capital may help fund the house. Do not put likely
   house-sale proceeds in the RRSP merely to defer tax, because an RRSP withdrawal creates tax and
   access consequences. If the gold is unquestionably retirement-only, account placement should be
   reviewed with the cross-border tax adviser.
5. If direct small-lot physical delivery is an essential feature, allocate at most a minority of the
   new money to OUNZ instead of GLDM; do not add OUNZ merely because delivery exists.

This would leave the completed gold sleeve diversified among Swiss allocated pooled gold, Royal
Canadian Mint ETR exposure, a highly liquid U.S. physically backed trust, and small mining-equity
positions. It is cleaner than putting the entire sleeve at OneGold.

## House-Horizon Constraint

Gold can suffer multi-year drawdowns. The 15% gold sleeve should remain risk capital and must not be
counted as guaranteed down-payment principal. Any amount that must be available on a known three-year
date belongs in currency-matched cash, insured deposits or short high-quality government instruments.
