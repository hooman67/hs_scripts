## Design principles (why these numbers)

1. **Operationalize "I have no edge" → an ETF core.** The biggest change: a real broad-global-equity core
   (~22%) you currently lack. For a self-described poor forecaster, the market's return is the baseline;
   conviction bets are satellites around it.
2. **De-correlate.** Cut the commodity/real-asset megafactor from **37% → ~26% of total** by trimming the
   *cyclical* legs (silver, steel, excess uranium equity, nickel-never) and keeping the *hedge* leg (gold)
   + the one structural leg (copper) + quality energy.
3. **Size to conviction × evidence, not to enthusiasm.** Canadian energy (7/10) and gold-as-hedge keep
   real weight; supercycle (4/10), nickel (3.5), "oil=AI" (3) get little or none.
4. **Keep it house-safe.** 30% cash + TIPS + the separate $250k reserve keeps total vol ~6-7% and a crash
   ≤ ~15% of total. House purchasing power protected.
5. **Count Adobe.** ~$732k US-tech RSUs = your US-tech allocation is already enormous. The liquid book
   deliberately underweights US tech and overweights everything you're missing.
