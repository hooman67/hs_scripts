## PART 1 — THE MISSING MACRO AXIS (the most important finding)

**You are insured against only ONE kind of crash.**

| Crash type | Example | Your inflation sleeve (gold/commodities/TIPS) | What actually rises |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inflationary** | 2022 | ✅ protects you (gold +, TIPS +, commodities +) | you're covered |
| **Deflationary / recession** | 2008, 2020, 2018-Q4 | ❌ stays ~flat while stocks −20-34% | **govt bonds** (+6.4% IEF in COVID) |

Your cash doesn't *fall* in a deflationary crash, but it doesn't *rise* either — it just sits. The one
asset that reliably rallies when stocks crash on growth fear is **government bonds** — the thing your
USD-debasement conviction made you reject. And critically: **the deflationary/recession crash is the SAME
scenario that hits your Adobe RSUs** — so you're doubly exposed to the one outcome you have no hedge for.

**The nuance (long vs intermediate — the two agents differed, here's the reconciliation):**
- The blind-spot agent said **long-duration** (IEF/TLT/EDV) — most powerful equity-crash hedge.
- The asset-class agent said **intermediate** (IEI/IEF) — better risk/reward, less rate risk for a 3-5yr goal.
- **Reconciled recommendation: IEF (7-10yr Treasuries) as the compromise** — enough duration to rally
  hard in a recession, not so much you take a 2022-style beating if inflation returns. Optionally a small
  TLT/EDV satellite if you want maximum crash convexity. Your USD-debasement instinct against *long* bonds
  is directionally right; the fix is *intermediate*, not zero.
- **Utilities (XLU/VPU) are a two-for-one:** a bond-proxy (rallies when rates fall in a recession) that
  ALSO expresses your AI-power/electricity-demand thesis. This is the most on-brand disinflation hedge for you.

**This is the single highest-value addition in the completeness audit. Grade 8-10.**
