## Jargon key (read once — used throughout)

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **E&P** | Exploration & Production — a company that finds oil/gas underground and pumps it out. It sells a raw commodity; it does **not** set the price (a "price-taker"). |
| **Upstream** | The E&P part of the oil business (finding + producing), vs. midstream (pipelines) and downstream (refining/gas stations). EOG is pure upstream. |
| **BOE / BOE/d** | Barrel of Oil Equivalent. Oil, gas and NGLs are bundled into one unit (6,000 cubic feet of gas ≈ 1 barrel of oil, by energy). **BOE/d** = barrels per day. "MBoed" = **thousand** BOE/day. Higher **oil mix** = more valuable barrels (oil sells for far more per BOE than gas). |
| **NGLs** | Natural Gas Liquids (propane, butane, ethane) — a middle-value product between oil and dry gas. |
| **F&D cost** | Finding & Development cost — what it costs to add one barrel of new reserves. Lower = a more efficient, higher-return driller. |
| **Breakeven** | The oil price at which cash from operations just covers the capital budget + the base dividend. Below breakeven the company burns cash / dips into savings. |
| **ROCE / ROE** | Return On Capital Employed / Return On Equity — profit generated per dollar of capital / shareholder equity. Higher = more efficient use of capital. |
| **Reserve life / replacement** | Reserves ÷ annual production = years of runway. "Replacement ratio" = new reserves added ÷ produced; >100% means the tank is refilling faster than it drains. |
| **FCF** | Free Cash Flow = operating cash flow − capital spending. The cash actually available to pay dividends, buy back stock, or cut debt. |

# PART A — Business Primer (for a newcomer)

## A1. What EOG does — in plain language

EOG Resources drills for and sells **crude oil, natural gas, and NGLs** in the United States. It is a **large-cap independent E&P** (market cap **~$69.7B**, VERIFIED yfinance 2026-07-03) — "independent" meaning pure upstream, no gas stations or refineries. Its revenue is simply *volume produced × the market price of oil and gas*. It has **no pricing power**; when oil falls, EOG's revenue falls with it. That single fact drives ~80% of the investment thesis (see the companion deep_dive.md).

**Scale (VERIFIED, EDGAR/Q4-2025 8-K):** In Q4 2025 EOG produced **1,399.0 MBoed** (≈1.4 million barrels of oil equivalent per day), of which **546.1 MBbld was crude oil** — an oil mix of ~39%, high for a US shale operator (oil is the money-maker). FY2025 revenue was **$22.63B** and net income **$4.98B**.

**Where it operates (the basins).** EOG is deliberately **multi-basin**, which diversifies its geology and gives it options on where to put each dollar:

- **Delaware Basin (Permian, West Texas / New Mexico)** — its largest oil engine; the core of US shale.
- **Eagle Ford (South Texas)** — a mature, high-return oil play EOG pioneered.
- **Powder River Basin (Wyoming)** — an emerging oil area.
- **Utica shale (Ohio)** — a **new leg** added Aug 2025 via the **$5.6B Encino Acquisition Corp acquisition** (a combined oil + gas play). This is the biggest deal in EOG's history and shows in the balance sheet (long-term debt jumped from ~$3.5B to ~$7.9B in Q3 2025 — VERIFIED EDGAR).
- **Dorado (South Texas)** — a **new dry-gas play** positioned to feed Gulf Coast **LNG export** and rising **AI/data-center power** demand — EOG's bet on the gas upcycle.
- **Trinidad + small international** — a legacy gas position (minor).

**Why "premium drilling" matters.** EOG only sanctions a well if it clears a **hard return hurdle** (historically framed as a minimum ~30%+ direct after-tax rate of return at a conservative oil-price deck, e.g. flat ~$45–50 WTI — UNVERIFIED, EOG investor framing / secondary). "Double-premium" wells clear an even higher hurdle. This is a **discipline filter**: EOG would rather drill fewer, better wells than chase production growth. It is the single behavioral thing that separates EOG from the shale pack.

## A2. Management & culture — the distinctive part

**Origin.** EOG was **Enron Oil & Gas**, spun out of Enron in **1999** (it kept the initials but shed the parent well before Enron's 2001 collapse). It has spent 25 years building the opposite of Enron's reputation: conservative, low-debt, engineering-led.

**Decentralized, returns-first culture.** EOG is known in the industry for a **flat, decentralized structure** — decisions pushed down to regional geoscience/engineering teams, who compete internally for capital by proving their wells clear the return hurdle. This is a *culture*, not a slogan: it is visible in the numbers below (highest returns, lowest debt, disciplined capex through the cycle). (Culture description: UNVERIFIED / widely reported; the *numbers* that corroborate it are VERIFIED below.)

**Capital-discipline track record (the reputation that matters for a value investor).** The US shale industry's historical sin was **growth-at-any-cost**: outspending cash flow, piling on debt, drilling marginal wells to grow production, and blowing up when prices fell. EOG is the standout counter-example:

EOG's stated model pairs a *sustainable, growing base dividend* with *special/variable dividends + buybacks* on top — so the base is safe and the extra flexes with the cycle. (EOG has a multi-decade record of never cutting the regular dividend — UNVERIFIED / company/secondary; the +8% raise is VERIFIED.)
- **Returned 100% of 2025 free cash flow to shareholders** (VERIFIED, Q4-2025 release): ~**$2.1B dividends + ~$2.5B buybacks** on **$4.66B FCF**.
- **Fortress balance sheet:** net debt only **~$4.08B** at 31-Mar-2026 even *after* funding a ~$5.6B acquisition largely with cash + modest new debt (VERIFIED, Q1-2026 8-K).

## A3. Business model & moat — what "quality E&P" means

An E&P has **no moat in the classic sense** — it can't raise prices, and its product is a global commodity. So "quality" and "moat" for an E&P are **relative and cost-based**: the ability to *out-earn peers at the same oil price* and *survive troughs that impair weaker operators*. EOG is considered best-in-class on every axis that defines that:

1. **Low finding & development cost.** EOG's premium-drilling filter means it only develops the cheapest, highest-return barrels. Lower F&D cost → higher return per barrel at any given oil price. (Directionally VERIFIED by its class-leading ROE/ROCE, below.)
2. **Deep, self-funded premium inventory.** Multi-basin footprint + the Utica/Dorado additions give many years of drillable locations that clear the hurdle — so it isn't forced to drill marginal wells or overpay for acreage as core inventory depletes (the structural risk for Permian-only pure-plays).
3. **Self-sourced materials + tech/data edge.** EOG is known for **self-sourcing sand, water, and materials** and for a strong **in-house data/analytics** capability to optimize well placement and completion design — squeezing more oil out per dollar. (UNVERIFIED / company framing; corroborated by cost leadership.)
4. **Fortress balance sheet = optionality.** Lowest leverage in the peer set means EOG can keep drilling (and buying back stock) through a downturn while over-levered peers cut — so it *gains relative ground in busts*.

**Bottom line on "quality":** EOG is the **highest-quality large US shale E&P** — the trifecta of *lowest cost + deepest inventory + lowest debt*. That is a real, durable **relative** advantage. It is **not** a secular compounder with pricing power; it is a best-in-class operator of a cyclical commodity business. (Consistent with the deep_dive.md quality verdict of 9/10.)

**How EOG differs from CNOOC (Hooman's other energy name — covered separately).** Both are oil & gas E&Ps, but they are almost mirror-opposite bets: **EOG = US onshore shale, reports in USD, no state control, capital returns via growing dividend + buybacks + specials, moderate ~6% dividend-equivalent total return, valuation ~5.9× EV/EBITDA.** **CNOOC (0883.HK) = Chinese state-controlled offshore producer, HKD-denominated, higher raw dividend yield (~6%), cheaper (~3.3× EV/EBITDA), but carries China state-ownership / geopolitical / sanctions-jurisdiction risk** (see the separate CNOOC file and the `canadian_investability.md` note — Hooman is a **Canadian citizen**, which changes the OFAC analysis). For a two-name energy sleeve, EOG is the *quality/USD/rule-of-law* leg and CNOOC is the *deep-value/EM/higher-yield* leg. A separate agent covers CNOOC in full.

## B4. What to watch (in priority order)

1. **Oil & gas prices — DOMINANT (~80% of the thesis).** EOG is a price-taker; everything else is second-order. WTI **~$68.78**, Henry Hub **~$3.25** (oilprice.com, 4-hr delayed ~2026-07-03). A push to **$50–55 WTI** (recession, OPEC+ oversupply) would cut FCF hard and the "cheap 5.9× EV/EBITDA" would re-rate to ordinary; a move back toward **$80+** would make it look cheap in hindsight. The 2025 earnings slide ($6.40B → $4.98B) is the live evidence of price dominance. **This is the number to track above all others.** (See deep_dive.md §2 for the full peak-cycle test — conclusion: oil has *already* normalized ~30% off its 2022 peak, so this is **not** a classic "low P/E on peak earnings" trap.)
2. **Capital discipline holding through the cycle.** The whole EOG thesis rests on management *not* reverting to shale's growth-at-any-cost habit. Watch that: (a) the **2026 capex stays in the guided $6.3–6.7B band** (VERIFIED guidance) rather than creeping up to chase the +13% production growth; (b) new wells keep clearing the premium return hurdle; (c) any future M&A clears the same hurdle rather than empire-building (the $5.6B Utica/Encino deal is the recent test — watch its type-curve results validate the price paid).
3. **Inventory depth / runway.** Shale wells decline fast, so EOG must continually replace reserves. **YE2025 proved reserves 5.51 Bboe (+16% YoY), 254% replacement ex-price-revisions** (VERIFIED, EDGAR reserves reconciliation + Q4-2025 8-K). The Utica + Dorado additions extend runway; watch that core Permian/Eagle Ford inventory quality doesn't erode and that Utica type curves prove out.
4. **Dorado gas ramp + the gas upcycle.** EOG's Dorado dry-gas play and Utica gas are levered to **LNG export growth + AI/data-center power demand**. Realized gas rose from $3.00 (Q4 2025) to $3.76/Mcf (Q1 2026) — VERIFIED. A sustained gas upcycle is an under-appreciated call option; a gas glut is the downside.
5. **M&A / balance-sheet use.** With the fortress balance sheet, watch whether management deploys it into disciplined bolt-ons vs. a large, richly-priced deal. Track record here is good, but it's the one place discipline could slip.
