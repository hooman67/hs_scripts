# LONG_TERM_PLANS — Roadmap / Where This Is Heading

Last updated: 2026-07-01 by Claude (Opus 4.8).

## Vision

A repeatable, data-backed pipeline that surfaces cheap-but-quality stocks for Hooman to
evaluate, combining top-down (cheap sectors → leaders) and bottom-up (cheap quality
companies) value investing, with a clear audit trail from every number to its source.

## Phased roadmap (draft — refine after methodology is chosen)

1. **Phase 1 — One-shot screen & report.** Stand up the data pipeline, run the funnel once,
   produce a ranked candidate list with deep dives on the top finalists.
2. **Phase 2 — Refinement.** Incorporate Hooman's feedback on what "quality" and "cheap"
   should weight; tune the scorecard; expand/contract the universe.
3. **Phase 3 — Recurring monitor (optional).** Re-run the screen on a schedule (e.g. monthly
   or quarterly), flag newly-cheap sectors/names and thesis-breaking changes on holdings.
4. **Phase 4 — Backtest / validate (optional, stretch).** Sanity-check that the screen's
   historical picks would have behaved reasonably — guard against overfitting.

## Open questions for later
