# Long-Term Plan

Last updated: 2026-09-21.

## Phase 1 — purchase foundation

- Verify exact trim/specification differences, warranty/CPO rules, recalls, common problems,
  California OTD mechanics, new-equivalent pricing, and applicable Kia-owner incentives.
- Define the travel-value threshold after real local and Southern California pricing is known.

## Phase 2 — market map and shortlist

- Collect a de-duplicated regional sample and establish normalized price ranges.
- Screen active candidates using title/history, mileage, seller, warranty, condition, and OTD
  evidence.
- Maintain a small ranked shortlist with expiry and price-change tracking.

## Phase 3 — finalist diligence

- With explicit approval, obtain written OTD quotes and VIN-specific documents.
- Arrange independent inspections and verify repair, recall, tire, brake, key, cosmetic, and
  service status.
- Compare cash and financing by total cost and preserve clear walk-away conditions.

## Phase 4 — transaction and ownership baseline

- Re-verify all terms immediately before travel or payment.
- Preserve the buyer's order, title/registration records, warranty contracts, inspection, service
  history, and due-maintenance baseline.
- Track early warranty deadlines and complete any immediate maintenance or recall work.

## Maintenance cadence

While actively shopping, refresh finalists at least twice weekly and immediately before any
travel. Use `/v_check` in Claude Code or `$v-check` in Codex for the standard refresh and change
report. Rebuild the market baseline when the sample becomes stale or incentives materially change.
The standard `$v-check` remains Kia-specific; refresh Toyota CPO Sienna inventory and Toyota's
95121 offer API separately whenever the Sienna alternative is reconsidered or before committing to
the Kia finalist.
