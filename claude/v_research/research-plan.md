# Research Plan — Eight-Seat Kia Carnival Hybrid Deal Search

## 1. Research questions

1. What are the current out-the-door baselines for new and leftover-new 2025–2027 Carnival Hybrid
   LXS, EX, SX, and SX Prestige vehicles near ZIP 95121?
2. What are fair-market prices for used/CPO eight-seat Carnival Hybrids after normalizing mileage,
   condition, history, warranty, and seller quality?
3. Which active candidates provide the best risk-adjusted value within roughly five hours' drive
   of San Jose, including Sacramento and Fresno/Visalia but excluding Los Angeles?
4. Which model-year issues, recalls, equipment differences, and warranty rules materially change
   long-term ownership risk or value?
5. Does buying lightly used still beat new/leftover-new after warranty, depreciation, incentives,
   financing or leasing, and what evidence is required before committing?
6. Does a 2021+ Toyota Sienna Platinum, Limited, XSE, or XLE provide better risk-adjusted value,
   including hybrid fuel savings and AWD, under the same geography, condition, and OTD constraints?

## 2. Confidence and status labels

- **VERIFIED:** directly supported by current primary evidence, or independently confirmed by
  multiple reliable sources. Include the URL/document, retrieval date, and scope.
- **USER-CONFIRMED:** a preference or decision explicitly provided by Hooman.
- **ASSUMED:** a transparent interpretation or estimate. State how it will be verified.
- **UNVERIFIED:** plausible but materially unsupported; cannot drive a purchase recommendation.
- **ACTIVE:** availability confirmed recently on the seller's own inventory and, for travel-stage
  candidates, directly reconfirmed.
- **STALE/SOLD:** no longer available or not recently verifiable; retain only as market evidence.

## 3. Candidate evidence schema

For every serious listing record:

- model year, exact trim, VIN, stock number, and VIN/build verification;
- seller legal/display name, address, seller type, franchise status, ratings pattern, and material
  complaint or enforcement signals;
- seller inventory URL plus discovery URL, retrieval timestamp, and last availability check;
- advertised price, price history, discount conditions, and financing requirements;
- mileage, exterior/interior color, seating configuration, and material equipment omissions;
- title brand, owner count, personal/rental/fleet use, accident/damage record, service history, open
  recalls, and geographic history;
- Kia CPO status or exact third-party warranty terms, exclusions, deductible, administrator, and
  transferability;
- known visible defects, tire/brake condition, keys, manuals, accessories, odors, warning lights,
  and inspection result;
- estimated California out-the-door cost and a low/high uncertainty range;
- travel cost/time and return-trip feasibility;
- comparable vehicles used for valuation;
- risk flags, evidence gaps, confidence, and next verification action.

## 4. Geographic search

Search outward from ZIP 95121 in tiers:

1. Bay Area and nearby Northern California markets suitable for an easy inspection;
2. broader Northern/Central California within roughly five hours' normal driving, including the
   Sacramento and Fresno/Visalia corridors;
3. exclude Los Angeles and farther markets from the active shortlist.

Do not recommend shipping. A remote candidate must clear stronger evidence gates before travel.

## 5. Pricing method

Build four baselines before labeling a listing a “deal”:

1. current-model comparable-new eight-seat hybrid transaction/quote baseline by trim;
2. leftover-new 2025/2026 hybrid transaction/quote baseline;
3. normalized market range for each used model year and mileage band; and
4. the candidate's estimated out-the-door and acquisition cost.

Use this comparison:

`effective acquisition cost = written OTD price + travel + immediate maintenance/tires − only verified usable incentives`

Keep finance interest separate from OTD and compare total paid under realistic payoff scenarios.
Report both vehicle price and OTD; never switch between them silently. Treat $40,000 OTD as the
target and $45,000 OTD as the absolute ceiling. Compare cash, finance, and lease structures without
letting monthly-payment framing conceal total cash outlay, residual, mileage limits, or fees.

The value score must account for year, mileage, history, condition, warranty, seller confidence,
and travel. A cheaper high-risk vehicle is not automatically a better deal.

## 6. Reliability and authenticity gates

Before shortlist status:

- VIN and trim corroborated independently;
- title/history reviewed with no automatic disqualifier;
- official recall status checked by VIN when possible;
- warranty status and CPO meaning verified from actual documents;
- model-year issues researched from authoritative and corroborated sources;
- advertised price qualifications decoded; and
- dealer identity and reputation checked beyond its own testimonials.

Before finalist/travel status:

- seller confirms physical availability and possession;
- complete written buyer's order or itemized OTD quote obtained;
- history report, service evidence, photos/video, keys, and visible defects reviewed;
- no surprise mandatory accessories or contingent discounts;
- independent inspection accepted and arranged, or equivalent buyer-protective evidence approved;
- California registration/tax handling and temporary operating documents understood; and
- walk-away conditions written down before travel.

## 7. Deal tiers

- **Exceptional:** meaningfully below the normalized market after all costs, with strong history,
  condition, seller, and warranty evidence and no unresolved material risk.
- **Good:** competitively below or near the lower end of the normalized market with evidence strong
  enough to support long ownership.
- **Fair:** market-consistent but not compelling after risk and acquisition costs.
- **False economy:** low headline price offset by mileage, damage, weak warranty, add-ons, financing,
  travel, condition, or unresolved evidence.

No vehicle can be rated Exceptional or Good while a material title, accident, OTD, inspection, or
seller-identity question remains unresolved.

## 8. Research sequence

1. Verify new-car baseline, model-year differences, warranty/CPO rules, recalls, and known risks.
2. Establish regional market ranges with a broad, de-duplicated sample.
3. Screen active listings against hard exclusions and normalize total costs.
4. Deep-check the strongest candidates and maintain a ranked shortlist.
5. With Hooman's approval, obtain written quotes and arrange inspections for finalists.
6. Refresh active listings and prices immediately before any travel or transaction.

- add durable findings to `LEARNINGS.md`;
- put only accepted choices in `DECISIONS.md`;
- update `listings/SUMMARY.md` and mark status changes;

- update `LONG_TERM_PLANS.md` if the acquisition or ownership roadmap changes.
