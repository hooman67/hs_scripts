## Conclusion

**[RESEARCHER RECOMMENDATION — HIGH CONFIDENCE]** Current Sienna pricing does not produce a better
purchase deal than the Tustin 2024 Kia CPO SX Prestige at $42,297.95 quoted OTD. Toyota's hybrid
efficiency, AWD availability, resale strength, and 10-year/150,000-mile hybrid-battery warranty are
real advantages, but the market charges heavily for them. No clean-summary AWD premium-trim Toyota
CPO in the verified official inventory sample plausibly fits the $45,000 OTD ceiling.

The only premium Sienna close to the ceiling is a **2021 Limited FWD, 45,009 miles, $40,510
advertised**, estimated at **$45.0k-$45.2k OTD**. It is a credible alternative if seven seats and
FWD are acceptable, but not an obviously better deal: it is three model years older and has about
23,700 more miles than Tustin for roughly $2,700 more OTD. Its fuel savings can offset much of that
gap during a ten-year hold.

Leasing changes only the short-hold answer. A current XSE AWD estimate is about $775/month under a
$0-down, 36-month/10,000-mile setting, slightly below the comparable Kia lease. But the estimated
taxed lease-end buyout makes total nominal lease-to-own cost about $72,100 before buyout/DMV fees.
Because Hooman would probably buy at lease end, buying—not leasing—remains the better structure.

## Financing and cash

**[VERIFIED 2026-10-07]** Star One Credit Union publicly listed new and used auto APRs from 4.75%
to 8.75%, up to 84 months, subject to credit qualification. Toyota showed no public Sienna special
APR in 95121. A 780 score is likely top-tier but does not guarantee the floor rate; vehicle age,
term, LTV, income, and lender underwriting still apply.

At the 4.75% advertised floor, financing the estimated $45,110 OTD 2021 Limited for 60 months is
about **$846/month and $5,657 total interest**; 72 months is about **$721/month and $6,822 interest**.
Financing Tustin's $42,297.95 quote for 60 months is about **$793/month and $5,305 interest**.

Because the current safe-cash comparison is about 2.4%-2.8% after tax, a 4.75% loan is not expected
to beat paying cash. Financing can still be rational as deliberately purchased liquidity, but it is
not free optionality. Obtain an outside preapproval before letting either dealer quote financing;
compare identical selling prices and reject prepayment penalties or finance-only add-ons.

## Long-hold economics and risk

**[VERIFIED]** EPA figures are 36 mpg combined for Sienna FWD, 35 mpg for Sienna AWD, and 22 mpg
for the 2024 gas Carnival. At 10,000 miles/year, the Sienna saves about 169-177 gallons/year. At
$4-$5/gallon, that is about **$675-$884/year**, or **$6,750-$8,840 over ten years** before discounting
and changes in fuel price/mileage.

**[VERIFIED]** Toyota states that hybrid batteries from model year 2020 onward carry a 10-year/
150,000-mile limited warranty from first use; hybrid-related components carry 8-year/100,000-mile
coverage. Toyota CPO hybrid inspection uses 174 points. Gold CPO advertises 12-month/12,000-mile
limited comprehensive coverage from certified purchase and a 7-year/100,000-total-vehicle-mile
limited powertrain warranty measured from original use. A 2021 vehicle therefore has materially
less CPO powertrain calendar coverage remaining than the 2024 Kia, so obtain the original in-service
date and actual warranty supplement.

**[INTERPRETATION]** The fuel savings make the 2021 Limited economically competitive over a long
hold even though its purchase proposition is worse on age/miles. They do not erase the need to
inspect a first-model-year example carefully or resolve every recall. NHTSA's model query currently
shows four campaigns for the 2021 Sienna Hybrid; VIN-specific completion remains required.
