## Executive synthesis

- **VERIFIED:** Both target years use a 3.5-liter V6, 8-speed automatic, and front-wheel drive; the
  SX Prestige trim includes dual sunroofs and Bose 12-speaker audio.
- **VERIFIED:** A 2022 SX Prestige has seven-passenger VIP Lounge seating and RSE. A 2023 defaults
  to eight-passenger Slide-Flex seating; VIP Lounge seating was a no-cost package and RSE a
  separate $1,000 option. **USER-CONFIRMED 2026-10-04:** either seating layout is acceptable and
  neither VIP seating nor RSE is required.
- **VERIFIED:** Kia CPO materially improves the transferred powertrain coverage but does not make
  inspection, history, and recall diligence optional.
- **VERIFIED:** Both years are included in a 2026 fuel-leak/fire recall. Other model-year recalls
  exist, so VIN-specific completion evidence is mandatory.
- **ASSUMED:** The $40,000 San Jose OTD target requires a dealer-fee-inclusive price around
  $35,800–$36,000; the $45,000 hard ceiling requires roughly $40,300–$40,500. Obtain a written
  buyer's order before relying on either threshold.

## Warranty and certification

- **VERIFIED:** Kia's 10-year/100,000-mile limited powertrain warranty applies to the original
  purchaser and to Kia CPO purchasers, subject to the written warranty terms. Do not assume the
  same remaining powertrain coverage for an ordinary subsequent-owner non-CPO sale.
- **VERIFIED:** Kia CPO advertises a 165-point inspection, remaining 10-year/100,000-mile limited
  powertrain coverage measured from original in-service date/zero miles, one-year/12,000-mile
  Platinum coverage, and roadside/towing/rental/travel-breakdown benefits.
- **ASSUMED:** CPO status is valuable at this age, especially near or over 60,000 miles, but
  a clean independent inspection and actual CPO paperwork remain necessary.

## Recall and complaint evidence

**VERIFIED model-level recalls:** NHTSA returned six recall campaigns for the 2022 Carnival and four
for the 2023. Both years include power-sliding-door, roof-molding, tow-hitch-harness, and April 2026
fuel-pipe/rail leak issues in at least part of the production population. The 2022 list additionally
contains a passenger-door latch actuator campaign and an earlier damaged-plastic fuel-rail-tube
campaign. Applicability and remedy completion must be checked by VIN.

The newest common campaign is NHTSA `26V232000`, Kia campaign `SC368`, covering a fuel leak at the
pipe connection with fire risk; the remedy is inspection and tightening or replacement as needed.

**UNVERIFIED as a comparative reliability rate:** NHTSA's complaint API returned 134 complaint
records for model-year 2022 and 50 for 2023. Structure/door, electrical, visibility, fuel/propulsion,
and engine categories appear in the data. Complaint counts lack an exposure denominator and the
2022 model has been on the road longer, so these numbers cannot prove that one year is more reliable.
They do justify focused door, fuel-system, electrical, and body/trim checks.

## California purchase-cost baseline

- **VERIFIED:** California's published San Jose sales/use-tax rate is 10.000% as of July 1, 2026.
- **VERIFIED:** California DMV states that dealer-purchased used vehicles incur use tax based on the
  new owner's city/county and that VLF is 0.65% of purchase price/value, alongside other registration
  fees.
- **VERIFIED:** California dealers must show a used buyer the NMVTIS report, and the state's certified-
  used rules prohibit “certified” labeling for several disqualifying histories including branded
  titles, frame damage, odometer issues, lemon-law buybacks, and as-is sales.
- **ASSUMED planning method:** use sale price + disclosed dealer fees, multiply that basis by 1.10,
  then add
  $450–$650 provisional DMV/title/registration. Replace this with the written buyer's order and DMV
  calculator before ranking a finalist.
- **VERIFIED new comparison:** Kia lists base MSRP plus destination at $52,335 for 2025, $53,035
  for 2026, and $53,135 for 2027 gas SX Prestige models. Current discounted leftover listings
  remain roughly $51.8k–$54.2k OTD, above the $45k hard ceiling.

## Kia owner and finance incentives

**UNVERIFIED / do not count:** Kia's public offers page for ZIP 95121 did not expose a current
existing-owner loyalty benefit applicable to a used or CPO 2022/2023 Carnival. Manufacturer offers
are frequently model-, region-, credit-, and purchase-type-specific. Owning a 2024 Niro PHEV should
be disclosed when requesting a finalist quote, but no discount belongs in deal math unless the
dealer supplies the current program number, eligibility, amount, expiration, and stacking rules in
writing.

## Contradictions and evidence gaps

- The initial premise that a 2023 SX Prestige always has VIP lounge seats is false; it was a
  no-cost option, not standard equipment. Hooman subsequently confirmed either seating layout is
  acceptable and RSE is optional.
- NHTSA VIN decoding validates VIN structure, model year, make, model, and a combined `SX, SX
  Prestige` value; it does not distinguish the exact trim. The original window sticker/build sheet
  remains necessary.
- Model-level recall lists do not prove that a specific VIN has an open recall.
- History-provider badges and dealer summaries are not substitutes for reviewing the full report.
- The current live market sample is limited by anti-bot blocks at several major marketplaces.
