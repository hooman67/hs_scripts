Rules C-Tech should apply when reviewing

No repeated visual families

A visual family may appear only once across each ten-prompt bank, even when the object moves between foreground and background.

Text and faces

Avoid readable text, portraits, human faces, branded objects, recognizable characters, and additional digital devices.

Populated storage

Shelving, display ledges, and pegboards must be visibly populated with other approved, declared prop families.

Camera-aware density

Close-ups stay compact; medium views establish one coherent background zone; wide views may use layered room detail.
