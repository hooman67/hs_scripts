## Method and limits

Relevance and quality are separate axes: source support may decrease as candidate settings broaden, but camera feasibility and example-compatible prose remain mandatory. A score is a rubric judgment, not a probability or validated image metric. Existing V2 text is scored by the same criteria, never automatically assigned100. No unsupported evidence is invented to justify a creative scene.

Two independent tag-conditioned banks per source preserve the agreed runtime contract. They are compared by source and relevance stratum, not described as identical scenes under two cameras. The batch will contain540 candidate texts and select108 images; generation expansion beyond this is out of scope. Close crops can support less visible diversity than wide views; changing the family label alone does not create a different image.

The proposed rubric weights setting30, activity30, visible work materials20 and use context20. Fixed discrete anchors and exact evidence are in the root-authored system template. Unsupported primary context/activity is labeled creative_departure and cannot score above59; unsupported material/setting inventions remain visible even in otherwise grounded candidates. A zero-prop crop cannot receive credit for paperwork omitted from its final text. Scores can tie and a weak source need not produce high scores.

Selection uses rank strata1–10,11–20,21–30, two candidates each. Pick the highest rank in each stratum, then prefer visible-signature distance from already selected candidates; break ties by novel family then rank. This is a reproducible sampling heuristic followed by semantic review, not a validated diversity metric. Strata are relative positions, not assertions of high/medium/low absolute support.
