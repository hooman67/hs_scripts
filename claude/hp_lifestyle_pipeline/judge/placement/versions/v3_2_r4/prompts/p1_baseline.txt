EVIDENCE-FIRST CONTRACT
Begin with the full image to establish object ownership and the station. Then inspect every supplied A tile for relevant local relationships and use B to resolve intrinsic product features. Return to the full image before assigning appearance severity. Do not claim native-pixel coverage if tiles were not supplied. Do not adopt a scene description's furniture category when the image shows something else.
For each distinctive in-scope cue, preserve a concise witness in the existing evidence text: object/relationship; visible cue; appearance effect; alternative and its relevance; retained, rejected or unresolved disposition. This is an evidence summary, not a reasoning transcript. A rejected acquisition cue needs a specific visual correction. An all-clear component should identify the relationships actually inspected and important hidden regions, not certify the whole assembly from one sound part.
An alternative can rebut the cue, rebut its awkward appearance, or only limit a physical explanation. State which. Possible use, possible construction, good contact and a suitable activity do not by themselves rebut a visible registration or contour concern. Conversely, a detectable difference, preferred arrangement or uncertainty alone is not a defect. There is no concern quota.

VISUAL TESTS
For product/support alignment, distinguish chassis width, chassis depth, support perimeter and support frame. Compare corresponding directions at the product's location. Describe the exposed working-edge strip across the footprint, together with readable base and support boundaries. Neither changing margins, unequal slopes nor opposite slope signs alone establishes a defect. Judge whether the extended relationship is visually compatible, mildly misregistered or conspicuously conflicting. A substantial appearance conflict may be material even when diagonal use is physically possible. Do not require every table end or prove incompatible vanishing points. Screens, trays and grain are not base axes. Curved supports have no mandatory rectangular axis.
For furniture, inspect relevant exposed silhouettes and their visible continuations, thickness changes, joins and terminations. Name the affected object. Do not substitute a sound table apron for inspection of a seating contour. A localized awkward projection or discontinuity can be an appearance concern without proving an extra member or impossible joint. Normal rounded returns, bevels, deep aprons, tapered members, fitted counters and asymmetry remain legitimate when the visible continuation supports them. Hidden attachments and missing supports must not be invented. Crop boundaries and occlusion are not defects.
For use, establish the functional front, actual approach, prop footprints and seating role. A laptop deck extends from hinge toward its user, including rear views. Ordinary chair angle, lateral offset or imperfect centering is harmless. A station conflict needs the combined operating direction, associated position and usable edge, not chair pose alone. Printers commonly permit standing use and require no aligned chair. Distinguish lateral props from intrusions into the operating strip. Minor crowding need not prove universal obstruction. Do not invent another seat, future movement or an off-frame route. Near-wall placement and closed lids do not prove blocked operation; do not invent service dimensions or mechanisms.
For support and staging, inspect suitability, siting, connected allocation and visible activity relationships. A chair or notebook does not establish an occupied workstation. Clean dedicated surfaces and negative space can be credible. Use bare_staged_setting only for an affirmative display-like immediate arrangement, not an accessory quota, blank wall, closed cabinet or missing paper stack.
For scale and contact, account for depth and inspect all exposed load-bearing boundaries. One small prop is not a ruler. Reference-consistent recesses and projecting trays are not floating bases. Soft shadows are valid. A specific awkward tangent or apparent intersection can be penalized without proving collision; ordinary background overlap cannot. Hidden contact remains unknown.

OWNERSHIP AND RETENTION
support_scenario owns support role, siting, allocation and supported staging. access_usability owns reach, operating paths and user-area crowding. orientation_alignment owns product/support registration and established station-direction or furniture-layout conflicts. scale_perspective owns size, plane inconsistency and support/seating contours. contact_integration owns contact and spatial integration.
Keep independently supported concerns separately described, including multiple concerns in one component. Assign each underlying defect one primary owner. Component severity is the greatest independently supported severity, never a sum; confidence follows its governing finding. Good findings elsewhere do not cancel it. Union only independently supported active codes. Distinguish furniture_axis_conflict for a demonstrated directional relationship from furniture_layout_conflict for arrangement/grouping; never use either merely to rename chair offset.
Lighting preference, branding, decor, negative-space balance, pictorial flatness and prompt adherence are not placement penalties without an independent allowed spatial defect. A visible architectural-junction or ancillary mounting-height concern without a suitable code may be noted as a scope gap, not forced into an unrelated code. Do not claim to have read external feedback.

CALIBRATION
0 means no supported visible concern in assessable evidence. 1 means a supported minor appearance concern, explicitly coded and eligible for the existing component reduction. 2 means clear material unnaturalness requiring correction; 3 major compromise; 4 impossible/effectively nonfunctional placement. Physical impossibility is not required for 1-3. Do not cap a material discrepancy at 1. Confidence high/medium/low describes evidence independently of severity. Do not inflate confidence to satisfy consensus. A concrete minor concern may have low confidence; missing evidence alone is not a concern.
Visibility observed means the essential relationship is exposed; partial means some is assessable; unobservable requires severity null, confidence low and no codes. Otherwise severity is integer 0-4. Severity 0 has empty codes; positive severity requires supported codes. Preserve weights 20/30/20/15/15, the existing scorer, material-failure rule at severity at least 2 with medium/high confidence, and ensemble behavior. Emit no scores or verdicts.
