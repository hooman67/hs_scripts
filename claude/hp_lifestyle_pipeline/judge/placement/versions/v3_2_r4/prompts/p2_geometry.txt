ACQUISITION-TO-FINDING CONTRACT
Establish object ownership in the full view. Inspect every supplied tile for relevant local contours, boundaries and spatial junctions, then return to the full view. Tiles are repeated views, not independent votes. Tile boundaries are not object terminations. If the detail is unresolved or tiles are absent, state the limit.
In each criterion's existing evidence string, preserve concise witness records for distinctive cues: object/relationship; literal observation; appearance effect. In counter_evidence, state whether the alternative contradicts the cue, makes its visible appearance acceptable, or only limits a physical diagnosis. In consequence, retain or reject each distinct candidate with a short reason. Use limit for unresolved ownership or visibility. This is an evidence summary, not a reasoning transcript. Correct acquisition errors rather than copying them.
Do not collapse multiple independently supported concerns into one vague whole-scene adjective. An all-clear criterion names what was inspected and what remains hidden. No anomaly quota applies. Another object's sound construction cannot rebut a localized contour, and feasible operation cannot rebut registration.

SUPPORT_CONSTRUCTION
Inspect the support and associated seating as separate objects. Follow exposed silhouettes, thickness bands, member continuations, joins and terminations. Report the actual affected owner and contour. A local projection, doubled-looking termination or uneven continuation can support minor awkwardness without proving a separate extra member or structural impossibility. Ordinary rounded returns, bevels, deep aprons, tapered/splayed members, fitted counters and asymmetry can be valid; use their visible continuation as counter-evidence rather than simply naming a possible design. Do not invent hidden attachments or defects. Missing support requires affirmative exposed evidence, not cropped legs, hidden seats or invisible hardware. Do not prescribe member counts. A weak rotation hypothesis does not prove construction failure.

RIGID_ALIGNMENT
Keep product body/base and supporting surface as separate rigid groups. Compare corresponding width and depth relationships at their actual image positions. Describe the working-edge strip across the footprint and the readable product/support boundaries together. Equal slopes and uniform margins are not required. Different slopes, opposite slope signs and changing margins alone are insufficient. Judge the extent of the combined visual registration: compatible, mildly awkward or conspicuously conflicting. Physical diagonal placement may remain possible in all three; that possibility does not determine the appearance band. A clear material discrepancy remains material despite contact or access. One reliable family can suffice without every table end. Do not invent calibrated yaw or incompatible vanishing points. Screens, trays and grain are not chassis axes; curved supports have no obligatory rectangular axis. If the support itself is unreliable, retain only the appearance actually established. Seating layout belongs to use discovery.

RELATIVE_SCALE_PERSPECTIVE
Assess depth-aware size and combined plane relationships. Product prominence, reference-render size, one small prop and cropped furniture dimensions are not adequate scale evidence. An oblique rectangle normally projects as a non-rectangular outline. Use conflicting_perspective only for a supported inconsistent plane appearance, not decorative flatness or a generic difference in slopes. Separate it from rigid rotation and contour malformation; do not score the same cue repeatedly.

PRODUCT_CONTACT
Inspect all exposed bearing boundaries and relevant nearby product/prop or prop/wall junctions. B distinguishes intrinsic recesses, support extensions, displays and trays from floating gaps. Soft shadows are valid; hidden contact remains unknown. A specific crowded tangent, conflicting depth cue or apparent interpenetration may support minor appearance awkwardness without proving collision or detachment. Ordinary background overlap, frame thickness and mounting shadow alone are not defects. Good contact at one edge does not certify another. General lighting and branding are outside this criterion.

OWNERSHIP AND CALIBRATION
Each underlying defect has one primary geometry owner. Preserve secondary descriptive manifestations without duplicate penalties. Multiple independent concerns within one criterion remain separately described; severity is their greatest supported severity, not their sum. Confidence follows the governing finding, not unrelated clear evidence. The final reconciler handles overlap with use findings; these are discovery findings, not extra scored votes.
Severity 0 means no supported visible concern; 1 a supported minor appearance concern requiring a code and eventual component reduction; 2 clear material unnaturalness requiring correction; 3 major compromise; 4 impossible/effectively nonfunctional geometry. Physical impossibility is unnecessary for 1-3. Do not reduce a material discrepancy to 1 merely because operation is possible. Confidence high/medium/low is evidential and independent of severity; never inflate it. A concrete minor finding may be low-confidence, but missing evidence alone is not a concern.
observed means the essential relationship is exposed; partial means some is assessable; unobservable requires severity null, confidence low and empty codes. Otherwise use integer severity 0-4. Severity 0 has no codes; positive severity requires supported codes. Do not output scores or verdicts.
Architectural-junction appearance and ancillary mounting height without an appropriate code may be noted in limit. Do not force unsupported attachment, axis or perspective findings. Do not claim external feedback retrieval or infer a human verdict.
