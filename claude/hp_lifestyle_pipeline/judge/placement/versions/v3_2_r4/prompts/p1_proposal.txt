WITNESS VERIFICATION
For each requested component, identify every distinct proposed visual cue, not merely its code or conclusion. Inspect the same object-relative relationship in the full image and available tiles. Check ownership and reference geometry. A tile boundary is not a furniture edge; tiles are repeated evidence, not votes. If a relevant contour is unresolved, state that limit rather than claiming detailed inspection succeeded.
In existing evidence/consequence strings, give a concise disposition for each distinct proposed cue: visible relationship; supported appearance or specific rejection; meaningful alternative; physical-claim limit. This is not a reasoning transcript. Reject a nonexistent cue directly. If the cue exists but its visible continuation makes it acceptable, explain that continuation. If only the allegation of bending, extra structure, collision or impossible use is unproven, assess the narrower appearance independently. Do not automatically clear it. Conversely, do not invent a weaker substitute merely to preserve a penalty.
A suitable activity, usable access, or possible construction cannot by itself rebut a registration or silhouette concern. Counter-evidence must address the same object and relationship. A clean table apron does not answer a different furniture contour. A proposed severity is not binding. Being asked to verify a component does not imply a defect.

REQUESTED TESTS
support_scenario: support suitability, siting, connected allocation and affirmative working-use appearance. A chair/notebook alone does not prove displaced work. A clear dedicated station is valid. bare_staged_setting requires an actual display-like relationship, not missing accessories, blank walls or unknown closed storage.
access_usability: functional front, usable edge and intervening footprints. Laptop deck runs from hinge toward user, including rear views. Distinguish lateral props from operating-strip intrusion. A pinched local user area can be a minor concern without proving every route blocked. Ordinary chair offset, angle or centering is harmless. Printers need no aligned chair. Do not invent off-frame routes, seats, movement, exact clearances or mechanisms. Near-wall proximity and a closed configuration alone do not establish obstruction.
orientation_alignment: corresponding chassis/support directions and the working-edge strip across the footprint. Unequal slopes, opposite slope signs and changing margins alone are insufficient. Judge the extended registration, not isolated edge arithmetic. Substantial visibly conflicting rigid groups can warrant severity 2 even with possible diagonal use; mild awkwardness can warrant 1. Neither requires incompatible physical planes. One readable family can suffice; cropped table ends do not automatically clear it. Screens, trays and grain are not base axes. Curved supports have no obligatory rectangular axis. Station direction requires the combined product, associated user position and approach. Use furniture_axis_conflict for a demonstrated directional mismatch and furniture_layout_conflict for arrangement/grouping, never chair pose alone.
scale_perspective: depth-aware size, support planes and relevant furniture contours. Follow the affected silhouette through visible continuations, thickness, joins and terminations. A local awkward projection can warrant 1 without proving an extra member. Visible rounded returns, bevels, aprons, tapered legs and fitted/asymmetric designs may rebut the appearance claim. Hidden hardware cannot. Cropping and occlusion do not establish missing supports. A weak alignment claim is not independent construction evidence. One small prop or product prominence does not establish scale error.
contact_integration: all exposed bearing contact and relevant product/prop/wall junctions. B may explain recesses and trays. Soft shadows are valid; hidden contact is unknown. A specific awkward tangent or apparent interpenetration may warrant 1 without proven collision. Ordinary background overlap alone does not.

OWNERSHIP AND CALIBRATION
Retain only supported codes. Keep distinct supported witnesses in text even when a component uses their greatest severity. Score one underlying defect once; do not add severities. Confidence follows the governing finding, not another clear relationship. Do not transfer a rejected allegation to another code without independent evidence.
Severity 0 means no supported visible concern; 1 supported minor appearance concern requiring a code and existing component reduction; 2 clear material unnaturalness requiring correction; 3 major compromise; 4 impossible/effectively nonfunctional placement. Do not require impossibility for 1-3 or reduce a material defect merely because use remains possible. Confidence high/medium/low is evidential. Never raise it to satisfy P1 consensus. Concrete minor concerns may remain low-confidence; missing evidence alone is not a defect.
observed means the essential relationship is exposed; partial means some is assessable; unobservable requires severity null, confidence low and empty codes. Otherwise severity is integer 0-4. Severity 0 has empty codes; positive severity requires supported codes. Preserve weights 20/30/20/15/15, deterministic scoring, material failure at medium/high-confidence severity at least 2 and the existing verifier/ensemble policy. Do not output scores or verdicts.
Lighting, branding, background richness, decorative balance, pictorial depth and prompt compliance are not placement penalties without a distinct allowed defect. Note a relevant scope limit briefly rather than forcing a code. Do not claim external feedback access.
