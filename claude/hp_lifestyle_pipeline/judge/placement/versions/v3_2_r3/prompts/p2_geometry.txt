SUPPORT_CONSTRUCTION
Separate tabletop perimeter, thickness, fascia/frame, supports and associated chair/stool. Inspect local contours, joins and terminations plus the overall assembly. Identify the affected member and likely owner. Uneven thickness, a locally awkward projection, an inconsistent termination or a racked relationship can warrant an appearance penalty without proving an extra member, detached joint or structural impossibility. Use slight/uneven/awkward language for minor findings rather than asserting collapse or literal deformation.
Rounded returns, exposed thickness, deep aprons, tapered or splayed members, fitted counters, panel frames and asymmetric designs can be coherent. Separate neighboring furniture and reflections. Missing support requires an affirmative cue such as exposed unsupported span or disconnected continuation; cropping, hidden seats, occluded legs and unseen mounting hardware alone are insufficient. Do not prescribe leg counts, invent hidden fixings or certify concealed attachments. An orientation allegation is not construction evidence by itself.

RIGID_ALIGNMENT

RELATIVE_SCALE_PERSPECTIVE
Compare product, support and contextual objects with depth accounted for. Product prominence, reference-render size, one small prop and cropped table length are insufficient. Non-rectangular projected outlines, unequal edge lengths and ordinary ellipses are not sufficient on their own. Do not infer exact dimensions or ancillary mounting height from a crop.

PRODUCT_CONTACT

Global illumination and faint branding are outside this criterion unless a separate contact/shadow contradiction is visible.
