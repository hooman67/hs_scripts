VERIFICATION STANDARD
Separate three questions: what is visibly present, whether that relationship looks awkward, and what physical mechanism is established. Rejecting a claim of proven rotation, bending, missing structure or complete obstruction does not automatically reject a supported minor appearance concern. Retain the narrower concern at severity 1 with an accurate code and honest confidence when warranted. A plausible physical explanation is counter-evidence, not automatic clearance. Conversely, a proposal alone, uncertainty alone or a prettier alternative is not evidence. Do not replace a rejected allegation with an unrelated defect to preserve a penalty. Give decisive evidence and its important alternative or limit, not a reasoning transcript.

REQUESTED-COMPONENT TESTS
support_scenario: assess support role, station location, connected usable-surface allocation and working-use appearance. Distinguish tabletop and floor-standing equipment. A chair or notebook alone does not establish an occupied workstation. Centering or a clear dedicated cabinet is not inherently wasteful; visibly fragmented allocation may be. bare_staged_setting can describe a minor display-like immediate station without blocked use. Judge actual object relationships, task/personal cues, wear and context, not prop counts. Missing optional supplies, sparse walls and closed cabinet contents are not sufficient. Use unpopulated_storage only for clearly functional, visibly unpopulated storage with a supported staging consequence.
access_usability: identify the functional front, working edge and relevant user area. A laptop's deck extends from hinge toward user even in rear views. A nearby prop may intrude into that approach without covering controls. Distinguish minor crowding from complete obstruction; visibly pinched user space can support severity 1 in blocked_user_workspace. Do not assume a narrow gap is the only passage or invent an off-frame route to clear it. Associate seating through the working surface, not proximity. Chair angle, offset and centering alone are harmless. Printers need no operating chair. Check exposed controls, output and lid/tray relationships without invented service clearances, mechanisms or opening trajectories. Near-wall proximity alone is not blocked operation.
orientation_alignment: distinguish product base, support surface, support frame and station layout. Compare corresponding width/depth relationships in perspective, not equal image slopes, screen tilt, trays or grain. Curved tables have no mandatory rectangular axis. Minor awkward registration can warrant severity 1 without proven incompatible convergence. Clear substantial disagreement between coherent groups warrants severity at least 2 even with good access/contact; one readable family can suffice without complete furniture visibility. Ordinary diagonal placement can remain coherent. A genuinely discordant product–seating–table operating relationship or visibly bunched furniture grouping can use furniture_layout_conflict; chair pose or lateral offset alone cannot. Independent room zones and loose papers need not align. Do not turn support distortion into product yaw without evidence or add unrequested construction findings.
scale_perspective: assess depth-aware size, plane coherence and table/chair construction. Name the affected contour, member and likely owner. Uneven thickness, a locally awkward projection or incoherent termination can warrant severity 1 without a detached joint or proven extra member. Distinguish these from ordinary rounded returns, deep aprons, tapered legs, fitted counters and asymmetric designs. Missing support requires positive exposed evidence, not cropped legs, occluded seats or invisible fixings. Do not invent hidden construction. Product prominence, one small prop or cropped table length is not adequate scale evidence. A rejected orientation claim does not establish malformation. Use conflicting_perspective for inconsistent plane appearance and malformed_support_furniture for the furniture contour/assembly itself.
contact_integration: inspect all exposed load-bearing base contact and nearby product–prop or prop–wall junctions affecting station integration. A specific awkward tangent or apparent intersection can support severity 1 in sinking_or_intersection while literal collision remains uncertain. Ordinary overlap and frame thickness alone do not. Intrinsic recesses, display parts and output trays must be distinguished using B. Hidden contact is unknown, soft shadows are valid, and no mandatory glossy reflection or upholstery indentation applies.

ATTRIBUTION AND SCOPE
Retain only independently supported codes from a multi-code proposal. Score a shared defect once under its narrowest primary owner. Within a component use the greatest supported severity, not summed allegations. Physical geometry, natural arrangement and global styling are distinct. Lighting preference, faint branding, wall-art taste, decorative balance, color separation and pictorial-depth requests are not placement penalties without an independent allowed spatial defect. Ancillary mounting height or an ambiguous architectural transition may be a real scope gap; do not force an unrelated code. Prompt compliance is separate.
For supplied claims concerning a requested component, state supported, disputed or uncertain portions and the appearance-versus-physical boundary in its evidence/consequence strings. Note an out-of-scope premise briefly where necessary, but do not add scene-level fields or findings for unrequested components. Human OK/FAIL never determines severity.

CALIBRATION
Severity 0: no supported appearance or functional concern in assessable evidence, not just absence of material failure. Severity 1: supported minor visible awkwardness requiring a code and component reduction. Severity 2: clear material unnaturalness requiring correction. Severity 3: major compromise. Severity 4: impossible/effectively nonfunctional placement. Do not require impossibility for a positive finding or demote a clear material defect to 1.
Confidence high/medium/low describes evidence independently of severity and proposer agreement. A concrete borderline concern may be severity 1 with low confidence; uncertainty alone is not a defect. observed means the essential relationship is exposed; partial means some is assessable; unobservable requires severity null, confidence low and empty codes. Otherwise severity is integer 0-4. Severity 0 has empty codes; positive severity requires supported codes. Confidence follows the governing finding. Preserve weights 20/30/20/15/15, deterministic scoring and material failure at severity at least 2 with medium/high confidence. Do not calculate scores, verdicts, consensus or change verifier policy.
