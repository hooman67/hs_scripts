access_usability: reconstruct the functional front and apparent user approach. The laptop deck runs from hinge toward user, including rear views. Props beside the product differ from props intruding into the working-side strip. Minor crowding or pinched user space can use blocked_user_workspace without proof that every operator position is blocked. orientation_alignment: compare corresponding rigid product/support directions in perspective, not exact image slopes. Separate width from depth, and chassis from screen, tray or grain. Curved supports have no mandatory rectangular axis. A minor visibly awkward relationship can warrant severity 1 without geometric reconstruction. Nonparallel or diagonal placement is not automatically awkward. Distinguish relative rotation from distortion within the support. For station layout, establish the product–seating–table operating relationship rather than chair pose alone. A genuinely discordant working arrangement or bunched furniture grouping can use furniture_layout_conflict even when use is possible. Independent room zones and loose papers need not align. Ancillary height or decorative balance is not axis conflict.
scale_perspective: assess depth-aware size, inconsistent planes and support-furniture construction. Inspect local contours, thickness, joins and terminations as well as overall assembly. Name the affected member and likely owner. An irregular projection or uneven fascia can warrant severity 1 without proving a separate extra member or impossible joint. Ordinary curved returns, deep aprons, tapered legs, fitted counters and asymmetry remain legitimate. Missing support requires positive exposed evidence such as disconnected continuation or a visibly unsupported span; occlusion, cropped legs and unseen mounting hardware are not enough. Do not prescribe leg counts or invent hidden attachments. Separate neighboring furniture. Product prominence, one small prop and cropped furniture length are insufficient scale evidence. A weak orientation hypothesis does not establish malformation.
contact_integration: inspect the whole exposed load-bearing base and nearby product–prop or prop–wall junctions affecting station integration. A specific awkward tangent, conflicting depth cue or apparent interpenetration can support severity 1 in sinking_or_intersection while literal collision remains uncertain. Ordinary occlusion and frame thickness are not defects by themselves. Reference-consistent recesses, trays and display features are intrinsic geometry, not missing supports. Hidden contact is unknown; soft shadows, matte surfaces and firm upholstery without indentation are valid.

CALIBRATION
Severity 0 means no supported appearance or functional concern in assessable evidence, not merely no material failure. Severity 1 is a supported minor concern requiring a code and component reduction. Severity 2 is clear material unnaturalness requiring correction; 3 a major compromise; 4 impossible/effectively nonfunctional placement. Positive severity does not require impossibility, and clear material defects must not be capped at 1.
Confidence high/medium/low describes evidence independently of severity. A concrete borderline concern may be severity 1 with low confidence; absence of proof alone is not a defect. observed means the essential relationship is exposed; partial means some is assessable; unobservable requires severity null, confidence low and empty codes. Otherwise use integer severity 0-4. Severity 0 requires empty codes; positive severity requires supported codes. Confidence follows the governing finding. Preserve weights 20/30/20/15/15, deterministic scoring and material failure at severity at least 2 with medium/high confidence. Do not calculate scores, verdicts or consensus or alter verifier policy.
