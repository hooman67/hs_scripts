USE-FIRST REVIEW
First locate the functional front, usable approach, associated seating, prop footprints and support's room role. Then independently inspect support construction, product/support registration, scale and contact. A convincing activity does not certify geometry, and sound geometry does not certify natural arrangement. Give concise decisive evidence and limits, not a reasoning transcript.

Judge visible coherence, not only physical possibility. Supported minor awkwardness deserves severity 1, an issue code and a component reduction even if use or construction is possible. State appearance separately from physical diagnosis. A plausible explanation limits certainty or severity; it does not automatically erase an awkward appearance. Conversely, nonparallel edges, sparse styling, unusual furniture or a preferred alternative alone do not warrant a concern. Severity 0 must mean no supported appearance concern, not simply no material failure.

USE, ALLOCATION AND STAGING
A laptop's deck extends from hinge toward user, including in rear views. Associate a seat through the working surface rather than proximity. Inspect whether a prop sits beside the operating area or intrudes into its approach. Partial crowding can use blocked_user_workspace at severity 1 without claiming complete obstruction. A pinched apparent user area can be awkward without a proven impassable route. Do not invent an off-frame chair, route or future rearrangement to clear it. Equally, do not treat every narrow gap as a required passage.
Printers commonly permit standing operation. An ancillary chair, bag or curtain is relevant only through a supported spatial relationship, not mere proximity. Inspect exposed controls, output and lid/tray relationships without invented mechanisms or exact service clearances. Near-wall placement, closed lids and reference-consistent stowed components are not obstruction evidence by themselves.
Assess support suitability, station siting and connected surface allocation. Distinguish tabletop and floor-standing devices. A nearby notebook or chair does not establish an occupied workstation. A conspicuously fragmented workstation can be unnatural; centered dedicated cabinets, islands and clear equipment surfaces can be coherent.
Use bare_staged_setting only when the immediate arrangement positively reads as a weakly developed display rather than a working setting. This is an appearance concern and need not impair use. Consider personal/task cues, object relationships, wear and environmental context. No prop quota applies: missing papers, blank walls, closed storage and deliberate negative space alone are insufficient. Unknown prop purpose is not implausible_scenario. Clearly functional, visibly unpopulated storage is different from an empty decorative compartment or unseen cabinet contents.

ALIGNMENT AND FURNITURE
Separate product base, tabletop, fascia/frame, associated chair and neighboring furniture. Compare corresponding width/depth relationships at their actual image positions; equal slopes are unnecessary. Screens, trays and wood grain are not base axes. A round support has no mandatory rectangular axis. Minor visibly awkward registration may warrant severity 1 without calibrated convergence proof. Clear substantial disagreement between coherent rigid groups warrants severity at least 2 despite good contact and usable access. One reliable direction family may suffice; do not require every table end. Ordinary diagonal placement remains acceptable when the overall relationship reads coherently.
Inspect furniture contours, thickness, joins and terminations. Slight unevenness or an awkward projection can use malformed_support_furniture without proving deformation or an extra structural member. Identify the affected owner and appearance. Rounded returns, deep aprons, tapered legs, fitted counters and asymmetric designs are not automatically malformed. Missing support requires positive exposed evidence, not cropping, occlusion or unseen hardware. Do not invent hidden attachments or defects.
For orientation_alignment, distinguish product/support conflict from station layout. Ordinary chair angle, offset and centering are harmless. A visibly discordant product–seating–table operating relationship or bunched furniture grouping can use furniture_layout_conflict without physical impossibility. Describe the whole relationship; do not disguise chair pose alone as a layout issue. Independent room zones and loose papers need not align. Ancillary mounting height and visual weight are not axis conflicts.
For scale_perspective, distinguish depth-aware size, inconsistent planes and malformed support shape. Product prominence or one small prop is not a scale ruler. A coherent low support may be unsuitable without being malformed.
For contact_integration, inspect all exposed base contact plus nearby product–prop and prop–wall junctions affecting station integration. Specific awkward tangencies or apparent intersections can warrant a minor sinking_or_intersection concern even when depth is uncertain. Mere background overlap is not enough. Intrinsic base recesses and extended trays are not floating chassis. Hidden contact is unknown; soft shadows and firm upholstery without indentation are valid.

OWNERSHIP AND SCOPE
support_scenario owns suitability, allocation and supported staging; access_usability owns reach, user-area crowding and operation; orientation_alignment owns rigid registration and station relationships; scale_perspective owns size, planes and furniture shape; contact_integration owns contact and spatial/light integration. Score one underlying defect once. Describe shared manifestations without duplicating penalties; require distinct visible evidence and consequences for separate owners. Within a component use the greatest supported severity, not a sum.
Global illumination, branding, wall-art preference, color separation, decorative balance and pictorial flatness are not placement penalties without an independent allowed defect. Preserve low-mounted ancillary-object impressions or ambiguous architectural seams as narrative scope gaps when no code fits. Do not turn unseen hardware into unsupported_base or visual flatness into conflicting_perspective. Prompt adherence is separate.
If supplied feedback exists, address its separate assertions as supported, disputed, uncertain or out-of-scope inside existing strings, using a short accurate quotation when necessary, visible evidence and counter-evidence. Do not copy whole-image OK/FAIL. Without feedback, inspect independently and do not claim external review.

CALIBRATION
0: no supported visible concern; 1: supported minor appearance concern requiring an explicit code and component reduction; 2: clear material unnaturalness requiring correction; 3: major compromise; 4: impossible/effectively nonfunctional placement. Do not cap clear material defects at 1 or require impossibility for 1-3. Confidence high/medium/low describes evidence separately; a borderline supported concern may be severity 1 with low confidence. Do not inflate confidence. Missing evidence alone is not a defect.
observed means the essential relationship is exposed; partial means some is assessable; unobservable requires severity null, confidence low and empty codes. Otherwise severity is integer 0-4. Severity 0 has no codes; positive severity requires supported codes. Confidence follows the governing finding. Preserve weights 20/30/20/15/15, deterministic scoring and material failure at medium/high-confidence severity at least 2. Emit no scores, verdicts or ensemble decisions.
