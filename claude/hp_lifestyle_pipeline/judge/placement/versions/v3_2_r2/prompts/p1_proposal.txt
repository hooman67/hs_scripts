REQUESTED-COMPONENT POLICIES
support_scenario: assess suitable support role, room siting and connected usable surface. Laptops need a coherent work surface and working side. Tabletop printers need equipment tables/cabinets, not an established active keyboard position, patron's cafe surface or low leisure table; floor-standing units differ. A conspicuously isolated middle placement on a broad wall-associated table can materially waste workspace. A centered printer on a narrow cabinet or legitimate island can be natural. Establish visible allocation, height/context or circulation consequences. Do not infer exact height, wall clearance or room centrality from a crop. A nearby chair or notebook alone does not prove an occupied workstation. Suitable support role does not certify construction.
access_usability: locate the operating front and actual working edge. A laptop's deck extends from the hinge toward the user; rear-lid visibility is not reversed operation. Associate seats from the working surface, not proximity. Chair angle, centering and lateral offset alone cannot establish excessive reach, no_user_position or incoherent use. Independently visible excessive depth, intervening footprints, unavailable body space or completely reversed operation relative to an established position can be material. Do not invent another seat or future rearrangement. Props beside the approach differ from props across it; a peripheral may be parked. Printer access concerns need visible controls, output, drawers and approach, not invented mechanisms or exact clearances. Tray projection is not unsupported chassis.
orientation_alignment: distinguish the product base, support surface and support frame as separate groups. Compare compatible perspective/minor obliquity, consequential relative rotation between coherent groups, and distortion within the support. The allegation may remain unresolved. Compare corresponding width/depth families at their image positions, not equal slopes, a screen, raised tray, chair pose, wood grain or width against depth. Two named boundaries do not prove the claim merely because both are steeper. Check whether their convergence relationship is actually readable and whether the apparent difference is material. Short, shallow or obscured edges limit certainty; opposite slope signs alone are not sufficient. Different direction families need not share a vanishing point. One reliable exposed family can establish substantial rotation despite another hidden family. Clear substantial skew remains material with good access/contact. Minor obliquity is not automatically material. If exposed support distortion makes the proposed reference unreliable, state that limit without inventing product yaw or adding an unrequested construction finding. A malformed chair does not automatically invalidate a readable tabletop. Independent room zones and loose papers need not align; accessory conflicts require a concrete consequence. Layout conflict requires an actual contradictory station, not chair pose.
scale_perspective: assess depth-aware relative size/projection and table/chair construction. Construction includes overall structural shape and assembly, not only impossible joins. A clearly racked top/frame, materially deformed contour or spatially incoherent member arrangement can be material while pieces still touch. Name the affected relationship and why it exceeds normal design variation. For suspicious members, establish likely ownership and exposed joins or terminations. For missing-support claims, identify genuinely visible empty space and actual occluders. Cropped or concealed members are unknown, not absent. Deep aprons, thick tops, splayed/tapered members, fitted supports, panels, rear supports, legal L designs and multi-leg frames are possible coherent designs, not automatic defects. Separate furniture or reflections can explain apparent extra members. Do not invent fixings or certify hidden connections. A weak orientation claim is not affirmative construction evidence. Product prominence, cropped table length or one small prop is insufficient scale evidence. A coherent low table can be unsuitable without being malformed.
contact_integration: inspect the whole exposed load-bearing base for gaps, sinking/intersection or incompatible shadows/reflections. Contact at one edge does not certify another. Soft diffuse shadows are valid; no dark outline or glossy reflection is required. Hidden contact is unknown, not floating. A printer tray is not the supporting chassis. Upholstery should plausibly carry weight without mandatory indentation on firm surfaces.

Decorative balance, blandness, preferred reflections, missing personal or paper props, unfamiliar accessories and mixed functions are not independent placement defects. Staging codes require a concrete spatial/use consequence. Architectural artifacts matter only through an in-scope support, siting or access relationship. Retain only independently supported codes from a multi-code proposal. Do not duplicate one allegation across dimensions without distinct visible consequences.

CALIBRATION
Severity 0: no supported defect in assessable evidence, not hidden-structure certification. Severity 1: optional small placement refinement. Severity 2: clear material unnaturalness requiring correction. Severity 3: major placement/use compromise. Severity 4: impossible/effectively nonfunctional placement. Confidence high/medium/low describes evidence, not proposer agreement. observed means the essential relation is sufficiently exposed; partial means some is assessable; unobservable means no defensible assessment. Unobservable requires severity null, confidence low and empty codes. For observed/partial use integer severity 0-4. A visibly grounded unresolved material concern may receive positive severity with low confidence; missing proof alone is not a defect. Severity 0 requires no codes; positive severity requires at least one supported code. Use the greatest independently supported severity within a component, with confidence tied to that finding.
The application retains weights 20/30/20/15/15, deterministic scoring and material failure at severity at least 2 with medium/high confidence. Do not calculate scores, verdicts or consensus or alter verifier policy.
