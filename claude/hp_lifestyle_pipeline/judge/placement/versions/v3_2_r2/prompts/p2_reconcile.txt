GEOMETRIC ATTRIBUTION
Distinguish the target base, tabletop perimeter, support thickness/frame and associated chair as separate groups. A preserved reference product does not prove the support is malformed.

CONSTRUCTION, SCALE AND CONTACT
Inspect overall structural shape and assembly as well as local joins. Materially deformed contours, a visibly racked top/frame or spatially incoherent member arrangement can be defective without an impossible joint. Identify the actual affected relationship and why it exceeds coherent design variation. Continuous contours or a few plausible joins do not certify the complete assembly.
Trace suspicious members to likely owners and exposed connections or terminations. For allegedly absent supports, distinguish genuinely exposed empty space from actual occlusion. Do not count legs or impose a freestanding-table template. Thick tops, deep aprons, tapered/splayed members, fitted counters, panel frames, rear supports, legal L-shaped designs and asymmetry can be coherent. Separate furniture or reflections can explain apparent extra members. Do not invent hidden fixings, certify concealed attachments or fail unfamiliar design alone. A weak product-axis allegation is not affirmative construction evidence.
Compare scale with depth accounted for. Product prominence, one small prop, reference-render size or cropped table length is insufficient. Separate relative rotation, projective inconsistency and construction unless independent consequences support more than one finding.
Inspect the whole exposed load-bearing base for gaps, sinking/intersection and incompatible shadows/reflections. Contact at one edge does not certify the rest. Soft diffuse shadows are valid; no mandatory dark outline or glossy reflection. Hidden contact is unknown, not floating. A printer output tray is not its supporting chassis. Upholstery should plausibly carry weight without mandatory indentation on firm surfaces.

USE AND LOCATION
Establish the operating front, support and actual approach. A laptop deck extends from the hinge toward its user; rear-lid visibility is not reversed use. Associate a seat from the working surface, not proximity alone. Movable-chair angle, lateral offset and centering alone are harmless and cannot become excessive reach, no_user_position or incoherent activity. Actual excessive working-edge depth, intervening footprints, unavailable body space or completely reversed operation relative to an established position remain material. Do not invent another seat or future arrangement. Props beside use differ from props between the approach and controls; a peripheral may be parked.
Tabletop printers need appropriate equipment supports, not an established active keyboard position, patron's cafe surface or low leisure table; floor-standing units differ. Inspect visible controls, output, drawers and approach without invented mechanisms or exact clearances. A chair matters only when it obstructs access. A conspicuously isolated printer in the middle of a broad wall-associated table can materially waste connected workspace; centering on a narrow cabinet or using a legitimate island can be natural. Establish visible allocation, support height/context or circulation consequences. Do not infer exact height, room centrality or mandatory wall contact from a crop. A chair or notebook alone does not establish an occupied workstation. A coherent low table may be unsuitable without being malformed. Independent room zones and loose papers need not align; rigid accessory conflicts require a concrete consequence.

CALIBRATION
Severity 0: no supported defect in assessable evidence, not certification of hidden relationships. Severity 1: optional small placement refinement. Severity 2: clear material unnaturalness requiring correction. Severity 3: major compromise. Severity 4: impossible/effectively nonfunctional placement. Confidence high/medium/low describes evidence, not agreement or rhetorical strength. observed means the essential relationship is sufficiently exposed; partial means some is assessable; unobservable requires severity null, confidence low and empty codes. For observed/partial use integer severity 0-4. A concrete but unresolved material concern may have positive severity with low confidence; missing evidence alone is not a defect. Severity 0 requires no codes; positive severity requires supported codes. Use the greatest independently supported severity within a component, with confidence tied to that finding. An established issue is not diluted by uncertainty about unrelated hidden relationships.
The application applies unchanged weights 20/30/20/15/15 and deterministic scoring. Medium/high-confidence severity at least 2 is material failure. Do not calculate totals or verdicts. This observation is the sole scored output; specialist reports are not extra votes.
