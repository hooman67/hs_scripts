support_scenario: a laptop needs a suitable work surface and coherent working side. A tabletop printer needs an equipment table/cabinet with a practical standing approach, not an active keyboard position, patron's café surface or low leisure table; a floor-standing printer is different. Inspect station allocation and visible floor/wall relationships. A printer marooned centrally on a broad wall-associated surface can be materially unnatural without impossible use. A centered printer on a narrow cabinet or a legitimate freestanding island can be natural. Do not require literal wall contact, infer exact height/clearance from pixels, or claim a room's center from a close crop. A chair or notebook alone does not establish an occupied workstation.

access_usability: identify the keyboard deck extending from the hinge toward the laptop user. A rear lid facing the camera does not mean reversed use. Establish seat association from the actual working surface, not proximity. Ordinary movable-chair angle, lateral offset and imperfect centering alone cannot establish excessive reach, no_user_position, blocked use or incoherent activity. A fully reversed operating side relative to an established position, excessive working-edge depth, intervening footprints or unavailable body space can be material. Do not invent another seat or future rearrangement. Distinguish props beside the approach from props across it. For printers inspect exposed controls, output and drawers from a plausible standing edge, not the camera direction or imagined mechanisms. An output tray can overhang while the chassis is supported.

orientation_alignment: compare corresponding rigid-base/support width and depth families where exposed, using paired boundaries and convergence at their image positions. Do not use display tilt, a raised tray, chair pose, equal-slope demands or width-to-depth comparisons. Different direction families may have different vanishing points, and curved supports need not have rectangular axes. Distinguish compatible perspective, consequential rigid rotation and support distortion. One independently decisive family can establish a conflict when another is hidden; a single weak edge difference cannot. If malformed support geometry prevents comparison, state the limit rather than inventing product yaw. Material laptop/table or printer/table rotation does not require obstruction. Independent room zones and loose papers need not share axes; a rigid accessory conflict needs a concrete consequence. Layout conflict requires an actual contradictory station, including established reversed use, not chair displacement.

scale_perspective: compare relative size and projection with depth accounted for. Product prominence, a long cropped table or one small prop is insufficient. Inspect tabletop outline/thickness/joins and associated chair/stool assembly separately. Follow questionable members to likely owners and exposed connections or terminations; inspect genuinely open under-furniture space if missing support is suspected. Identify the actual contradictory join, contour or load path. Crops and occlusion are not absent parts; do not prescribe leg counts or reject legal L-shaped, rear-support, panel or multi-leg designs. Reflections and separate furniture can explain apparent extra members. A plausible hidden attachment is not a certified attachment. A coherent low support can be unsuitable without being malformed.

contact_integration: inspect the whole visible load-bearing base, not just a plausible shadow at one corner. Gaps, sinking/intersection, detached shadows or contradictory reflections can establish a problem. Soft diffuse shadows and hidden edges alone cannot. Upholstery must plausibly support weight, without requiring indentation on a firm surface.

Keep placement separate from styling. Plain walls, sparse surfaces, asymmetrical artwork, preferred reflections, generic boxes, mixed room functions and missing notebooks or paper stacks do not independently fail. Staging codes require an observable spatial/use consequence. Unknown prop purpose is not itself contradiction. Architectural discontinuity matters only when it changes an in-scope support, room-placement or access relationship; a hidden return or partition termination is not proof of penetration. Do not double-count construction as rotation, floating or bad activity without separate evidence. Physical possibility does not excuse material unnaturalness; a prettier alternative does not establish a defect.

Severity 0 is no supported defect in assessable evidence, not hidden-geometry certification. A visibly grounded but unresolved material concern may have low-confidence positive severity; uncertainty alone is not a defect. Positive severity needs supported codes; severity 0 has none.
